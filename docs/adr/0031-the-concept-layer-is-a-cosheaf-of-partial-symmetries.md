## 0 · THE THEOREM IN ONE PARAGRAPH

**The concept layer on the ambo is a connection whose coefficients are PARTIAL SYMMETRIES, not groups.** To each corner a cast concept-space (a finite relational structure, three-valued in the record); to each edge the person's identification `J` — a partial isomorphism, symmetric, possibly empty. Transport along a path composes partial bijections in the symmetric inverse monoid `I(R)`; the residue of a closed walk is an element `h` of it, read by its canonical decomposition **`Fix · Mov · Und`** — *returned unchanged · returned changed · did not return* — and classified by its cycle–path type. **The classical connection (Step 1's permutations, D69's linear transports, 0030's automorphisms, the engine's own metric and Z₂ connections) is exactly the sub-case in which every transport is total** — `Und ≡ ∅` — obtained by killing the idempotents. The layer is a **cosheaf** on the complex: a vertex carries its cast; an edge carries the AMALGAM `A ⊔_J B` of its corners (the pushout — the born midpoint's reading; the cospan-realization of the partial symmetry `J`; injective from both parents; richer than either); **a face carries the glued colimit of its boundary — the world where its three identifications hold at once, which identifies `a` with `h(a)`**; the 3-cell carries the total colimit. **Injectivity holds from vertices to edges and fails from edges to faces — where the holonomy is: connection on 1-cells, curvature on 2-cells, Bianchi (automatic, weak) on 3-cells.** Faces and the cell are readings: never walked into, never named. Generation composes: every higher-generation `J` is CARRIED or COMPOSED from the six by the ambo's incidence, except where a medial edge reaches beyond its shared corner — the BORN room, the person's to give (§3.5, restored 2026-09-23); every surviving loop's residue is invariant under subdivision; the face's residue concentrates on the medial triangle; disagreement at depth is housed as doubled roles, never pooled into a vertex.

## 1 · DEFINITIONS

1. **Corner.** A finite relational structure `A = (R, Σ, ar, I)`: roles `R`; relation-types `Σ` with arities (any finite arity; unary types included); a three-valued instance function `I : ∐_σ R^{ar σ} → {T, F, ?}` (known-true · known-false · unrecorded); axioms as sentences over `Σ`; marks unread. Cast outside the engine by the mold (`.handoff/MOLD_v4_…`); the engine takes it. *A cast can refute the model and never establish it.*
2. **Partial isomorphism** `A ⇀ B`: a pair `(j, τ)` of partial bijections on roles and on types (arity-preserving) with **no known conflict**: for `σ ∈ dom τ` and a tuple `r ⊆ dom j`, `I_A(σ, r)` and `I_B(τσ, jr)` are never `{T, F}`. Silence is exposure, not disagreement; an import into a silent slot is marked and refusable by a declared negative.
3. **Identification `J_e`** on an edge `e : u → v`: a partial isomorphism `A_u ⇀ A_v`, **GIVEN** by the person **by pointing — role to role and word to word; the device refuses a contradiction by name and otherwise takes it; nothing is offered, ranked, or marked by support** (Δ80, 2026-09-22 — the earlier clause *"from the candidates the device offers (ranked by weight; each pair marked by support; a fiat pair marked)"* is retired; `fiat` is retired as a mark: a glued role no tuple witnesses twice is already described by the trace, and a flag for it is a grade); **symmetric** (`J_ē = J_e⁻¹`); **possibly empty** (`0`: the corners stand beside each other). ⛔ Never machine-posited (the name-slot law at the fiber grain).
4. **Transport** along a path `p = e₁…e_n`: `J(p) = J_{e_n} ∘ … ∘ J_{e₁}`, composed in the inverse category of structured sets and partial isomorphisms. It satisfies `J(pq) = J(q)J(p)`, `J(p̄) = J(p)⁻¹`, `J(p p̄ p) = J(p)`, commuting idempotents `J(p p̄) = 1_{dom J(p)}` — and **not** `J(p p̄) = 1`.
5. **Residue** of a closed walk `ℓ` at `v`: `h_ℓ = J(ℓ) ∈ I(R_v)` (and `t_ℓ ∈ I(Σ_v)` at the type grain), read as `Fix(h_ℓ)` · `Mov(h_ℓ) = dom ∖ Fix` · `Und(h_ℓ) = R_v ∖ dom`, classified by cycle–path type. `1` = flat on this loop; `0` = the **empty core** (nothing returned) — top and bottom idempotents, never confused; every `1_D` between them is *flat but lossy*. **`Und` reads *did not return* — never *"not given"*:** a role can be `Und` where the person has spoken on every edge its routes use (2026-09-22: 5,860 of 20,000 random triples). **Every reading is relative to the loop's direction** — `Und(h⁻¹) = R_v ∖ im h`, not `R_v ∖ dom h` — **and states the direction it walked** (added 2026-09-23).
6. **Midpoint (the born concept of an edge):** `M_e = A_u ⊔_{J_e} A_v` — the pushout. Its NAME is GIVEN and truly absent until the person names it (0029 §2); its READING is the amalgam with its countable caption (`|A_u| + |A_v| − |dom j_e|` roles; shared / `u`-only / `v`-only).
7. **The cosheaf:** a space for every cell of the complex, each higher cell's space the colimit of its boundary's diagram (§3.3).
8. **Gauge:** `Aut(A_v)` at each corner; rigidity = trivial gauge. The record — the `J`'s in order, the names — is the written gauge of this layer.

## 2 · THE CLASSICAL CONNECTION IS THE TOTAL SUB-CASE

A **G-connection** is a P-connection in which every `J_e` is total on both sides. Then all corners are isomorphic, `J(p p̄) = 1`, transport factors through the free groupoid of the 1-skeleton, and the residue at `v` is a homomorphism `π₁ → Aut(A_v)`. **The free groupoid is the free inverse category with its idempotents killed**; hence every statement of §3 specializes to the classical statement under `Und ≡ ∅` — Step 1 §3 (permutations; cycle types; Bianchi `= 1`), D69's regimes (with the fiber category changed to vector spaces), 0030 §1 (automorphism transports; curvature; holonomy), the engine's metric transport and its `±1` orientation connection. **Your difficult corners and the engine's easy ones are one object; the easy ones satisfy one more equation.**

## 8 · LEGIBILITY — the opposite vertex is CLUEING at the edge and GLUING only at the face (added Δ80, `Tue Sep 22 10:09:26 +0330 2026`, HEAD `10ccb00`)

The project's oldest meaning system (`docs/governance/PLATONIC_ENGINE_TRISONIZED_MIDWIFE_SEMANTIC_CLUEING_METHOD.md`) says a child born on edge `A—B` is *made legible by the opposite vertex*, which *projects onto the edge*, and forbids two shortcuts — the parent blend (§12.4) and the opposite vertex as a third parent (§12.5). This ADR was silent on the opposite vertex, and the silence would let a builder wire it into the edge. Three claims close it; none changes a sentence above.

**(a) The edge is a functor of the span alone.** `M_e = A_u ⊔_K A_v` takes the two corners and the person's `(J, τ)` and nothing else (§6 invariant 9). The midpoint is not a blend of its parents — it is their union over what the person identified, larger than either (§3.1) — and it is not a mixture with the opposite vertex.

**(b) The formal shadow of "`C` projects onto `AB`" is the route-map through `C`, and its comparison with the direct identification IS the face's residue.** With the three edges of face `ABC` given, `proj_C = J_CB ∘ J_AC : A ⇀ B` is the identification of `A` with `B` that the route through `C` induces; the face's residue at `A` is `h_A = J_CA ∘ J_BC ∘ J_AB = proj_C⁻¹ ∘ J_AB`, so `h_A = 1_D` exactly where the person's direct `J_AB` agrees with the projection, and `Mov`/`Und` are where they part.
