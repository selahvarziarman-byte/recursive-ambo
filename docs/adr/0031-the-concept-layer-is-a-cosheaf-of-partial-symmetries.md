## 0 · THE THEOREM IN ONE PARAGRAPH

**The concept layer on the ambo is a connection whose coefficients are PARTIAL SYMMETRIES, not groups.** To each corner a cast concept-space (a finite relational structure, three-valued in the record); to each edge the person's identification `J` — a partial isomorphism, symmetric, possibly empty. Transport along a path composes partial bijections in the symmetric inverse monoid `I(R)`; the residue of a closed walk is an element `h` of it, read by its canonical decomposition **`Fix · Mov · Und`** — *returned unchanged · returned changed · did not return* — and classified by its cycle–path type. **The classical connection (Step 1's permutations, D69's linear transports, 0030's automorphisms, the engine's own metric and Z₂ connections) is exactly the sub-case in which every transport is total** — `Und ≡ ∅` — obtained by killing the idempotents. The layer is a **cosheaf** on the complex: a vertex carries its cast; an edge carries the AMALGAM `A ⊔_J B` of its corners (the pushout — the born midpoint's reading; the cospan-realization of the partial symmetry `J`; injective from both parents; richer than either); **a face carries the glued colimit of its boundary — the world where its three identifications hold at once, which identifies `a` with `h(a)`**; the 3-cell carries the total colimit. **Injectivity holds from vertices to edges and fails from edges to faces — where the holonomy is: connection on 1-cells, curvature on 2-cells, Bianchi (automatic, weak) on 3-cells.** Faces and the cell are readings: never walked into, never named. Generation composes: every higher-generation `J` is derived from the six by the ambo's incidence; every surviving loop's residue is invariant under subdivision; the face's residue concentrates on the medial triangle; disagreement at depth is housed as doubled roles, never pooled into a vertex.

## 1 · DEFINITIONS

1. **Corner.** A finite relational structure `A = (R, Σ, ar, I)`: roles `R`; relation-types `Σ` with arities (any finite arity; unary types included); a three-valued instance function `I : ∐_σ R^{ar σ} → {T, F, ?}` (known-true · known-false · unrecorded); axioms as sentences over `Σ`; marks unread. Cast outside the engine by the mold (`.handoff/MOLD_v4_…`); the engine takes it. *A cast can refute the model and never establish it.*
2. **Partial isomorphism** `A ⇀ B`: a pair `(j, τ)` of partial bijections on roles and on types (arity-preserving) with **no known conflict**: for `σ ∈ dom τ` and a tuple `r ⊆ dom j`, `I_A(σ, r)` and `I_B(τσ, jr)` are never `{T, F}`. Silence is exposure, not disagreement; an import into a silent slot is marked and refusable by a declared negative.
3. **Identification `J_e`** on an edge `e : u → v`: a partial isomorphism `A_u ⇀ A_v`, **GIVEN** by the person from the candidates the device offers (ranked by weight = known agreements; each pair marked by support; unrecorded pairs as exposure; a fiat pair marked); **symmetric** (`J_ē = J_e⁻¹`); **possibly empty** (`0`: the corners stand beside each other). ⛔ Never machine-posited (the name-slot law at the fiber grain).
4. **Transport** along a path `p = e₁…e_n`: `J(p) = J_{e_n} ∘ … ∘ J_{e₁}`, composed in the inverse category of structured sets and partial isomorphisms. It satisfies `J(pq) = J(q)J(p)`, `J(p̄) = J(p)⁻¹`, `J(p p̄ p) = J(p)`, commuting idempotents `J(p p̄) = 1_{dom J(p)}` — and **not** `J(p p̄) = 1`.
5. **Residue** of a closed walk `ℓ` at `v`: `h_ℓ = J(ℓ) ∈ I(R_v)` (and `t_ℓ ∈ I(Σ_v)` at the type grain), read as `Fix(h_ℓ)` · `Mov(h_ℓ) = dom ∖ Fix` · `Und(h_ℓ) = R_v ∖ dom`, classified by cycle–path type. `1` = flat on this loop; `0` = the **empty core** (nothing returned) — top and bottom idempotents, never confused; every `1_D` between them is *flat but lossy*.
6. **Midpoint (the born concept of an edge):** `M_e = A_u ⊔_{J_e} A_v` — the pushout. Its NAME is GIVEN and truly absent until the person names it (0029 §2); its READING is the amalgam with its countable caption (`|A_u| + |A_v| − |dom j_e|` roles; shared / `u`-only / `v`-only).
7. **The cosheaf:** a space for every cell of the complex, each higher cell's space the colimit of its boundary's diagram (§3.3).
8. **Gauge:** `Aut(A_v)` at each corner; rigidity = trivial gauge. The record — the `J`'s in order, the names — is the written gauge of this layer.

## 2 · THE CLASSICAL CONNECTION IS THE TOTAL SUB-CASE

A **G-connection** is a P-connection in which every `J_e` is total on both sides. Then all corners are isomorphic, `J(p p̄) = 1`, transport factors through the free groupoid of the 1-skeleton, and the residue at `v` is a homomorphism `π₁ → Aut(A_v)`. **The free groupoid is the free inverse category with its idempotents killed**; hence every statement of §3 specializes to the classical statement under `Und ≡ ∅` — Step 1 §3 (permutations; cycle types; Bianchi `= 1`), D69's regimes (with the fiber category changed to vector spaces), 0030 §1 (automorphism transports; curvature; holonomy), the engine's metric transport and its `±1` orientation connection. **Your difficult corners and the engine's easy ones are one object; the easy ones satisfy one more equation.**

## 3 · THEOREMS (proofs in `.handoff/RULING_THE_FORMAL_SYSTEM_…`; computed instances cited)

**3.1 The edge.** `M_e` exists for every `J_e`, is unique up to isomorphism, and both corners embed as total injections that reflect on translated types; `j_e = ι_v⁻¹ ι_u` — the edge is walked forward through one injection and backward through the other; **restricted to a corner, an edge is the identity: no edge identifies two roles of one corner.** `|M_e| ≥ max(|R_u|, |R_v|)`, equality iff `j_e` is total on the smaller: **generation grows by exactly `Und`.** In the total case `M_e ≅ A_u ≅ A_v`. [the reframe; computed 09-08]

**3.2 The face.** `h_ℓ` for the face's boundary is a partial bijection of `R_v`; the face's colimit restricted to `A_v` is `R_v / ∼` with `x ∼ y` iff `y = h(x)` for some holonomy `h` of a loop at `v` (the holonomy inverse monoid `H_v`). **A role returns changed exactly when the face's glued world identifies it with another role of its corner — coarsening exists in the layer only here, derived, never given.** [the reckoning; the first face, computed by hand]

**3.3 The cosheaf, and where injectivity fails.**
| cell | space | map from the boundary |
|---|---|---|
| vertex | the cast | — |
| edge | `M_e = A_u ⊔_J A_v` | injective |
| face | the glued colimit over its three `J`'s (identifies `a ∼ h(a)`) | injective iff `Mov(h) = ∅` |
| 3-cell | the total colimit | from the faces |
**The face's cone-deficit at a corner** `= |R_v| − |image of R_v in the face colimit|` = the number of that corner's roles the face merges into others — LAW-23 countable. *(Corrected on the run: the difference between the independent tally `Σ|R| − Σ|J|` and `|face colimit|` counts roles returning UNCHANGED, not gluings.)* Faces and the 3-cell are READINGS: not walked into (the walker lives on the 1-skeleton), not named (Arman, 2026-09-10).

**3.4 Loops, faces, the cell.** The residue of the complex at a corner is the closure of ALL closed walks' holonomies; faces are minimal loops but do not generate (words equal in the free group differ in the free inverse monoid by spurs, which restrict domains; computed: a quadrilateral strictly exceeds the product of its faces in 31 % of random cases). The boundary word of the 3-cell composes to a partial identity for EVERY connection (`J(w) ≤ 1` whenever `w` is trivial in the free group): **Bianchi is automatic, weak, and disciplines nothing** (0 failures / 20 000; vacuous in 80 %). The 3-cell's reading is the total colimit.

**3.5 Refinement — the lift law holds for the layer.** Subdividing an edge at its midpoint replaces `J_e` by the two injections; the composite along the refined path equals `J_e` (0028 CARRIED, proved); the factorization freedom is `Aut(M_e)` — gauge freshness at a born cell. **Every surviving loop's residue is invariant under subdivision** (anomaly matching). No identification is given above generation 0: every sub-edge's `J` is COMPOSED by the ambo's incidence — a corner edge carries the parent's injection; a medial edge (two midpoints sharing a corner) carries the identity on the shared corner — so at generation 1 **the corner triangles are flat and the medial triangle carries the face's residue** (the spanning-tree gauge: content concentrates where cycles close). A vertex born on a corner edge takes the pushout over its parents' shared part, which is the midpoint's own space: **the interior of an edge IS its born concept; stations are positions inside it.** From generation 3, cells whose parents disagree about a role take the meet of the shared identification and **house the disagreement as two roles** (`a`-via-`B`, `a`-via-`C`), never pooled. The interpolation obstruction of the permutation form (no eighth root of a swap) does not arise: a partial symmetry always factors through its born objects.
> **Amendment on the run (2026-09-16, `g3_doubled_roles.py`, 25 triples + the other two midpoints): the set that doubles is broader than "the disagreement". At the gen-3 midpoint of `P_A — P_B`, the doubled roles of `A` are exactly `(im J_CA ∖ Fix h_A) ∪ (dom(J_BC ∘ J_AB) ∖ dom h_A)` — every role the third edge identified whose circuit does not return it unchanged (for a moved role the doubling sits at its TARGET `h(a)`, where the two routes collide), plus every role whose circuit reaches `C` and is stranded there. So a face that is flat-but-lossy (`h` a partial identity ≠ `1`) still doubles at generation 3, on exactly the roles its third edge identified and the circuit did not bring home. The first two seals of the instrument ("doubled = `Mov(h)`"; "doubled = unconfirmed pairs from both sides") both fired and are kept in the file.**

**3.6 The three strata.** Candidate `J`'s on an edge fall into equal-weight families that structure does not distinguish; the person's choice among them is content, not gauge (the exchange doctrine: an act is the lawful symmetry-breaker); the residue under his `J`'s is the COVARIANT meaning with the `J`'s as record; its cycle–path type and `Aut`-class are INVARIANT; **the part of a loop's residue fixed by structure alone is the MEET of the residues over all admissible connections** (always exists in `I(R_v)`). Structure disciplines `J` in exactly three ways — weight separation, refusals by declared negatives and types, and nothing at the cell. On Flow–Φ–T the meet is `0`: the first face's meaning is entirely the person's; two refusals are where structure bit.

**3.7 The relational residue.** For each known instance at `v`, the loop's transported instance compares as **agree · contradict · unknown · undefined**; an automorphism-valued (classical) connection always agrees; a P-connection can contradict a corner's own record around a loop — route-disagreement at the relation grain.

## 6 · INVARIANTS, each with its falsifier
1. **Symmetry:** `J_ē = J_e⁻¹` — a stored reverse that is not the inverse is a stamp that drifts.
2. **Consistency:** no known conflict on any given edge — a candidate offered across a `{T, F}` pair is a fabrication.
3. **The stone on the skeleton:** every vertex-to-edge map injective — a vertex whose space pools two roles of one corner has become the op-set.
4. **The vacuity pair:** `0` printed as `1` (nothing returned as nothing moved) repeats the telescoping error.
5. **Anomaly matching:** a surviving loop whose residue changed under subdivision with no act.
6. **Bianchi:** a boundary word that composes to a non-identity on its domain has mis-oriented a face.
7. **Gauge:** a residue reading that changes under a relabeling by `Aut(A_v)` reads gauge as content.
8. **The registers:** a midpoint's name filled by the machine; a face named at all.
