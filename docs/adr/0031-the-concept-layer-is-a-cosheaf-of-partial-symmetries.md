## 0 · THE THEOREM IN ONE PARAGRAPH

**The concept layer on the ambo is a connection whose coefficients are PARTIAL SYMMETRIES, not groups.** To each corner a cast concept-space (a finite relational structure, three-valued in the record); to each edge the person's identification `J` — a partial isomorphism, symmetric, possibly empty. Transport along a path composes partial bijections in the symmetric inverse monoid `I(R)`; the residue of a closed walk is an element `h` of it, read by its canonical decomposition **`Fix · Mov · Und`** — *returned unchanged · returned changed · did not return* — and classified by its cycle–path type. **The classical connection (Step 1's permutations, D69's linear transports, 0030's automorphisms, the engine's own metric and Z₂ connections) is exactly the sub-case in which every transport is total** — `Und ≡ ∅` — obtained by killing the idempotents. The layer is a **cosheaf** on the complex: a vertex carries its cast; an edge carries the AMALGAM `A ⊔_J B` of its corners (the pushout — the born midpoint's reading; the cospan-realization of the partial symmetry `J`; injective from both parents; richer than either); **a face carries the glued colimit of its boundary — the world where its three identifications hold at once, which identifies `a` with `h(a)`**; the 3-cell carries the total colimit. **Injectivity holds from vertices to edges and fails from edges to faces — where the holonomy is: connection on 1-cells, curvature on 2-cells, Bianchi (automatic, weak) on 3-cells.** Faces and the cell are readings: never walked into, never named. Generation composes: every higher-generation `J` is CARRIED or COMPOSED from the six by the ambo's incidence, except where a medial edge reaches beyond its shared corner — the BORN room, the person's to give (§3.5, restored 2026-09-23); every surviving loop's residue is invariant under subdivision; the face's residue concentrates on the medial triangle; disagreement at depth is housed as doubled roles, never pooled into a vertex. **Two grains (Δ110–Δ111, ruling §31).** At the CONCEPT grain — each corner taken whole — the triangulation is GIVEN content before any pairing: an edge (the two are related), a face (the three belong together), the opposite vertex projected into the edge's midpoint (the Midwife's `J`, restored), and the angles (ADR 0023). At the ROLE grain — the items inside each concept — it is silent, and all role-grain content is the person's (Δ80). *To each edge the person's identification `J`* names the edge's ROLE-grain content, beside the relation the triangulation gives it; it is not what the edge IS.

## 1 · DEFINITIONS

1. **Corner.** A finite relational structure `A = (R, Σ, ar, I)`: roles `R`; relation-types `Σ` with arities (any finite arity; unary types included); a three-valued instance function `I : ∐_σ R^{ar σ} → {T, F, ?}` (known-true · known-false · unrecorded); axioms as sentences over `Σ`; marks unread. Cast outside the engine by the mold (`.handoff/MOLD_v4_…`); the engine takes it. *A cast can refute the model and never establish it.*
2. **Partial isomorphism** `A ⇀ B`: a pair `(j, τ)` of partial bijections on roles and on types (arity-preserving) with **no known conflict**: for `σ ∈ dom τ` and a tuple `r ⊆ dom j`, `I_A(σ, r)` and `I_B(τσ, jr)` are never `{T, F}`. Silence is exposure, not disagreement; an import into a silent slot is marked and refusable by a declared negative.
3. **Identification `J_e`** on an edge `e : u → v`: a partial isomorphism `A_u ⇀ A_v`, **GIVEN** by the person **by pointing — role to role and word to word; the device refuses a contradiction by name and otherwise takes it; nothing is offered, ranked, or marked by support** (Δ80, 2026-09-22 — the earlier clause *"from the candidates the device offers (ranked by weight; each pair marked by support; a fiat pair marked)"* is retired; `fiat` is retired as a mark: a glued role no tuple witnesses twice is already described by the trace, and a flag for it is a grade); **symmetric** (`J_ē = J_e⁻¹`); **possibly empty** (`0`: the corners stand beside each other). ⛔ Never machine-posited (the name-slot law at the fiber grain). **Re-worded (Δ111):** the core `J_e` is *given by pointing, unconditionally or in every light* — his unconditional pairs, together with the MEET of the respect-pairs of the faces through `e` (§3.11). **A second GIVEN record kind, the RESPECT:**
   - keyed by (edge, opposite corner); tuples `(a, b, c)`, written `a ↦ b ⟨c⟩` — *a is b as regards c* — with `c` a role of that face's third corner;
   - holds-only, with no one-to-one requirement: respects stand together; words likewise (`w ↦ w′ ⟨c⟩`);
   - on `M⁺`, the relation-type `⟨C⟩` in the signature (beside the derived `≡_C` of §3.10), binary on `M_e`'s points; `c` is the tuple's WARRANT, never a term;
   - INPUT, serialized as his act — the concept-grain face reaching into a role-grain act.
4. **Transport** along a path `p = e₁…e_n`: `J(p) = J_{e_n} ∘ … ∘ J_{e₁}`, composed in the inverse category of structured sets and partial isomorphisms. It satisfies `J(pq) = J(q)J(p)`, `J(p̄) = J(p)⁻¹`, `J(p p̄ p) = J(p)`, commuting idempotents `J(p p̄) = 1_{dom J(p)}` — and **not** `J(p p̄) = 1`.
5. **Residue** of a closed walk `ℓ` at `v`: `h_ℓ = J(ℓ) ∈ I(R_v)` (and `t_ℓ ∈ I(Σ_v)` at the type grain), read as `Fix(h_ℓ)` · `Mov(h_ℓ) = dom ∖ Fix` · `Und(h_ℓ) = R_v ∖ dom`, classified by cycle–path type. `1` = flat on this loop; `0` = the **empty core** (nothing returned) — top and bottom idempotents, never confused; every `1_D` between them is *flat but lossy*. **`Und` reads *did not return* — never *"not given"*:** a role can be `Und` where the person has spoken on every edge its routes use (2026-09-22: 5,860 of 20,000 random triples). **Every reading is relative to the loop's direction** — `Und(h⁻¹) = R_v ∖ im h`, not `R_v ∖ dom h` — **and states the direction it walked** (added 2026-09-23).
6. **Midpoint (the born concept of an edge):** `M_e = A_u ⊔_{J_e} A_v` — the pushout. Its NAME is GIVEN and truly absent until the person names it (0029 §2); its READING is the amalgam with its countable caption (`|A_u| + |A_v| − |dom j_e|` roles; shared / `u`-only / `v`-only). **With `J_e` empty the midpoint is `A_u ⊔ A_v`: the two together, as two, related by the triangulation, with no item yet identified — never *"apart"*. Its opposite corners stand on it from the start (§3.10's blocks, silent).**
7. **The cosheaf:** a space for every cell of the complex, each higher cell's space the colimit of its boundary's diagram (§3.3).
8. **Gauge:** `Aut(A_v)` at each corner; rigidity = trivial gauge. The record — the `J`'s in order, the names — is the written gauge of this layer.

## 2 · THE CLASSICAL CONNECTION IS THE TOTAL SUB-CASE

A **G-connection** is a P-connection in which every `J_e` is total on both sides. Then all corners are isomorphic, `J(p p̄) = 1`, transport factors through the free groupoid of the 1-skeleton, and the residue at `v` is a homomorphism `π₁ → Aut(A_v)`. **The free groupoid is the free inverse category with its idempotents killed**; hence every statement of §3 specializes to the classical statement under `Und ≡ ∅` — Step 1 §3 (permutations; cycle types; Bianchi `= 1`), D69's regimes (with the fiber category changed to vector spaces), 0030 §1 (automorphism transports; curvature; holonomy), the engine's metric transport and its `±1` orientation connection. **Your difficult corners and the engine's easy ones are one object; the easy ones satisfy one more equation.**

**3.8 The face at generation ≥ 1** (added 2026-09-23; ruling §12, ratified §123.1; `born_face_reading.py` on the fixtures flow · t-cell · phi · triangle, the 42 hand triples). After a dissection a born face's edges carry three things — the CARRY (corner edges), the COMPOSED meet (medial edges) and the person's BORN pairs — and the face reads their loop. **(a) What it reads; what in it is news.** With every born room empty the reading is DETERMINED by generation 0: a face around a corner (`AB·AC·AD`) reads the identity on that corner's part — `1_D`, flat but lossy; a corner cell's face (`A·AB·AC`) reads `1` on all of `A`; a medial face (`AB·BC·CA`) reads `ι_A ∘ (J_CA ∘ J_BC) ∘ ι_B⁻¹` — `h_A` transported on the glued part, and it also moves roles of `B` that `A` does not hold into roles of `A` (9 roles in 9 of the 42 triples): derived, but not `h`. **The news is exactly the born pairs' contribution:** with born pairs every born face's holonomy EXTENDS its born-room-empty holonomy, and every added route uses a born pair. **(b) Refusal — inherited whole, never reduced.** With its born room empty a medial face's world IS its seed face's world, so it refuses exactly where the seed face does (6 of 42, at T); a face around a corner and a corner cell's face refuse nothing (a star, a path). Born pairs only add refusals, and a new one needs a born pair in a cycle that merges two roles of one corner that declares a negative. **A refusal names PAIRS of tuples** — "the offending tuple" shifts under born acts though nothing un-contradicts. **(c) The domain** — the face's core at a born corner is the holonomy's domain inside that corner's space: derived from derived spaces, re-derived at every read; with born pairs it only grows. **(d) `Und`** — one kind, one address: an edge of the born face; a break on the edge leaving a born vertex descends from the silence of that vertex's own parent edge. Both hands — a born pair on the face's edge, or an act on the parent edge at its midpoint — are the person's; the device chooses neither. **(e) Direction.** A born face inside a seed face inherits its cycle. **A face between two cells (`AB·AC·AD`, between a corner cell and the core) is walked by each cell in the opposite direction; each walk has its own D14 name, composed from its corners in that walk's order; and the reverse walk IS the flipped face — which here, unlike at a seed face, is a face of the solid: the other cell's.** A reading names the cell whose walk it is.

**3.11 The triad as respects, the meet-core, and the readings** (added `Fri Sep 25 14:52:29 +0330 2026` at HEAD `7abb8f7`; Δ108–Δ111; rulings §28–§31; `triadic_act.py`, `respect_pairing_P2.py`, `meet_core_P6.py`).

**THE ACT.** At a face `X·Y·Z` he points `(x, y, z)` as ONE act, recording three respects: `x ↦ y ⟨z⟩ · y ↦ z ⟨x⟩ · x ↦ z ⟨y⟩` — a face-section, his reading of the whole face.
- **ATOMIC:** a leg that cannot be recorded refuses the whole act, and the leg is named with its existing partner and its face.
- It merges nothing and binds no other face.
- The unconditional pair stays available beside it.

**THE MEET-CORE.** The core on an edge is his unconditional pairs, together with the pairs he gave as respects in EVERY face through that edge — COMPOSED (0028) from his pointing, never posited. Two conditions:
- **(i) Conflicts left out.** A meet pair that pairs a role twice, or contradicts an unconditional pair, is left out and SAID. A respect never causes a refusal; an unconditional pair that contradicts another is refused, as always.
- **(ii) The dependency reading.** A meet pair that would break a born act is held back from the core and SAID, naming the born act, until he withdraws one or the other.
- **One light only.** On an edge whose other face has given no respects, the meet is empty and the core is his unconditional pairs alone: the child is the two together, with marks.

**THE READINGS — marks, never demands.** Each respect `a ↦ b ⟨c⟩` is read against the core, leg by leg:
- a leg is KEPT (paired as the respect says), BROKEN (either end paired elsewhere on that edge), or OPEN;
- the respect reads HONORED (both legs KEPT), BROKEN (the leg named), or NOT YET.
- The reading is content — *the light changes the mappings* (Δ105), shown as the difference between his section and his core. Nothing is asked of him.

**THE CARRY** (§3.10's rules): the lift carries respects verbatim (they are record); the next pushout carries them as FOREIGN words; a door preserves them (holds-only, so no conflict); the cargo does not carry them.

**FENCES.**
- One `J` per edge, shared by both faces; never two born concepts on one edge.
- The device names no respect, never completes a triad from two legs, never synthesizes a respect from the legs, and never proposes a leg's repair (§8(c)).
- A respect never glues on its own, never enters a door's lines, and never causes or answers a refusal.
- A respect's `c` is a role of that face's own third corner, pointed by him.
- No merge except by the core. No face named or walked. Words stay separate unless he points them too.

**Measured.**
- On the tetrahedron, triads entered as core pairs could never make a role return as another (0 in 4,000). That is why the act is respects, not core pairs.
- The respect readings are non-vacuous on the first face: over every core pair × every role of phi, HONORED 6 · BROKEN 1,905 · NOT YET 195.
- The meet of two faces' respects pairs a role twice in 47 of 4,000 random tetrahedra, and changes the core in 958 — the reasons for conditions (i) and (ii).
