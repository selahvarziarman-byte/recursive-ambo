## Context

## Decision
- **Interior content = a section of a bundle over the quotient 2-cell, consistent across all identifications simultaneously, boundary-clamped, picking up the w₁ twist across orientation-reversing glues** — concretely, the **lowest-energy `L_U` section over the subdivided cell, boundary-clamped** (researcher ruling `RESEARCHER_RULING_INTERIOR_CONTENT`). It is the **same object** as the already-ruled rich-field eigenmode, restated per-cell.
- **The "wall" that defeated the field is a THEOREM, not a bug.** On w₁ ≠ 0, `dim ker L_U = 0` — no smooth, global, nonvanishing consistent section exists. The field kept chasing that impossible object. The resolution: **accept the forced defect** — the section *must* carry a node/vanishing locus, and that locus is exactly the disclination `Σ = PD(φ)`. **The defect is the content's signature, not a failure.**
- **Render and content are separable** (two bundles — R³-positions vs field-values — at two grains): the R³ immersion needs *a* consistent section (any immersion; engineer-ready), the field content needs the `L_U` section (per-surface, defect at `Σ`).
- **Product intent (Arman, 2026-07-02): BOTH levels ship, and the field render is REOPENED.** Level 2 = geometry + identification/lineage (loops + historied merge point, co-location ≠ identity). Level 3 = + the interior field content (`L_U` section with defect `Σ` over the surface). Sequencing by operational dependency (render immersion first), **not trauma**.
