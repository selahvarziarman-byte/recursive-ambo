# 0011 — The reading is the user's mirror; five structural necessities make it possible; meaning never enters operations

Sharpens ADR 0005. The sovereign's dialectical **reading** — a form is an *argument*, the twist is a *contradiction*, a signed loop is a *contradictory constellation* — is the human's **mirror**, not anything the engine builds. The error to avoid is importing those meaning-words into operations (the "resolution operation" was exactly this mistake, dropped): that is the ADR 0005 error from the meaning side.

Decision: build the **structural necessities** that make the reading possible; keep every operation purely structural; let meaning live in the mirror.

## The five structural necessities (all structural; meaning rides on top)

```txt
(1) DESCENT recoverable        — every site pulls back to its roots            [the committed pull-back]
(2) each identification carries ORIENTATION (sign / w₁)                         [the signed pull-back]
(3) INCIDENCE preserved        — the local links                               [the registry]
(4) INVARIANTS computable      — orientability, genus                          [topology signature / GlueCoh]
(5) the WHOLE ASSEMBLED COMPLEX with its signed loops                          [beyond per-site data]
```

## The wall, and the locked reading

```txt
contradiction              → orientation-reversing identification (sign −1, w₁)
proposition                → vertex      ·      inference → edge      ·      argument → the form
"a contradictory constellation"  →  a SIGNED LOOP in the assembled complex
"solving" / resolution     → nothing (reading-only; no operation)
```

A meaning-word **never** crosses into an operation's definition. "Intelligible after transformation" is therefore **mirror-ability** — the structure is honest and complete enough that the user's mirror forms cleanly — never a meaning the engine outputs.

## Open (routed to the researcher)

Whether the fifth necessity is satisfied by **one** signed loop or requires the form's **whole loop-structure** (all essential cycles together) is a grounding question — the researcher's to settle, not the designer's to assert.
