# 0023 — The conformal layer: angle is a first-class combinatorial relation (0003 gets a scalpel; Bound 8 stays held)

ADR 0003 ruled the systemic trace *structural, not metric* — Ambo's definite angles and distances are "secondary bends," not to be rebuilt as a metric field. The conformal layer (§2) revisits this for one quantity: the **angle**. An angle is a semantic **relation between relations**, not a secondary bend: a concept sits at a vertex, its *edges are its relations* to other concepts, and the angle between two edges is *how a concept's two relations stand to each other* (sharp = the relations nearly coincide; right = independent; straight = opposed). So the form is an **argument** about its concepts — the same three concepts and three relations arranged 60‑60‑60 vs 90‑45‑45 assert different things; the topology is identical and the *angles carry the whole claim*. The engine derives angle (per-vertex deficit, per-edge dihedral — e.g. the aperture's E³/S³/H³ routing) and discards it; no angle field is carried on the form. The conformal layer closes that gap: carry the angle as an owned, operation-preserved property.

## Decision

1. **Angle crosses to first-class carried structure; distance does not.** 0003 conflated "angles *and* distances" as "the metric" and rejected the bundle. Conformal is angles *without* distances — the scale-free relation. So 0003 is reopened for the **angular relation only**: it becomes first-class carried structure. Distances, positions, and ray-traced interiors stay stripped (Bound 8's core).
2. **The angle is DEFINITE and never person-supplied — Bound 8 stays held.** It enters from a SOURCE and is transformed by operations; the person never types it. There are **two sources**: (a) **invocation** of a regular seed — combinatorial, a triangle is 60‑60‑60, a square's corners 90; and (b) **loading / lifting** from an Ambo universe — the Ambo emits a rigid metric (ADR 0003), so the entity's own **definite angles travel verbatim in its vertex positions**. Non-regular is **not** arbitrary: the median of an equilateral is *exactly* 90‑60‑30, a square's diagonal *exactly* 90‑45‑45 (no free parameter), and the Ambo's angles are the *source's*, not the person's. The gesture set supplies no angle (it invokes / loads / selects cells, corners, cycles, edge-pairs — no position or angle input), so Bound 8 (a person-supplied arbitrary corner angle) is held **by the gesture set itself**.
3. **The angle is STRUCTURE; its meaning is the user's mirror (ADR 0005 survives).** The engine carries honest angular structure; the relation-among-concepts the angle expresses is the user's mirror, not engine-minted meaning.

## Rejected

- **Reopening Bound 8 / admitting arbitrary angles** — considered (a non-regular angle *looked* like it required arbitrary input) and rejected: the chord shows a non-regular angle is combinatorially determined. The full metric (distances, ray tracing) stays held.
- **Carrying the angle as discursive/semantic content** — would reopen 0005. The angle is structure; the meaning is the mirror.

## Consequence

The module's layering refines from "metric stripped below" to **"distances stripped, angles carried."** The conformal layer is the **angular reading of the operation history**, as the meaning ledger (O27) is its *lineage* reading — both read the same operations; conformal *owns* the derived angle so its dynamics through transformations are tracked, not re-derived-and-discarded. The subdivide (first-class in the meaning ledger as `'resolution'`) is also a key angle-*generating* op — an equilateral's median is where 90‑60‑30 is born.

**The transform is forced by the geometry — the layer carries consequences, not decisions.** No operation has an "angular mode" the way identify has a topological mode; the geometry supplies each op's angle-transform. The layer's contract is therefore strict and simple: own the angular configuration, update it geometrically each op, never invent a value. Defining each op's forced transform, and sounding the depth (the DUAL is the wall — the op that most flagrantly computes-and-discards the angle, ADR 0020 / `dualView`), is the next work — a formalization + audit for the researcher, the same pass the meaning ledger got.

**The lift is now an argument-import.** Loading anything richer than a bare edge (a segment carries no angle; two edges meeting at a vertex do) brings the Ambo's angular relations among its concepts across *whole* — in the vertex positions the snapshot already carries verbatim. The old lift imported topology, labels, and lineage; under this layer it also imports the **argument**. This is the primary way the Ambo's semantic-angular content enters the playground, and it means the conformal substrate is not built up only from invoked regular seeds — it can arrive pre-formed from a universe.
