## 1 · DEFINITIONS (the vocabulary the reading names)
- **concept** = a vertex. What the argument is about.
- **relation** = an edge. How two concepts bear on each other.
- **stance** = a corner angle — a *relation between relations*: how two relations meet on one concept. `⊾` right = **independent** · acute = **converge** · obtuse = **lean apart** · reflex (a cone) = **over-committed**.
- **closure** = open (a boundary — a frame/outside) · closed (self-contained).
- **verdict** = the stance-composition's consequence (§3.4). NEVER the headline.
- No meaning-words live in the operations; the reading is a MIRROR on the trace (O27) — the middle grammar between the raw atom and the semantic layer.

## 2 · THE READING IS A MAP (map first, verdict last)
For `O : Source ⟶ Result`, four pieces in order of weight:
- **3.1 IDENTIFICATION MAP** (the spine): one row per result-part, named by the source-parts it IS, typed `identified/survived/born/died`. `p ← {A,B,C,D}` · `a ← {AB,CD}`. *Read from the trace* (provenance names + the glue word/ledger); never recomputed.
- **3.2 INCIDENCE CARRIED**: the relation among new parts, descended from old — `a ⊾ b @ p ← the four corners`. *Read from the acquired complex ∘ 3.1.* The piece most easily dropped.
- **3.3 STANCE-COMPOSITION**: each result concept's stance = the source stances the map routed to it — `p : 90⊕90⊕90⊕90 = 2π`. *Read from `cornerAngles`, summed over the source corners 3.1 sends, WITH the acquired complex for valence.*
- **3.4 VERDICT (consequence):** local — *seamless* (Σθ=2π) · *cone +δ* · *saddle −δ* ; global — **GATE ON CLOSURE first** (a form is BOUNDED iff any vertex reads `valence:boundary`, `conformalAtom:192-197`): **CLOSED** → sign(Σδ): *curls up* (>0) · *tiles* (0) · *splays* (<0) ; **BOUNDED** → NOT a Σδ sign (`gaussBonnetTotal` is the boundary-CORRECTED total `Σ_int(2π−θ)+Σ_bnd(π−θ)=2πχ` — its sign conflates the rim: a FLAT disk reads **+360° yet is flat**, interior curvature 0°, `researcher_bounded_verdict.cjs`): read as *open* + the local-cone character (△⟶cone = "bounded disk · apex +300° over-committed · rolls to a point, open at the rim") ; **Form-distance** — uniform local verdicts ⇒ at its Form. R1's deficit = 3.4-local; R2's geometry-type = 3.4-global (**CLOSED forms only**).

## 4.5 · THE DRAWN BODY vs THE CELL BODY (two corrections, measured at HEAD `90e8b80`)
The stance (3.3) lives on the **cell body** (the complex). Two forms DRAW a *different* body that hides it — the register must DECLARE the gap, never fake the smooth skin:
- ⛔ **immersion / flip-glue-RP²** (corrects my earlier "immersion = N-A because flat" — the coder MEASURED the opposite; substrate is the arbiter): flip-glue/RP² carries **TWO real 180° cone points** on its cell body (Σδ = 2×π = 2π = 2πχ, χ=1). The immersion RENDER draws the **smooth representative** surface, not the cell complex; a cone on the smooth skin would be a lie. So the register correctly declares **N-A on the SMOOTH body** — the stance is REAL, it just lives on the cell body the immersion doesn't show. The argument reading of an immersion form therefore HAS a stance (the cones); the N-A is about which body is drawn, not "flat."
- ⛔ **collapse-sphere** (engineer's finding 1430, ruled here): the collapse quotient is a **topological sphere** (v=1,e=0,f=1 ⇒ χ=2) whose apex RIDES the flat corner-sum (`readVertexCurvatures`: deficit = (4−n)π — **0 for the square**, matching the census; it varies with n and never tracks χ=2's 4π). **The 4π is not "lost at the seam" — it is genuinely absent:** collapse is an IDENTIFICATION (composes existing flat angles), not a metric spherification. A piecewise-flat (1-vertex,1-face) sphere would need apex θ = −2π to carry 4π — **not a real angle** (re-grounded durably by the mothership, `ms_collapse_sphere.cjs`: n=3/4/6 → 180°/0°/−360° = (4−n)π, χ=2, never 4π; the original researcher `/tmp` probe evaporated — durable receipts only). So this is an **HONEST LIMIT, NOT a stamp-fix** (the crucial contrast with the ascent gap, whose medial faces ARE regular and stampable). The register reads deficit 0 FAITHFULLY; it should DECLARE "topological sphere, no piecewise-flat metric" — like immersion's N-A. **Do NOT mint 4π** — that fakes a metric the cell can't carry. No R1 impact (collapse → immersion → N-A).
