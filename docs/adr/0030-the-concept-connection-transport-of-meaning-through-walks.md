## 0 · THE LAYER IN ONE PARAGRAPH

**The op-set IDENTIFIES carriers: glue pools corners into a class — the quotient forgets which was which (STAMP R-1 Q1: the class's designation composes from its members).** The concept connection is the quotient's non-quotienting sibling: **it does not pool concepts; it TRANSPORTS them.** To each door (each face-pairing the person made — a generator of the form's deck group) it assigns a fiber-map: *crossing this door, a concept becomes this other concept.* The person's walk composes these along its word; **what the cargo comes back AS is the residue the quotient would have forgotten** — the compass document's *homotopy quotient* said in this device's vocabulary. Meaning, in this layer, is the residue; the record is its gauge.

## 1 · THE OBJECT — definitions

1. **THE FIBER.** Over a vertex (a concept-carrier), the fiber `F_v` is the space of concepts it may carry — concretely the PACKET (`label · notes · color · tags · custom`; lineage `createdBy`). The RESIDENT concept at `v` is GIVEN-register content (the person's, 0029 §1). A vertex CARRIES a concept; it is not one (R-1 Q4).
2. **A DOOR-TRANSPORT.** To a door `g` (a face-pairing: `faceA ⟷ faceB`, `mode ∈ {preserving, reversing}`, corner `map`) the connection assigns a fiber-map `τ_g : F → F`. The pairing's corner `map` is already a transport of CARRIERS across the door (which corner meets which — measured substrate); **`τ_g` is the transport of CONTENT — what a concept becomes when carried through.** Two species:
   - **GEOMETRIC transports — DERIVED** from the pairing isometry `g` itself: position, frame, handedness (LAW 22's carried frame IS the first transported concept of this device; its transport is `ρ(g)` on frames — built, walked, and instrumented).
   - **SEMANTIC transports — GIVEN** by the person on his own doors (*"through this door, sky becomes earth"*), a traced act; or DERIVED by a STATED rule. ⛔ **An unassigned door carries a TRUE ABSENCE of transport** — never a silently assumed identity (positive presence: a fact carried by nothing). The trivial connection (`τ_g = id` everywhere) is a lawful CHOICE, given or ruled, never a default the machine fabricates.
3. **TRANSPORT ALONG A WORD.** For a door-word `w = g₁ g₂ … gₙ` (the walk's own gluing-word TRACE, capitals for inverses), the carried concept after the walk is `τ(w)·c = τ_{gₙ} ∘ … ∘ τ_{g₁}(c)`, arriving at position `Γ(w)·p` where `Γ(w)` is the composite pairing-isometry.
4. **CURVATURE, and the concept deck-fit.** Around each interior EDGE-CYCLE (the carried `tower.gate.edgeLinks[]` — 0026 §3's census, the same structure that carries the metric deficit) the transports compose to `τ(cycle)`. **The connection is FLAT iff `τ(cycle) = id` for every edge-cycle** — the exact analogue of the metric deck-fit `Σ dihedral = 2π` (0026 §1). **A non-identity `τ(cycle)` is CURVATURE: a semantic cone-deficit living at the edge, where the metric cone-deficit lives.**
5. **HOLONOMY.** A flat connection's transport depends only on the word's class in π₁: `τ` descends to a representation `ρ_τ : π₁(form) → Aut(F)` — Goldman's object, `Hom(π₁, Aut F)/Aut F` after gauge (§3). Its restriction to any commuting instrument is the ABELIAN part (§4.iv).

## 2 · TRANSPORT OF A CONCEPT AGAINST THE LIVE ORDER SURFACE (what the charter asked: what a carry along `a·b·a⁻¹·b⁻¹` IS when the composite is not the identity)

✔ The ratified surface (`ExploreWindow.tsx:720-743`): the walk writes its TRACE (one letter per door, capitals for the way back); the TALLY is the per-door net count — **the abelianization of the word, by eye**; the SENTENCE fires when every count is zero and says *home* or *not home* by the position-return machinery alone (§3). The concept layer adds exactly one datum to that surface — **the CARGO** — and its reading has two shapes, both LAW-20-legal (nothing felt; only arrival compared):
- **Cancelled and HOME (`Γ(w)·p = p`): the cargo is compared with itself.** For a FLAT connection a home-walk is a null-homotopic loop and `τ(w)·c = c` necessarily — **so any cargo change at home is CURVATURE, read around an edge-cycle: the walker went round an edge and came back holding a different concept.** The semantic cone, at the person's feet.
- **Cancelled and NOT HOME (`Γ(w)·p ≠ p`, the Seifert–Weber case): the cargo is DISPLACED WITH THE WALKER** — `τ(w)·c` sits at `Γ(w)·p`, and there is no resident concept it is "supposed to equal." **Its reading is ROUTE-DISAGREEMENT: two door-words to one position that deliver two cargos** (`τ(w₁)·c ≠ τ(w₂)·c` with `Γ(w₁)·p = Γ(w₂)·p`) — the holonomy of the loop `w₁w₂⁻¹`, which is exactly the commutator content of §4.iv. ⇒ ***Route-dependence of the CARGO is the layer's phenomenon, and it is the same shape as route-dependence of the POSITION that the person has now walked.*** The order-stratum arc's surface already carries the word and the count; the cargo rides beside them.

## 3 · THE WARRANT (the B-2 rider, folded in)

✔ At source the sentence's second clause is *"licensed by the position-return machinery alone (a return fired at THIS door count) — never by anything felt (LAW 20)"* (`ExploreWindow.tsx:736-740`: `home = returnCount > 0 && doorsAtLastReturn === doors`). ⇒ **Ruled: the lawful warrant for `not home` is the PER-WORD, MODEL-GRAIN fact `Γ(w)·p ≠ p` — the composite pairing-isometry of the doors actually crossed moved the walker — which is what the return machinery measures.** `deck abelian: no` (`deckAbelianModel.ts`, Station 2) is a DIFFERENT and WEAKER fact: the FORM-LEVEL necessary condition (an abelian deck sends every cancelled word home; a non-abelian one merely permits some cancelled word not to). **The sentence states the per-word fact; it may CITE the form-level bit as the reason such walks exist. It must not substitute the bit for the licence.** Concept analogue, same grains: per-cargo (`τ(w)·c ≠ c` at home; route-disagreement when displaced) is the warrant; *concept connection flat: yes/no* and *concept holonomy abelian: yes/no* are the form-level certificate bits.

## 4 · THE INVARIANTS — each with what would falsify it

- **(i) FLATNESS / the concept deck-fit:** `τ(cycle) = id` on every carried edge-cycle. *Falsifier:* one mis-assigned door's cycle composes to `≠ id` and must be REPORTED as curvature, never silently corrected — and any reading that keys the cycle set on transported positions rather than the carried census gauges itself to triviality (the R1/DUAL/B.4 trap, a fifth row).
- **(ii) GAUGE, and the record:** transports are defined up to per-vertex re-choice of fiber basis (`τ_g ↦ h_j τ_g h_i⁻¹`); the gauge-INVARIANT content is the conjugacy class of each holonomy (its Wilson trace); **the MEANING is the covariant holonomy WITH its record** (the gauge reading: *the record is the written gauge*). Relabeling concepts is gauge; the person's names and packets are the basis that makes covariant content shareable. *Falsifier:* a reading that changes under a pure relabeling of concepts is reading gauge as content.
- **(iii) REFINEMENT by the lift law (ADR 0028, unchanged):** across a minting gesture the connection CARRIES (subdivision: composites forced, every surviving loop's residue invariant — **anomaly matching is the acceptance witness**), COMPOSES (born-of-incidence: through the birth-correspondence — the `apexB` mechanism one field over), is BORN (new independent cycles: a true absence, the person's — the tower's growth is the meaning-room's growth), and DESCENDS at quotients iff equivariant (a failed descent is content, not error). *Falsifier:* a refined connection whose surviving loop's residue changed without an act.
- **(iv) THE ABELIAN / COMMUTATOR SPLIT (ADR 0028 App. B):** the TALLY (and every H₁-grade instrument) reads exactly the abelianization of the holonomy; **the commutator content is readable ONLY through route-dependence** — two words, one position, compared cargos. For an abelian fiber group the tally is complete; for a non-abelian one it is an abelian eye. *Falsifier:* a commutator-content claim read off counts alone.
- **(v) THE HALF-TURN:** an involutive transport (`τ_g² = id`, the concept comes back as its own MIRROR) is the abelian world's symmetric residue (0028 App. A) — the concept analogue of w₁ and the doubled image; the ratified antipode rule `2(a₁−a₀) ≢ 0 mod n` governs which identifications may carry it. *Falsifier:* a symmetric abelian residue that is not 2-torsion.
- **(vi) THE REGISTERS (0029, undisturbed):** a transported concept (the cargo `τ(w)·c`) is a READ — derived, re-derivable, never displacing the resident GIVEN concept; **the layer may TRANSPORT the given register but never WRITE it.** The dual has no fiber of its own (Δ57 — read-only): a dual vertex's concept is the primal face's composed reading, and transport on the dual is transport of readings. The composer is the one composer (D14). *Falsifier:* a walk that leaves a resident concept changed.
- **(vii) THE MEANING-TRACE LAW applies to the layer's own acts:** assigning a transport is a traced act; the walker's carried state is exhausted by the TRACE plus its INITIAL DATA (frame₀, cargo₀) — any further component, i.e. one that changes with no traced act, would be a trace-law violation (the walker-state closure, ruled to the hermeneutic chair; wording amended 2026-09-03 on ratification). *Falsifier:* cargo that changed with no door crossed and no act recorded.
