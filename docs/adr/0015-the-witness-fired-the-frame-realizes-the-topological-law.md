Item 5 is defined as the **gauge-invariant Poincaré-dual of the `w₁` cochain**: the orientation-reversing dual `Σ = PD(φ)` (equivalently `[φ] ∈ H¹`), whose **invariant is the intersection pairing `[Σ]·[γ]`** (where `Σ` itself lands depends on closed vs bounded — see the researcher-ruled wording below). It is **not** a site-set — for a flat ℤ/2 connection the flip-support moves by a coboundary `φ → φ + δg` while the holonomy is fixed, so **no nonempty gauge-invariant site-set exists** (verified by reproducing the gauge orbit on a 6-cycle: all 64 gauges → holonomy bit invariant `{1}`, 32 distinct flip-supports, sites common to all = ∅). The BFS flip-support the runner emits (`{bd,cd}`, Klein-b `{3,4}`) is kept as the **concrete F3 witness** (non-empty ⟺ winding — the gauge-invariant gate content, sealed and held), not as item 5's cross-form definition. A later additive engineer pass computes `Σ` (the dual 1-chain crossing the flip edges, closed and reduced to its `H₁` class via the committed cycle-basis) and emits it alongside the labeled BFS representative; the fired result above stands untouched.
