# 0009 — The genealogy is the standing object; population consumptive, record monotone, depth the only arrow

Birth is **consumptive** — the parents do not survive; two forms go in and one comes out. So the live **population** of forms is non-monotone, and forms are transient. What persists across a session is the **genealogy**: the DAG of who-was-born-from-whom and who-died.

Decision: the playground's **persistent state and real product is the genealogy/DAG**, not any form (Ground Plan §4.5's "own session state"). A form's "life" is its **arc** through the DAG — born as a frontier form, then consumed into a child or cut. The live forms are the genealogy's growing edge.

## Two counts, two directions

```txt
POPULATION (the cast)   : non-monotone — shrinks on each birth (consumptive)
GENEALOGY (the record)  : strictly monotone-growing — every birth and death adds a node
```

## The arrow

```txt
DEPTH (genealogical)  : the ONLY monotone — every operation makes a child deeper than its inputs;
                        descent is irreversible (= the engine's `generationDepth`, lifted to forms)
COMPLEXITY / level    : FREE — combine/product raise it, collapse/cut lower it
```

So the "story of life" has a direction — forward in descent-time — but no forced complexification: **ascent is a choice the user makes by combining, not a law.**
