## Decision

### 1 · THE STANDARD — one Schläfli symbol {p,q}, one cell, and the vertex IS the curvature

A regular tiling is `{p,q}`: p-gon cells, **q of them at every vertex**. The whole difference between the three geometries is **q — how many flat cells meet at a point** — and it is the engine's own **angle-deficit instrument**, the DEFICIT/FLAT/EXCESS already ruled for cone edges, now read on a surface:

| {p,q} | q flat p-gons at a vertex | angle sum | verdict | geometry |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| {4,3} | 3 × 90° | 270° | a 90° **GAP** — it must close up | **SPHERICAL** (the cube) |
| {4,4} | 4 × 90° | 360° | **CLOSES EXACTLY** — stays flat | **EUCLIDEAN** (the plane) |
| {4,5} | 5 × 90° | 450° | a 90° **OVERLAP** — it must ruffle open | **HYPERBOLIC** (order-5 square) |

**The cell never changes; only q does.** *Every cell stays flat (local flatness); they do not fit (global curvature); the surface bends to absorb exactly the mismatch.* The sign of `(p−2)(q−2) − 4` is the curvature sign. ⇒ **The tiling is not a new instrument — it is the cone-edge deficit read at a tiling vertex.** [[the-three-marks-and-law-22]] ⛔ **SUPERSEDED BY §7 (`B-104` amendment): the world's mark is WHAT THE CELLS DO (the phenomenon, shown), NOT the vertex count (a proof-token, demoted to the record). q "checkable in principle" is not q "checked by him."**

**The three CONFORMAL models (the field's convention, adopted):** spherical as a **stereographic projection**, euclidean as the **plane**, hyperbolic in the **Poincaré disk**. All three are conformal ⇒ **angles are true and geodesics are circular arcs** — one drawing convention serves all three, which is why one Schläfli symbol can run across them.

### 2 · HYPERBOLIC-FIRST — the method is the reflect-across-edge tiling; the others derive by the same {p,q}

**The observed method (hyperbolic, Poincaré disk):**
1. **The cell's size is forced, not chosen:** `cosh R = cot(π/p)·cot(π/q)` (the circumradius). `<1` spherical · `=1` euclidean · `>1` hyperbolic. In the disk the base p-gon's vertices sit at Euclidean radius `r = tanh(R/2)`.
2. **The tiling is generated by REFLECTION across each edge** — and in the disk a geodesic reflection is a **circle-inversion** in the unique circle orthogonal to the boundary through the edge's two endpoints. Reflect the base cell across its p edges, then its images across theirs (frontier BFS), deduping cell centres.
3. **The boundary circle is INFINITY, not an edge** — the cells shrink toward it and never reach it (the horizon doctrine: address the rim, never draw it as a solid boundary the person could mistake for a wall).

⇒ **DERIVATION to the other two (clause 2):** the *same* {p,q} and the *same* `cosh R` law project into the other two conformal models — **spherical by stereographic projection** of the cube's spherical tiling (from a pole; the cell containing the pole becomes the whole EXTERIOR — a finite world drawn one cell inside-out), **euclidean as the literal plane tiling** (every vertex identical). The method is one; the model is the projection. **This is exactly what the reference computes**, and it is why hyperbolic-first is not mere sequencing: the reflect-across-edge generator IS the general method, and sphere/plane are its degenerate (finite / flat) cases under the same `cosh R`.

### 3 · DESCENT — a non-orientable / quotient surface is the tiling under a FREE symmetry; the engine CHECKS `σ ∈ Sym(tiling)`, never assumes

**RP² is not a fourth geometry. It is `{4,3}/2` — the HEMICUBE: the spherical panel with an antipodal identification laid on it.** Observed, not assumed (`rp2_quotient.py`):
- The antipodal map `−I` is a **symmetry of the cube tiling** — *asserted*, not presumed (`assert every face's antipodal image is a face`). Because it is, the tiling **DESCENDS**: cube `(V8,E12,F6, χ=2)` → hemicube `(4,6,3, χ=1)`.
- **The vertex is UNTOUCHED** — still 3 squares, still 270°, still a 90° deficit. **Locally indistinguishable from the sphere; globally not.** This is the gate's own local/global distinction, here **TOPOLOGICAL rather than metric** — the same two-singular-sets frame as [[the-fold-is-a-cone-on-RP2-not-an-axis]].
- **Gauss–Bonnet holds on both sides:** total deficit `= 2π·χ` (cube 720°=4π, χ2; hemicube 360°=2π, χ1).
- **The "disk with antipodal boundary" ARISES, it is not imported:** stereographically the antipodal map is `z ↦ −1/z̄` (radius `r ↦ 1/r`, angle `θ ↦ θ+π`); the unit circle (the equator) is fixed as a set with each point sent straight across; the southern hemisphere is a fundamental DISK whose rim is glued antipodally.

> ### ⛔ **THE DESCENT LAW: a tiling descends to a quotient surface iff the identifying symmetry σ is (a) a symmetry of the tiling and (b) FREE (fixed-point-free). The engine MUST CHECK both — never assume.**
> Measured precedent (parked, ✔ re-derived here against `rp2_quotient.py`'s method): **cube ✔ · octahedron ✔ · tetrahedron ✘** — the tetrahedron is not centrally symmetric, so `−I ∉ Sym` and the tiling does NOT descend. **The tetra ✘ is the LAW-24 positive control: the check is real because it FAILS where it must.**

★★ **This closes two questions with one definition.** The `−I ∈ Sym(tiling)` descent check IS the **free-involution condition** of the RP²-from-the-octahedron ruling ([[op-set-is-pairwise-identify-not-group-quotient]]): descent-to-RP² and the tiling-symmetry check are ONE mechanism. And the freeness clause is load-bearing exactly as ruled there — *at least three isometries send a vertex to its antipode, and the two with fixed points quotient to an ORBIFOLD, not RP²* ([[vertex-identification-is-a-wedge-out-of-scope]]): **under-determination does not blur the answer, it changes the KIND of object.** So σ must be pinned as an operand (the symmetry), and its freeness measured, before descent.

### 4 · THE TWO TRAPS — verified, on the record, both bite (LAW 24: a seal must exhibit the wrong mechanism failing)

- ⛔ **`cosh R = cot(π/p)·cot(π/q)` — NOT the naive `cos(π/p)/sin(π/q)`.** The naive form makes the cells too small and the corner comes out **79.47° instead of 72°** for {4,5}. *Caught by MEASURING the corner angle, not by looking.* ⇒ the build's witness asserts the corner angle `= 360/q` to 0.01°, and the interior vertex valence set `= {q}` exactly.
- ⛔ **Dedup tolerance MUST scale as `(1 − |c|²)`** (c = the cell-centre in the disk). Repeated circle-inversions drift; exact rounding never re-matches, so the frontier walk **unrolls into a TREE (1+4+12+36+… = 4373 for {4,5})** instead of closing its cycles. *Caught by noticing the tile count was exactly the tree count.* ⇒ the witness asserts the tile count is the CLOSED-cycle count, never the tree count.

### 5 · THE PERSON'S EYE — the render laws that bind any claim about what he sees (`docs/design/adr/0004`)

- **The rim is addressed, never drawn as a wall** (horizon doctrine): the Poincaré boundary is INFINITY; the sphere's pole-cell is the whole EXTERIOR (the far side shows through — *"antipodes are never both on the near side"*, the S²/RP² fixture's constraint). A rim drawn as a solid edge is the horizon lie.
- **LOD: the mark STOPS, never degrades** — a cell whose perimeter falls below the floor is DROPPED, not drawn wrong (the `min_edge` cutoff). A degraded mark is a mark that stops meaning; an absent one is honest.
- **LAW 24 (a negative needs a positive control):** no "this surface has no tiling / no descent" claim without the case where it MUST appear — the tetra ✘ for descent; the flat {4,4} as the null control for curvature.
- **Countable captions (LAW 23), SHARPENED by §7:** a number "checkable in principle" is not the mark if the person is never ASKED to check it — a caption that ASSERTS the count is OURS, not his. The mark is the phenomenon he reads (§7); the count lives in the record. The countable thing at his eye is *how many places his inhabitant-mark appears* (§7.1: two = descended, one = not), not a caption's assertion.

### 6 · THE FALLBACK — on record (clause 3)

If a geometry (or a surface) admits **no derived tiling method** under §2, the standing options, in order: **(a) approximate a tiling convention** (a nearest regular {p,q} with the honest deficit named), or **(b) move to another standardized representation** of the surface. **Neither is a silent degrade** — the approximation or the switch is named to the person, per §5.

### 7.1 · THE ANTIPODAL-IDENTIFICATION MARK (W-4) — an INHABITANT's doubled image, never the descent prose

> ### ⛔ **RULED: the visible mark of a descent (antipodal identification) is an INHABITANT — an ASYMMETRIC marker drawn in one cell whose ANTIPODAL IMAGE is visible in the partner cell** (via the fixture's far-side-shows-through: the partner faint, at once). **The person sees the SAME asymmetric mark in two places and reads "these two cells are one" — the identification SHOWN.** The descent SENTENCE and the `−I ∈ Sym` check demote to the RECORD (our proof), like the count.
> **LAW-24 CONTROL (binding): a NON-descended spherical window (the plain cube {4,3}, no identification) must show the inhabitant's mark ONCE — no antipodal double.** Descended ⟺ the mark appears in a cell AND its antipode; non-descended ⟺ once. That is the countable phenomenon (LAW 23: *how many places does my mark appear* — two vs one).
> ⚠ **The window carries NONE of the class-body's own marks** — "the pinch" Arman saw is the cross-cap BODY's pinch-point bleeding through the frame; the window's mark is the inhabitant's double, nothing of the body's.
