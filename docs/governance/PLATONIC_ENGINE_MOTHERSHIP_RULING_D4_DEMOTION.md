# PlatonicEngine Mothership Ruling — D4 Demotion
## Re-deriving the D4 sitting's purpose after the IV-A negative and the Option-A pivot

Audience: the human (Arman, sovereign) and the prompter/planner/auditor (lieutenant).

Status: binding ruling on the sovereign's challenge to the D4 sitting. Issued: 2026-06-11.

## 1. Findings

```txt
1. A non-mechanical judgment DOES exist (the challenge's option C is non-empty):
   comparative reading-usability of true content — whether structure-with-
   statuses helps a human distinguish, orient among, and correctly trust the
   six sites better than the baseline. The 185 assertions check what the text
   claims; they cannot check what a reader can do with it.

2. But the only KIND of legibility judgable now is NAVIGATIONAL (distinguish /
   orient / trust). DWELLING legibility (naming value, semantic usefulness)
   requires layers that do not exist (carrier-borne field, semantic/Trison
   reading) and is premature by construction.

3. Post-pivot, D4's gating force is gone:
   - old Station V (UI) — D4's original downstream — is SUPERSEDED by the
     successor charter's forward ladder;
   - replacing the old reading surface is forced by HONESTY alone (its
     field-pressure language is disproven by IV-A), needing no sitting;
   - what remains is note-sized: the composite verdict's human-fruit clause
     and a fresh-eyes calibration baseline for the successor's F-IV.

4. Mothership error, owned: the IV-A addendum reshaped D4's CONTENT but did
   not re-derive its DECISION-WEIGHT after the Option-A/successor ruling.
   A gate survived by inertia. The sovereign's challenge caught it.
```

## 2. Ruling

```txt
1. IV-B formal honesty: ACCEPTED as audited (185/185, contract-hash chain
   verified, baseline fairness confirmed with the legibility-preview annex).
   The IV-B build stands.

2. D4 is DEMOTED from gate to D4-NOTE: an optional, non-blocking, time-boxed
   (~15 min) recording. If the sovereign chooses to hold it: per-site
   navigational judgments (improves / no improvement / worse), the halo rule
   still applying, judgments recorded in the closing memo and carried as the
   F-IV fresh-eyes baseline. If declined: the F-IV baseline is the archived
   artifact pair (baseline reading + revised reading + annex), to be judged
   side-by-side at F-IV time, with the contamination caveat recorded
   (carrier-trained eyes).

3. The honest source-state reader REPLACES the pre-campaign reading as the
   standing surface for the proving event — by honesty, not by sitting: the
   old reading's field-pressure framing is disproven. The do-not-trust-raw-
   field warning ships with it.

4. Old Station V is formally SUPERSEDED by the successor charter's forward
   ladder (display contract after F-IV). Recorded; no further old-campaign
   stations exist beyond closure.

5. CAMPAIGN CLOSE proceeds now: the lieutenant drafts the closing memo —
   composite verdict per the taxonomy notice ("a generalizable source-state
   carrier law, field-inactive under the accepted emission stack"), seals
   revealed against hashes, the human-fruit clause worded per whether the
   D4-note was held ("navigational improvement recorded" or "human fruit
   untested-by-sitting; reader replacement forced by honesty").

6. NEXT REAL WORK, confirmed exactly as the challenge names it: the successor
   campaign's Gate 0 — the carrier/fiber observable field validity contract
   (well-defined gauge-invariant holonomy under the Moufang algebra) — upon
   charter finalization at close.
```

## 3. Charter adjustment

The Carrier-Borne Field Campaign charter draft's finalization condition 2 ("the D4 baseline is recorded into Station F-IV") is amended to: "the F-IV baseline is recorded — either the D4-note (if held) or the archived artifact pair with the contamination caveat."

## 4. Standing lesson (added to campaign law)

```txt
Every gate must be re-derived when the ground under it moves. A gate whose
outcomes no longer change any decision is ceremony, and ceremony is cut on
sight — the same discipline applied to features applies to process.
```
