## Ruling 2 — Anti-staple / earned-field-activity discipline: RATIFIED, with two riders

Sections §4(1)–(5) and the bright-line test are ratified verbatim as binding design constraints on any new field. Two mothership riders close the gaps the drafted discipline leaves open:

```txt
RIDER A — NO TARGET-MATCHING IN RECONSTRUCTION.
  The discipline as drafted regulates emitted observables. It must equally
  regulate the OBSERVER: the label-blind reconstruction procedure may not
  hard-code expected holonomy/closure values as matching targets. Recovery
  is scored against blind structured + strict controls (the IV-A pattern),
  never against the values the designer hopes to find. A reconstructor that
  would still "succeed" if the field were replaced by a control voids the run.

RIDER B — DESIGN/CERTIFICATION SEPARATION (one-shot sealed certification).
  Motivated design's subtlest form is the tuning loop: adjust the emission
  law until blind recovery passes, then call the final iteration "the test."
  Therefore: design runs and certification runs are formally distinct.
  A certification run requires (i) the emission law FROZEN and hash-committed,
  (ii) sealed predictions filed to mothership, (iii) one shot. A failed
  certification may be followed by redesign, but each redesign->certification
  cycle is counted, budgeted, and disclosed; kill criteria (Ruling 6) bound
  the number of cycles. Tuning inside a certification run voids it.
```

The leak-scan + closed-blinded-types machinery from IV-A is mandatory equipment in every certification run.

## Ruling 6 — Corrected observable target and the bracketing risk: CONFIRMED, and made the FIRST GATE

The field-activity target of the successor campaign is the gauge-INVARIANT relations (holonomies, closures, invariant pairings), not raw-carrier recovery. This is internally grounded: Bench-2 proved the lift is gauge (values vary per labeling) while closure/holonomy are invariant across 7x24=168. A raw label is bookkeeping; only a transported, invariant relation can be physics. The anti-staple line is thereby principled, not just procedural.

The non-associativity problem is acknowledged as the CENTRAL TECHNICAL RISK, in the lieutenant's exact framing: the A0 associator residues and the deeper-generation bracketing problem are RELOCATED into the connection/holonomy definition, not solved by the gauge framing. Consequence, binding:

```txt
GATE 0 OF THE SUCCESSOR CAMPAIGN — OBSERVABLE BEFORE BROADCAST.
  Before ANY field emission work, the campaign must prove its observable
  exists: a model card + finite diagnostic establishing a well-defined
  holonomy on the medial hub under the octonionic (Moufang) algebra —
  either bracketing-INVARIANT for the loop classes used (lead to chase:
  Moufang diassociativity — any two elements generate an associative
  subloop; loop classes whose link variables are 2-generated may have
  theorem-level bracketing independence), or bracketing-POLICY-EXPLICIT
  with the policy's gauge-invariance proven. If the holonomy cannot be
  made well-defined, the campaign's kill criteria fire BEFORE the
  expensive field build, not after.

KILL CRITERIA (to be enumerated in the charter, non-negotiable categories):
  - Gate 0 failure: no well-defined gauge-invariant holonomy obtainable;
  - certification exhaustion: N sealed design->certification cycles fail
    blind recovery (N fixed in the charter, before the first cycle);
  - staple detection: any certification voided by leak/target-matching is
    disclosed and counts toward N;
  - the honest negative: if the richest defensible emission still cannot
    carry the invariants, the composite verdict hardens and the field
    layer's boundary is final for this architecture family.
```

The octonion caution stands as drafted: Fano/octonion/G2 is the proven local fiber chart, not a declared field ontology. The disciplinary analogies (lattice gauge, Wilson loops, G2) are templates whose mapping must be EARNED through Gate 0 and the gauntlet — affirmed exactly as the lieutenant framed it.
