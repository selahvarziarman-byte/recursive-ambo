## 1. What the field layer was supposed to be

Two different things have been called "the field layer." The founding documents define the first; the campaigns escalated into the second.

### ORIGINAL (the founding definition)

```txt
- Ground Plan section 4.3 / 12 (docs/PLATONIC_ENGINE_GROUND_PLAN.md):
    "a derived phenomenon of the current closed shape" answering
    "What field arises if vertices are treated as sources?" — derived, bounded,
    disableable, never a historical operation.

- Field Layer Appendix section 2 / 23 (docs/PLATONIC_ENGINE_FIELD_LAYER_APPENDIX.md):
    "a dynamic source-population interference atlas over a selected geometric
    source-domain." Every active vertex (INCLUDING born Ambo children) is a wave
    source; it computes complex superposition psi = sum c_i, intensity |psi|^2,
    phase, genealogical contribution ratios, and later features (nodes, gates,
    routes, supports). Its stated job: "It does not tell us what a site means. It
    tells us what the world makes possible, difficult, reinforced, cancelled,
    mixed, or blocked" — i.e. it SUPPLIES CONSTRAINTS for later interpretation.

- Event Legibility Pivot Charter section 4 (docs/governance/...PIVOT_CHARTER.md):
    the field "loses sovereignty" and is one WITNESS around a generated site:
    "Field says what the active source-domain reinforces, cancels, mixes, blocks,
    tensions, or leaves ambiguous."
```

So the field layer was meant to be a **modest, derived interference / constraint atlas — a reading aid, not a sovereign object.**

### THE ESCALATION (what was actually chased, and just closed absent)

```txt
- The Appendix left exactly ONE thing open (section 8): the source-parameter
  POLICY for born children. Section 8.3 sketched an "atomic-aware policy" — a
  child computed from parents plus projection vertices ("AB | C", "AB | (C,D)").

- That open slot became the Fano / octonionic CARRIER policy
  (FANO_OCTONIONIC_CARRIER_GRAPH_PROJECTION_LAW_MODEL_CARD_V0.md;
  src/lib/fanoOctonionic*). The campaigns then asked a FAR bigger question than the
  Appendix ever did: is the carrier / fiber structure a FIELD-ACTIVE OBSERVABLE?

- That carrier-borne octonionic FIELD OBJECT — not the interference atlas — is what
  the field-generalizability -> carrier-borne-field -> W-gate campaigns chased, and
  what just closed ABSENT (loop channel: a theorem; first-birth higher form: trivial).
```

**Bottom line for question 1:** what was proven absent is the *escalated carrier-borne field observable*. The *original* interference atlas is a separate, far more modest thing (`src/lib/fieldAtlas*`, `fieldSampler.ts`, `fieldCueV0.ts`), and the charter always permitted it to "honestly report when it does not" contribute. Setting it aside is consistent with the founding design — not a loss of the project's center.

## 2. The project's center was never the field

```txt
- Ground Plan section 1 / 12: "A generated world should remain intelligible after
  transformation."
- Pivot Charter section 0 / 4 / 13: the central question is "What concept can dwell
  at this generated site?"; "the generated site is the center"; success = "Does the
  generated event let the human understand something they could not understand before?"
```

The field was always a servant of legibility. The carrier campaigns were a long, disciplined, honest **detour** into the field's hardest sub-question — the very "monster that postpones the human act of understanding" the charter warns against (section 15). With it closed, the project returns to its stated center, intact.

## 4. The one-line answer

The field layer was meant to be a derived interference / constraint atlas serving generated-site legibility — a witness, not the goal. The carrier-borne field OBSERVABLE escalation is what was chased and is now absent. **What remains is the project's original center, untouched by that negative:** make the named-tetrahedron -> Ambo -> generated-site event humanly legible (the honest reader is already the field-free foundation), then build out the atomic birth-law witness, and only when named material demands it, the semantic-topological workspace. None of the remaining work depends on the field being field-active.
