## 1. What PlatonicEngine is (the necessary minimum)

PlatonicEngine is a geometric-semantic workspace. Its surface subject is polyhedra
under a repeated operation; its real subject is whether a GENERATED WORLD stays
intelligible after transformation.

The one operation that matters for you is the **Ambo** (rectification): replace a
polyhedron's edges by their midpoints and reconnect. Applied repeatedly it makes a
lineage:

```txt
tetrahedron --Ambo--> octahedron --Ambo--> cuboctahedron --Ambo--> ...
```

The midpoints born at each step are not passive points. The project's binding intuition
is that **generated children must become sources** — they must inherit something
legitimate from their parents and from the operation that bore them, and a "field"
should arise from these sources as a derived phenomenon.

The cuboctahedron is the convergence object of the first two steps and is mathematically
special: it is the **A3 root polytope** (its 12 vertices are the 12 roots eps_i - eps_j),
it is the vector equilibrium (edge = circumradius, adjacency 60 deg), and its central
structure is four hexagonal great circles = four A2 subsystems. These facts are not
decoration; they are proven load-bearing results you will stand on (§4).

That is all the project you need to begin. The rest is the problem.
