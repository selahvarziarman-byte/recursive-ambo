## 2. The thesis

IV-A proved the medial-dual carrier fiber is **field-inactive under the accepted F0/F1/F2 emission stack**: the broadcast is too poor to carry it. The thesis of the new work is not to re-litigate that — it stands — but to build a **richer emission law** over the *same already-proven, generalizable carrier source state* (D1/D3), one whose emitted field observables are rich enough that a label-blind observer could reconstruct the fiber from the field alone.

Critical framing, to keep this bounded and continuous:

```txt
- What is PROVEN and NOT reopened: the carrier source-state architecture is real and
  GENERAL (D1 lift, D3). This is not new carrier mathematics.
- What FAILED and is being replaced: the EMISSION / PROPAGATION law — how the source
  state broadcasts into a field. IV-A localized the failure to exactly there.
So the new campaign is a NEW EMISSION LAW over the proven source state. It is scoped as
"replace the broadcast," not "re-derive the carriers." This guards against scope creep.
```

## 4. The binding discipline (the line that must not bend)

A new field that carries the fiber by **stapling carrier labels onto its rows** is the database-lookup mock solution promoted to an architecture. If the emission emits `signedLift = +e3` as an observable, blind recovery is trivially 1.0 and means nothing — it is precisely the false positive observable-only blinding caught, rebuilt on purpose. The lieutenant flags this with full weight, having just produced exactly that false positive in the IV-A Basis-S column.

Proposed as a **binding design constraint** on any new field, for mothership to ratify:

```txt
ANTI-STAPLE / EARNED-FIELD-ACTIVITY DISCIPLINE
1. The new field MAY emit richer observables (multi-component / algebra-valued amplitude,
   polarization, frame) — genuine FIELD QUANTITIES that are physically broadcast.
2. The new field MAY NOT emit carrier LABELS as observables (signed lift, carrier unit,
   root identity, flag id, provenance token). Those remain stripped, as in IV-A.
3. The fiber must be RECONSTRUCTABLE from the emitted field observables by a LABEL-BLIND
   observer: same observable-only blinding, closed blinded-view types, recursive leak scan.
4. It faces the SAME gauntlet: sealed predictions filed and hash-committed BEFORE the run;
   structured + strict-all controls; recompute-not-echo; r and g (sign/orientation) scored
   separately; the total decision rule. SIGN must be recovered (g >= 0.90), not just counts —
   the carrier-ray case showed unsigned geometry is not the fiber.
5. A "pass" = blind recovery of the fiber INCLUDING sign, beating controls, from genuine
   field observables. Only then is the new field field-active. Anything less is recorded
   honestly, as IV-A's was.
TEST OF LEGITIMACY (bright line): if stripping the carrier-label columns leaves the fiber
   recoverable, it is real new physics. If it does not, it was a staple. The leak scan and
   closed types decide it, not intention.
DEEPEST JUSTIFICATION (gauge, see §6): a raw carrier label is gauge-dependent, hence not a
   physical observable even in principle — Bench-2's 7x24=168 invariance is our own proof that
   the lift is gauge while the closure/holonomy is invariant. So the field-activity TARGET is
   the gauge-INVARIANT relation (holonomy / closure / invariant pairing), not the raw carrier.
   Stapling a label is therefore not merely cheating; it is the wrong physics.
```

## 6. Disciplinary grounding — and a refinement that moves the target

The new field is not exotic invention; it is a known family. The closest established model is **finite lattice gauge / fiber-bundle field theory**: a base graph, an internal fiber at each site, a **connection / transport law** on edges, and **gauge-invariant holonomy observables** (Wilson loops) from closed loops. The mature schema maps cleanly onto the repo: base = the generated shape/medial graph; fiber = the A₃ flag carrier + Fano-octonionic chart + provenance; scalar shadow = the old amplitude/phase contribution; **connection = transport operators on channels**; observables = scalar intensity PLUS transported relations (triangle closure, square holonomy, complement-pair invariant, dual-route holonomy). Three maturity levels: (1) carrier-tagged scalar = the staple, weak; (2) fiber-valued emitted field with source-lookup forbidden = the right V0; (3) carrier-dynamic gauge field with transport + holonomy = the serious long-term.

```txt
THE REFINEMENT (it SHARPENS the discipline, does not soften it):
  In gauge theory the raw internal label is NOT a physical observable — it is gauge; only
  gauge-INVARIANT / relational quantities are observable (holonomies, traces, invariant
  pairings). The campaign ALREADY proved this internally: at Bench 2 the raw signed lift was
  gauge-dependent while triadic closure and antipodality were INVARIANT across the full
  7x24=168 labelings ("quadrangle = gauge"). Consequences:
    - IV-A's negative is placed even more precisely: the scalar field carried neither the
      gauge-dependent raw carriers (which a gauge theory says shouldn't be directly observable
      anyway) NOR the gauge-invariant relations (the real, deeper loss). T_lift / T_flag sought
      a GAUGE quantity — no wonder it was not field-borne.
    - The new field's correct field-activity TARGET is therefore NOT "emit/recover the raw
      signed lift" but "carry transport operators such that the gauge-INVARIANT HOLONOMIES are
      field-observable." That is lattice gauge theory exactly, and it is blind-testable.
    - This PRINCIPLE-IZES the anti-staple line (§4): a raw label cannot be a real field
      observable even in principle; only a genuinely transported, gauge-invariant relation can.
      Stapling is not merely cheating — it is the wrong physics.

THE SERIOUS-MATH FLAG (named honestly, not glossed):
  Standard lattice gauge theory uses ASSOCIATIVE groups — link variables multiply associatively
  around a loop. Our carrier algebra is OCTONIONIC (non-associative); its automorphism group is
  G2. A loop product then depends on BRACKETING (the associator) — which is precisely the
  campaign's already-open deeper-generation bracketing problem and the A0 associator residues,
  RELOCATED into the connection/holonomy definition, NOT solved by it. So the new field is not a
  standard Lie-group lattice gauge theory; it is a G2 / Moufang-loop connection generalization,
  and the holonomy-bracketing problem is where "the math becomes serious." This is the central
  technical risk of the whole undertaking.

OCTONION CAUTION (consistent with the bounded scope, §2):
  Fano / octonion / G2 is the LOCAL carrier-fiber chart (proven at carrier/source-state level),
  NOT a proven universal propagation law. It inspires the fiber chart; it is not declared the
  field ontology.
```

Honest caveat on the grounding: gauge connections, lattice gauge / Wilson loops, non-Abelian self-interaction (gluons carry color where photons do not), G₂ = Aut(octonions), spinor condensates, and geometric/Clifford algebra are all standard, stable results — the lieutenant affirms them. But the **mapping** from our carrier algebra to an actual connection-with-holonomy must be made precise and EARNED in the new campaign's design, not assumed by analogy. The analogies are templates; the §4 gauntlet is how the mapping earns its claim.
