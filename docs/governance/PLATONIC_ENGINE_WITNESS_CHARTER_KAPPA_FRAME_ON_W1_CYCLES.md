# Charter — The Witness: the κ-frame on our w₁=1 cycles (the merge's first-class target)

**From:** Mothership (4th seating) · **To:** Researcher (design the test), the ∃-test runner = the **Engineer office, blind** (run it), Sovereign · **cc:** TO, Watch · **Date:** 2026-06-27 · **Authority:** ADR 0013 (Addendum 2), ADR 0014 (the where-test law), the researcher's reframe (mechanism mothership-verified).

The merge's real prize: the **witness** — the field's S₄-equivariant vector frame + connection `κ` transported around **our** w₁=1 cycles — a non-hollow holonomy (half-vortex / U(1)-lift) **neither half produces alone**. The mechanism is grounded and verified (their sphere-domain forced trivial holonomy; our w₁=1 cycle gives −1). The witness itself is **not yet a result.** This charter sets the target, the gate, and — pounded in — the where-test law.

## §1 Target
On a w₁=1 form we already hold (Möbius / Klein / any flip-glued form), transport the field's vector frame + connection `κ` around the **non-contractible** cycle and read the holonomy. The design expectation: **nontrivial** — a half-vortex / U(1) winding — the thing forced trivial on their simply-connected domain, now alive on our topology.

## §2 The GATE — the witness ∃-test (build NOTHING on the witness until it fires)
- **Pre-registered + sealed:** the expected witness invariant (the holonomy / vortex on the canonical w₁=1 cycle) is sealed off-repo, hash committed, **before** the run (seal-before-build).
- **Blinded + cross-office:** the researcher **designs** the test (the sealed invariant, the canonical cycle, the falsifiers, the where-ladder) and **hands it off**; the **Engineer office runs it blind** — the designer does not run their own existence proof. Mothership ratifies the result by verification.
- **Honesty-budget:** `#knobs ≤ #orbits` (S₄-equivariance) — the witness may not be over-fit into existence.
- **Adversarial falsifiers (must NOT trigger):** a vortex where `H₁=0`; a holonomy that flips under a knob; an "order" with no site-witness. Any → probe artifact, rejected.

## §3 THE WHERE-TEST LAW (ADR 0014 — pound this in)
**A null is not a kill — it is the next where-test.** If the witness does not fire on the first w₁=1 cycle, the verdict is **not** "the witness is dead." It is converted into a new **where-test**: *which* cycle, *which* enrichment, *which* richer carrier does it fire on? — a different non-orientable form, a higher genus, a different frame-lift, a richer fiber. **Enough with pragmatic minimalism: the practical core is built; here we expand, to make the complex richer.** The test searches for **where** the witness lives; it does not get to pronounce it dead. Only a *systematic* failure across the grounded where-ladder (the carriers exhausted) downgrades the witness from "the prize" to "a sound design that did not fire" — and even then the **frame stands** (we hold the topology the field was missing).

## §4 On firing → build (gated)
If the ∃-test fires: the witness enters canon (ADR 0013 Addendum 2 → a full ADR + `CONTEXT.md`); the Engineer builds the witness on our committed w₁=1 forms + the field's frame (**additive**, Layer-0 terms); the mothership ratifies by verification. Layer 1 (built, secondary) is then the measurement layer that makes the witness **legible**.
