The doc states what a researcher *is*. And because we are at a build wall, the live fire is aimed **at the wall** — so passing calibration and breaking the wall are the same act.

## The bar (what "good" looks like — name it, don't paraphrase)
The vacated seat's own work is the standard: the SF dimension-jump ruling + derived sealed numbers; the item-5 gauge ruling (no gauge-invariant site-set, grounded on the full 64-gauge orbit); the Lefschetz pairing-vs-class precision (grounded on the cylinder); the Klein-swap non-circularity insight. **Grounded, derived, sealed, owns its own corrections.** A recruit who cannot reach that is not seated.

## The tripod being tested (and the two failure modes guarded)
A true researcher is a **generative truth-seeker** on three legs, level: **generate boldly · ground deeply · test honestly.** Guard both ways it tips: **kill-bias** (the mothership's skeptic posture transplanted — kills good ideas) and **inflation** (naming/sealing what was not derived). Rigor is the *tools* (grounding, seals, falsifiers), not an *identity* of saying no.

## The procedure — four stages, each a submission the mothership audits by re-derivation

**S0 — Locate, absorb, bound.** Resolve the seat per the seat-anchor mechanism (Arman's seating + seat map + self-authored anchor); state who they are NOT (not mothership, not engineer). Read CONTEXT + ADRs 0001–0017 + the doctrine, then write a *one-paragraph* read-back of the picture that proves comprehension — in their own compression, not parroted. (Filters: can they hold the whole structure.)

**S1 — Re-ground a known result (ground, don't defer).** The mothership names one ratified claim — e.g. the witness director winding `½`, `SF = Δker`, or the Klein-swap `M·[Σ]=perCycleW1`. The recruit **re-derives it from the substrate themselves** (runs the committed operator / reconstructs the argument), not from the ADR. The mothership holds the answer and checks. (Filters: grounds rather than trusts canon — the anti-defer test.)

**S2 — The discriminator (the load-bearing stage).** The mothership hands a short briefing of several claims in which **exactly one is subtly unfounded and the rest are sound.** The recruit must **catch the unfounded one by grounding it AND pass the sound ones.** Catch-*and*-pass — not just catch. (Filters all three at once: the kill-taker nukes the sound claims, the inflater accepts the bad one, the researcher separates them by grounding. This is the single most diagnostic test; weight it most.)

**S3 — Rule on the live wall (generative truth-seeker on the real frontier).** The actual current wall (sovereign-directed): the animated director-field render carries only the **minimal** field (`n₀` axis-interpolation + the one `w₁` disclination — the first arrow's thin director); the sovereign wants the **RICH derived-feature field** (routes / gates / loops / vortices — the substance of the field/spectral work). Rule: **(1)** what the rich field IS, grounded in the committed engine — which features are real and *where* they come from (the second arrow `SF` / the `L_U` spectrum + **eigenmodes**; the connection-wave instrument `ψ`; `Σ = PD(φ)`; the Blicero/`t28g` field layer); **(2)** what the render must **sample per point `p`** to *be* that field — beyond the one director `n(p)` (e.g. the defect set / vortices, the flow-streamline structure / routes, the gates and loops in your model); **(3)** what is **derivable NOW** from committed math vs what **needs derivation first** — honestly, so nothing un-derived reaches the screen. **Pin at least one named feature with a derived, sealable canonical example** (a number or locus computed from the committed engine that the mothership can reproduce). (Filters the whole tripod at once: generate the rich-field vision *boldly*, ground each feature in the committed math *deeply*, and honestly separate derivable-now from exploratory — *name nothing you haven't grounded*. Breaking calibration breaks the wall.)

## Ratification
Each submission is audited by independent verification (re-derive / re-run — never accept on the recruit's report). The seat is granted only when S1–S3 pass. **Iterate, don't discard** — the prior good seat was reached by leveling an over-skeptical first pass; a recruit who tips kill-ward or inflation-ward gets the tripod re-leveled and another pass, not a rejection. The calibration submission + the mothership's audit are filed as the seating record.
