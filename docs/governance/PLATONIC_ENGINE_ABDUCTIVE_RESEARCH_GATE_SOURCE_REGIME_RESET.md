## 4. Binding discipline on the W-gate (the mothership value-add)

### 4.1 Legitimacy-before-activity is a re-derivation of proven campaign law

```txt
The reset's "legitimacy before field activity" IS the campaign's own Station-I
(source-state-real) vs Station-IV (field-active) distinction, generalized. This is
not new caution invented for a new hope — it is the exact discipline that produced
the IV-A negative. Blessed as the ordering law: no field-observability claim for
any W until that W's child sources are proven LEGITIMATE (geometric/combinatorial/
source-state validity, equivariant, derived-not-inserted, lineage-correct).
```

### 4.2 The same gauntlet — no easier ride for a newer, more exciting W

```txt
A new W is intellectually seductive precisely because it is new ("operation-born
local world," "vortex-like mediation"). Seduction is where motivated reasoning
enters. Therefore every W candidate faces the SAME machinery that broke W_0:
  - sealed predictions (VALUES, not just structure), hash-committed before runs;
  - anti-staple + Rider A (no target-matching in any recovery/legitimacy scorer);
  - design/certification separation + Rider B (frozen, one-shot, counted cycles);
  - blind controls, reality-non-genericity checks, mock-scramble;
  - legitimacy diagnostics must FAIL if the construction's defining facts are
    removed (the mock-solution test at the ontology level);
  - observability, when reached, is blind-recovery scored (Rec(O) beats controls
    without label leakage) — direct OR mediated, but always blind.
A W that would only pass because it is described beautifully fails.
```

### 4.3 Finite-hole boundedness law (binding)

```txt
A finite hole is admissible in a candidate W ONLY IF:
  - it is a single named slot with an ENUMERATED candidate set (e.g. reverse law
    in {negation, return-complement, <named third>}), not an open "somehow";
  - the REST of W is fixed and testable while the hole's branches are swept;
  - each branch is independently finite-diagnosable;
  - the hole is declared in advance, in the W model card, before any run.
A "hole" that is really "the law that makes the test pass, TBD" is FORBIDDEN —
it is the motivated-design escape hatch wearing a research costume. The number of
open holes is itself budgeted: a W carrying more than a charter-fixed few open
holes is not a candidate, it is a wish.
```

### 4.4 Budget and verdict

```txt
- The W-gate is plan-mode-first. Phase structure (§5). Each phase: plan ->
  mothership audit -> finite diagnostic (sealed) -> memo.
- The gate produces one of:
    W-PASS-LEGITIMATE: a named W under which recursive Ambo (at least tetra->octa,
      and tested toward octa->cubocta) yields legitimate child sources, with only
      bounded named holes. -> authorizes the field-observability sub-question.
    W-LOCAL-ONLY: legitimate at one generation, fails or needs controlled
      extension at the next. -> scope named, recorded, escalated.
    W-FAIL: no abducted W within budget makes births lawful without unbounded
      holes. -> a real finding: the field layer is not yet foundational, and the
      reason is named. (This is the K-class kill outcome for the W-gate.)
- Budget: the abduction (model-card) phase is research-design, but it is TIME/
  CYCLE-bounded like every station; a W-gate that produces neither a testable
  candidate nor a failure finding within budget is itself escalated to the
  sovereign as a possible signal that the object is not abductable.
```
