# Platonic Engine Gate C.5 Multi-Projection Source-State Acceptance Review

## 1. Status

Gate C.5 accepts the multi-projection source-state regime v0 as the source-state basis for later FieldCueV0 adaptation.

This is a governance acceptance of a source-state regime, not a runtime implementation change, not a FieldCueV0 adaptation, not a GeneratedSiteReadingV0 unblock, and not adoption of a general reduction law.

## 2. Purpose

This memo records the final Gate C decision after the C.4, C.4D, C.4L-D1, C.4L-D2, and C.4L-D3 diagnostic sequence.

The purpose is to decide whether the project may use a multi-projection source-state regime as the basis for future FieldCueV0 work while preserving the governing law that event legibility comes before field expansion.

## 3. Authority context

The controlling authority remains the event legibility pivot charter:

- Generated sites are the interpretive center.
- Field-facing outputs must not be treated as more truthful than their declared source-state basis.
- Human names and semantic labels remain downstream readings, not source-state evidence.
- Field expansion must not precede event legibility.

The structured source-state amendment remains binding:

- The source signature is the structured source state.
- The emitted scalar tuple is a field-facing reduction.
- Reduction honesty is required.
- A field cue cannot be more truthful than the source-state regime that produced it.

The FieldCueV0 target contract remains binding:

- FieldCueV0 must be a field witness, not a semantic oracle.
- It must preserve generated-site centering.
- It must not overclaim human names, antipodal semantics, or source identity from field behavior alone.

## 4. Evidence summary

Gate C.3 showed that R4-S1 passes emitted-source recovery. It recovered 3/3 antipodal axes from emitted tuple behavior.

Gate C.4 showed that R4-S1 fails raw field-behavior recovery. Raw field behavior recovered only 1/3 with false positives. Geometry-only recovery passed for all regimes, exposing geometry-coded behavior risk. FieldCueV0 and GeneratedSiteReadingV0 remained blocked.

Gate C.4D preserved diagnostic integrity but found only an ambiguous residual. The residual diagnostic recovered only 1/3 with false positives, so R4-S1 was not promoted.

Gate C.4L-D1 separated emitted-tuple success from raw field-behavior failure. R4-S1 remained a baseline failure under raw field behavior. C4L-O1 was ambiguous and insufficient. C4L-S1 passed only under a known-propagation-dephased structural-channel basis. Gate C.5 was not authorized by that diagnostic alone.

Gate C.4L-D3 established the relevant witness under an explicit multi-projection basis:

- `projectionModelId = multi-projection-source-state-v0`
- `candidateLawId = c4l-s2-structural-carrier-split-v0`
- generated child projection count: 6
- propagation projection count: 6
- structural projection count: 6
- relation visibility count: 3
- structural-channel recovery: 3/3
- false positives: 0
- ambiguity: 1
- `structuralChannelCandidateStatus = structural-channel-witness-supported`
- FieldCueV0 remained blocked
- GeneratedSiteReadingV0 remained blocked
- Gate C.5 remained `not-authorized-by-this-diagnostic`
- `reductionLawAdoptionStatus = not-adopted`

The C.4L-D4 review found that C.4L-D3 is a structural-channel witness, not a raw-field victory. It is sufficient evidence for a Gate C.5 governance decision only if the accepted regime remains explicitly multi-projection and reduction-honest.

## 5. Accepted regime

Gate C.5 accepts the multi-projection source-state regime v0.

The accepted regime has these components:

- Structured source state remains the source signature.
- The emitted scalar tuple remains a propagation-facing reduction only.
- The propagation projection carries ordinary field behavior.
- The structural projection carries source-state structural relations.
- Relation visibility statuses are required.
- Reduction honesty is required.
- Raw-field failure remains a caveat, not an erased result.
- Structural-channel recovery is accepted as a field-facing witness only under the declared multi-projection basis.
- FieldCueV0 must later consume the split honestly if and when it is adapted.

## 6. Rejected or demoted regimes

Gate C.5 rejects scalar tuple behavior as a full source signature.

Gate C.5 rejects R4-S1 as a field-behavior law. R4-S1 remains retained only as an emitted-tuple success.

Gate C.5 preserves R0 metadata-only structure as a control only.

Gate C.5 preserves the Pythagorean scalar regime as a harmonic baseline only.

Gate C.5 preserves C4L-O1 orbit-common carrier as a control and bounded diagnostic family only.

Gate C.5 rejects raw propagated field behavior as a sufficient witness for antipodality.

Gate C.5 rejects treating the structural channel as semantic naming.

Gate C.5 rejects automatic FieldCueV0 unblocking.

## 7. Acceptance criteria satisfied

The accepted regime satisfies the Gate C.5 criteria because it:

- Preserves generated-site centering.
- Keeps source signature and emitted tuple distinct.
- Explains why raw propagated field behavior failed.
- Provides a structural projection that can carry relation visibility.
- Recovers the three antipodal structural relations under the declared basis.
- Avoids false positives in the C.4L-D3 structural-channel witness.
- Preserves ambiguity as a reported status instead of hiding it.
- Keeps reduction law adoption separate from diagnostic witness status.
- Keeps FieldCueV0 blocked until it can consume the projection split honestly.

## 8. Limits of acceptance

This acceptance does not prove that raw field behavior contains sufficient antipodal structure.

This acceptance does not make the emitted scalar tuple a source signature.

This acceptance does not adopt R4-S1, C4L-S1, or C4L-S2 as a universal reduction law.

This acceptance does not authorize semantic naming from structural-channel relations.

This acceptance does not authorize GeneratedSiteReadingV0.

This acceptance does not unblock FieldCueV0.

This acceptance authorizes only the use of multi-projection source-state regime v0 as the source-state basis for a later, separate FieldCueV0 adaptation branch.

## 9. Gate C result

Gate C is accepted with strict limits.

The accepted result is:

`multi-projection source-state regime v0 is accepted as the source-state basis for later FieldCueV0 adaptation`

The rejected result is:

`raw field behavior or emitted scalar tuple behavior alone is sufficient as a source signature`

The reduction law status remains:

`not-adopted`

## 10. Gate D authorization

Gate D is authorized only as a later design and implementation sequence that preserves this Gate C.5 decision.

Gate D may design how FieldCueV0 should consume a multi-projection source-state regime.

Gate D may not treat this memo as approval to read ordinary field behavior as source truth, to promote R4-S1 as a field law, or to infer semantic names from structural-channel recovery.

## 11. FieldCueV0 remains blocked until adapted

FieldCueV0 remains blocked.

It may be adapted only in a future branch that explicitly consumes:

- structured source state as source signature
- propagation projection as ordinary field behavior
- structural projection as relation-carrying witness
- relation visibility statuses
- reduction honesty caveats
- generated-site centering

Until that adaptation exists and is separately reviewed, FieldCueV0 must not be unblocked.

GeneratedSiteReadingV0 also remains blocked.

## 12. Branch permissions after Gate C.5

After Gate C.5, a later branch may be opened for FieldCueV0 adaptation against the accepted multi-projection source-state regime v0.

That later branch may not silently change source policy, registry behavior, GeneratedSiteReadingV0, field atlas policy, package scripts, or reduction-law adoption status.

Any later implementation must preserve the diagnostic distinction between propagation-facing behavior and structural-channel relation visibility.

## 13. Compact binding statement

Gate C.5 accepts multi-projection source-state regime v0 as the source-state basis for later FieldCueV0 adaptation.

The source signature is structured source state.

The emitted scalar tuple is only a propagation-facing reduction.

Raw field behavior failed as a sufficient antipodal witness.

Structural-channel recovery is accepted only under the declared multi-projection basis.

Reduction law adoption remains not adopted.

FieldCueV0 remains blocked until adapted.

GeneratedSiteReadingV0 remains blocked.
