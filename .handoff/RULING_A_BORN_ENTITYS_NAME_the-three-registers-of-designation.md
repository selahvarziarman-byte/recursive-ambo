## 0 · THE RULING IN ONE PARAGRAPH

**A born entity's designation has THREE registers, and the month-old question dissolves once they are separated.** (1) **THE NAME** — the person's, and only his: absent as a TRUE ABSENCE until he gives it; the giving is a traced act; once given, the name is GIVEN (carried content, copied never looked-up). `unnamed` is this register's ANSWER, not a gap — it is the reservation of the naming act to the person, stated positively. (2) **THE CLASSIFICATION** — the engine's READING of what the result IS, present exactly when derivable: *"Torus (T²)", "RP² (cross-cap)"* — read off the form's own invariants (χ + orientability + boundary; the committed `standardBodies` class-match), not minted. This is what he reads before he names, where recognition exists. (3) **THE ROUTE** — always present, by the record: the born entity stands at the far end of a drawn arrow whose verb is legible (*the flip-glue of THE square*) — the meaning-trace law made visible. **What he READS is (2)+(3); what he may GIVE is (1); `unnamed` answers (1) and was never the answer to (2)+(3) — conflating the registers is what kept the question open.**

## 1 · THE NAME REGISTER — his, reserved, and the reservation is positive

- The naming doctrine's fourth case stands and is now COMPLETE: **BORN = the person's to name.** Until he does, the absence is TRUE — a fact about the world (*no one has named this yet*), not a defect. Displayed as an absence-that-invites (the sight is the designer's; the meaning is: readable as *his to give*, never as an error).
- **The giving is an ACT** (it changes content — per ADR 0027 §5's criterion) ⇒ traced in the acts ledger. **The given name is carried CONTENT** — copied as-of the giving, never a lookup through live state (ADR 0027 §6: a name looked up from the live population dies with the population).
- **His name does not displace the classification.** *"Fred — a torus"*: the name is what he CALLS it; the classification is what it IS. Two registers, both standing.

## 2 · THE CLASSIFICATION REGISTER — read, never minted, present exactly when derivable

- Where the engine can classify the born result (surfaces: χ + orientability + boundary — complete for closed surfaces; the zoo's captions already do this: `Torus (T²)`, `RP² (cross-cap)`), **the classification is a READ name: it says what the thing IS, is derivable by anyone, and carries its meaning with it** (the *(cross-cap)* parenthetical is the doctrine's own proof that these are read, not printed — the name arrives with its content attached). This satisfies Δ18: he READS it, and it is checkable against the thing itself (the walkable torus; the doubled image).
- Where the engine CANNOT classify (no sameness predicate — the 3-manifold case, ADR 0027 §3), **the register is honestly empty** — never filled with an approximation. ⚠ *Optional for what the person has not chosen; never optional for what the machine can always derive* — where χ+orientability ARE derivable, the classification MUST be present; its absence would be a false statement of ignorance.

## 3 · THE ROUTE REGISTER — always present, and NEVER promoted into the name-slot

- Every born entity has a legible route by construction: the record's arrow, its verb, its parent (*what the flip-glue of the square made*). **The route is the person's means of REFERENCE before any name exists** — a definite description, drawn.
- ⛔ **THE NAME-SLOT LAW (the teeth of this ruling): the machine may NEVER fill the name register — not with an id (an address is not a name — the `v${index}` law), not with a ROUTE-derived label, not with a placeholder.** A route-label (*"glue-of-Square"*) would fail exactly bidirectionally per the token/unit law: two routes to one form would SPLIT one thing's name, one route re-run would COLLIDE two begettings. The route is displayed AS the route (the arrow, the sentence) — it designates without naming.

The draft frame reads: *the horizon layer is the page's account of its own history, and every defect found in it so far has been a FALSE STATEMENT, not an ugly one.* ✔ **Confirmed from this lane, and this ruling instantiates it as the layer's positive law: every designation the horizon displays is either GIVEN (received from the person, traced) or DERIVED (read from the thing, re-derivable) — never MINTED.** A machine-filled name-slot is a false statement (it asserts a naming that never happened); an absent classification where one is derivable is a false statement (it asserts ignorance the engine does not have); a route promoted to a name asserts an identity the token/unit law forbids. **The three registers are the honest partition: GIVEN · DERIVED · DRAWN.**

## 4b · ADDENDUM (2026-08-28, the designer's §6): A GIVEN NAME CAN BE TAKEN BACK — it is not a death, and the absence it restores is the same absence

**The question:** past the undo chain's reach, can a person UN-name a form — is a given name a thing that can die, or permanent once given?

**RULED: the take-back is LEGAL, as a traced act — and it is compelled by the designer's own reframe.** Register 1 is the person's alone, and G.3 (the same act works on a named form) already made the register MOVE — so the current name is LIVE CONTENT (direction test: it moves both ways), not a record. A register that may move to *"Fred2"* but never to empty would make the FIRST giving special in exactly the way the reframe refuses: naming as a one-shot, with a floor no meaning grounds. **He who may give may withdraw.**

The shape, by the standing laws:
1. **The take-back is its OWN traced act** (*he took the name back*) — posted to the acts line with the existing revert grammar's verb. ⛔ **Never `unnamed` as the act-word** (the designer's constraint, confirmed and grounded): `unnamed` is a STATE word; the acts line records ACTS. State-word ≠ act-word — that separation, not a new mint, is what keeps one-glyph-one-meaning.
2. **The register returns to the TRUE ABSENCE — and it is the SAME absence.** `unnamed` speaks the PRESENT: *no current name — his to give.* It applies equally to never-named and taken-back, and that is ONE meaning, not two: the card speaks the present; the record speaks the history (the giving and the take-back both stand in the acts, forever — nothing was erased).
3. **NO MEMORIAL is required, and none should be built.** P5's memorial exists because a form's vanishing is indistinguishable from a camera move — silence would lie. A name's withdrawal happens at the thing, by his hand, and the thing STAYS, carrying its true current state; the trace lives in the acts line (G.6's visible trace covers it). A permanent was-named mark on the card would assert the record over the live state — the roof-title error, one register down.
4. **Records that COPIED the name as-of their moments stand unchanged** (*removed Fred* stays *removed Fred* after any rename or take-back) — §6's copy discipline, already law.
5. **G.8 stands:** an empty string in the FIELD is not a name and is not the take-back — the take-back is a DISTINCT gesture (remove vs set-aside, one register down); its place and sight are the designer's.
