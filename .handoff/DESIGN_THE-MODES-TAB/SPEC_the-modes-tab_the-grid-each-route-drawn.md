  The converse and stand-in lines leave the pairing column.

### 1.3 The corners (point 2)

### 1.4 The grid (points 2 and 4: where)
- **The layout.**
  - Rows are the first end's roles (F's, in its cast's order); columns are the second end's roles (Φ's, in its cast's order).
  - Heads print the role's name. A pressed head shows the role's gloss (the mold amendment of 11:50).
  - At 1689 × 897 the grid fits the tab's width with no sideways scroll; a cell wraps its word rather than cutting it.
- **A cell holds:**
  - **His relating(s) at that pair.** The word, exactly as typed, with **↑** when it reads from the column's role (`the form passes as the signal` sits in the signal's row under the form, ↑) or **↓** when it reads from the row's. A bar's word is struck. A pair in IS prints `≡` in the pair's amber.
  - **What reaches it through the corners, by state:**
    - **waiting:** `▲N` by T's edges in ink, and `●N` in T's light in the light's colour;
    - **decided:** `✓N`, in the acts' amber;
    - **refused or cut:** `✕N`, dim.
  - **With two corners speaking at the edge**, each mark carries its corner's name (`T●6 U●2`).
  - **An empty cell is empty:** no mark and no zero.
- **The legend, one line under the grid:**
  `a word with ↑ or ↓: a relating, read from the column's role or the row's · struck: a bar · waiting: ▲ by T's edges, ● in T's light · ✓ decided · ✕ refused or cut`
- **Choosing a cell outlines it in amber and opens its card under the grid.** Choosing it again closes the card.
- With no cell chosen: `choose a pair of roles: its routes open below, each drawn`.

### 1.6 The drawing of a route (design 2's box)
- **Three places:**
  - the row's role, low left;
  - the corner's role, high in the middle, or across a relation its two roles, high, joined by a line carrying the relation's word;
  - the column's role, low right.
- **Lines:** from the corner's role to each end, the word written just above its line, exactly as typed.
- **Colours:**
  - T's edges in ink, T's light in the light's own colour, one glyph and one meaning as in the light's drawing;
  - a refused or cut relation's line dashed, as a denial is dashed in the light.
- **Size:** one route high, about 64 px at 1689 × 897, within the tab's width.
