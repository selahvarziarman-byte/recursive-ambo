## 1 · WHAT A TRANSPORT MAY BE — the first lawful form: a PERMUTATION OF THE GIVEN VOCABULARY

**The fiber, here.** 0030 §1.1's fiber is the packet. At the Ambo grain the packet's LABEL is the concept's NAME in the person's vocabulary; its other fields (notes, color, tags, custom) are the concept's CONTENT and ride with the name. So the fiber that a transport acts on is **the GIVEN VOCABULARY `S` of that solid — the finite set of concept-names the person has given** (the four moments; or whatever he has named) — the same set at every carrier: a trivialized bundle over his words.

**The transport, here.** A transport on edge `uv` is a **BIJECTION `τ_uv : S → S`** — *"carried along this passage, sky arrives as earth"* — GIVEN by the person, with the connection axiom FORCED: the reverse passage carries the inverse (`τ_vu = τ_uv⁻¹`; store one direction, derive the other — a stored reverse that is not the inverse is a stamp that drifts). Content follows the name-map: the concept that arrives as `earth` arrives with `earth`'s content transported, i.e. the map re-addresses WHICH concept sits where; it writes nothing.

**Why a bijection and not any map:** a transport must be undoable by walking back. A non-injective assignment (*sky ↦ earth, earth ↦ earth*) loses `sky` on the way back — that is not a transport, it is a POOLING, which is the op-set's business (identification) and not this layer's. **Invertibility is the exact line between "carried into" and "identified with," and it is the boundary the whole layer stands on.**

**Why not "named relations":** a relation composes but need not invert; its "holonomy" would be a relation, not a group element, and would have no class — no residue in 0030's sense. That is a later, different form (a quiver/category on the vocabulary), **not a connection**; **trigger for widening: a person's honest passage-act that is neither a bijection of `S` nor a pooling** — if he needs *"sky becomes sky-and-earth,"* the form must widen and it says so then, not now.

**The S₄-on-the-four-moments** (the compass document's exhibit) is **an INSTANCE of this form, not a later form** — `S` = the four moments, transposition on each edge of K₄. It is the natural rich case and the right eventual target; **it is not the right FIRST BUILD** — the first build should be the smallest instance that already shows a residue: `|S| ≥ 2` with one non-identity transport on one edge of one triangle (a mirror — the half-turn, instance-zero's fiber, 0028 App. A). Sequencing is the mothership's; the definitional point is that nothing in the form changes between the two.

**Coherence with the composer (an invariant, with its falsifier):** a COMPOSED cell (a midpoint born of `uv`, reading `u·v`) is transported by transporting its components — `τ(u·v) = τ(u)·τ(v)` through the one composer (D14). **A transport that does not commute with the composer has MINTED a reading** — forbidden.

## 2 · ABSENCE vs IDENTITY — what the person must SEE (the name-slot law at the transport grain)

The edge has a **TRANSPORT SLOT**, and 0029's registers apply to it verbatim: **GIVEN** (his assignment — including the identity, when he gives it) · **DERIVED** (by a stated rule: 0028's lift across a mint; a declared default the record shows as declared) · **the TRUE ABSENCE** (nothing said). ⛔ **The engine may never fill an unassigned slot with the identity** — *"I said: nothing changes here"* is CONTENT (an act, traced, with the same given-mark every assignment carries; identity is merely its value), and *"I said nothing here"* is the absence of any act.

**What must be distinguishable at the eye — the meaning requirement, the form hers:**
1. **On the edge:** an assigned identity bears the GIVEN-mark like any assignment; an unassigned edge bears none. The distinguisher is the presence of an ACT, never the value.
2. **On a loop through an unassigned edge:** the residue is **NOT MEASURED** — *"passage `uv` carries no transport — nothing is fabricated"* (the refusal-order grammar; no reroute tail) — **never `residue = nothing moved`.** A silence read as identity is the layer's version of `H₁ = 0` printed alone: a positive fact carried by nothing.
3. **The trivial connection** (identity everywhere) is a lawful CHOICE — given edge by edge, or declared as a rule that the record shows AS a declaration. Never assumed.

## 3 · WHAT A TRIANGLE'S RESIDUE IS — the semantic cone at the Ambo grain, and its lawful sentence

**The residue.** Around a triangle `abc` the transports compose: `R = τ_ca ∘ τ_bc ∘ τ_ab ∈ Sym(S)`. **`R` is the CURVATURE of the connection on that face** — 0030 §1.4 exactly: holonomy around the boundary of a 2-cell. **It is the SAME KIND of object as the metric deficit** (0026's deck-fit): both are the curvature 2-cochain evaluated on a 2-cell — the metric one on the edge-links of a 3-form (dual 2-cells, valued in angles), this one on the faces of the dissected solid (primal 2-cells, valued in permutations). **Same kind, different cell, different group; the semantic cone lives on the faces, where the compass document said the residue lives.** Three precisions:
- **Its value is a CLASS, not a number:** `R` is COVARIANT — start the triangle at a different corner and `R` is conjugated. The gauge-invariant residue is the **cycle type** of `R` (*a swap · a 3-cycle · nothing moved*); the covariant `R` WITH its record (start corner, direction) is the MEANING (the gauge reading: covariant-with-record = the meaning; invariant = the classification).
- **Flatness / the concept deck-fit:** the connection is FLAT iff `R = id` on every face. **Bianchi (the build's witness):** on a closed tetrahedron the four face-residues, each conjugated to a common corner, multiply to the identity — three independent of four (the compass document's count, ratified as the K₄ arithmetic). *Falsifier: a build whose four residues do not compose to `id` has mis-composed or mis-oriented a face.*
- **Gauge:** renaming the vocabulary (`sky → heaven`) changes no cycle type; the covariant residue re-indexes with the record. *Falsifier: a residue reading that changes under a pure renaming reads gauge as content.*

**The lawful SENTENCE** — a reading the person can check by walking the triangle with the eye, nothing felt, countable in NAMES (LAW 23 and LAW 20 at this grain): **state the residue by its ACTION on his vocabulary and then its class** — *"around `a·b·c`: sky came back as earth, earth as sky, mortals as mortals — a swap."* / *"— nothing moved."* Its licence is per-loop: `R ≠ id` on the names actually carried. The form-level fact — *flat: yes/no* — may be cited as the reason such triangles exist, never substituted for the per-loop sentence (0030 §3's grain rule, unchanged).

**Does the Manuscript reading transfer?** It transfers as the HOME case exactly, and ONLY that case: on the dissected solid every triangle is a closed loop by construction — position always returns — so the Ambo residue is 0030 §2's *cancelled-and-home with changed cargo* = CURVATURE, and nothing else. **There is no displaced-cargo / route-disagreement reading at this grain**, because the solid is a ball: every loop of its 1-skeleton bounds in its 2-skeleton, so every holonomy is a product of face-residues — **at the Ambo grain, residue IS curvature; flat-nontrivial holonomy (the deck representation) arises only after gluing, at the Manuscript grain.** Two paths from `u` to `v` may disagree — but their disagreement is the curvature of the faces between them (Stokes), read as the same kind. **This is precisely why a+c is a chain: Ambo defines the curvature; the lift (0028) carries the transports; the glue creates the loops where flat holonomy first appears; the walk reads both.**

## 4 · WHAT THE ACT LEAVES — the trace PAIRS with the registers, exactly as naming does

Assigning a transport is a traced act (0030 §4.vii). **The ACT enters the ledger** — *assigned `τ_uv`: sky↦earth, earth↦sky (given), in order* — the record's order being content. **The VALUE stands in the edge's transport slot as GIVEN-register content** — live, re-givable and withdrawable by the take-back's own shape (a traced act on the existing revert grammar; the restored absence is the SAME absence). **The RESIDUE is never stored** — a derived READING of the face, re-derived on demand (record-not-reading); a person's sighting of it may be recorded as an act (*walked `a·b·c`, read a swap*), which is the walk's own trace. So the answer to *"pairs with or stands beside":* **it PAIRS — the same structure as christening: the act in the ledger, the value in a register, the readings derived.** And the meaning grain follows: a solid with an assigned connection is a DIFFERENT MEANING from the bare solid (0027 §2.2: the constitutive trace now includes the transports) — same class, different meaning, which is the whole point of the layer.

## 5 · INVARIANTS, collected (each with its falsifier — the acceptance's raw material)
1. **Bijection** — a non-bijective transport is a pooling wearing a transport's clothes.
2. **Connection axiom** — reverse = inverse, derived never stored.
3. **Composer coherence** — `τ(u·v) = τ(u)·τ(v)`; violation mints a reading.
4. **Absence ≠ identity** — a loop through an unassigned edge is *not measured*, never *nothing moved*.
5. **Flatness / Bianchi** — the four residues of a closed tetrahedron compose to `id`; three independent.
6. **Gauge** — renaming changes no cycle type; the covariant residue re-indexes with the record.
7. **The registers** — the layer transports GIVEN content and writes none; the dual has no slot; the composed cells transport by composition.
8. **The trace law** — every assignment traced and ordered; residues derived.
