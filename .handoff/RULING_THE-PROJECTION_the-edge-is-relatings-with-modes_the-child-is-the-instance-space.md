## 1 · The finding that forced the change

Across an edge, the built concept layer has one relation: identity. A role of A IS a role of B (J); a word of A IS a word of B (τ). Identity composes to identity, so around a face the passage through the opposite corner either returns what the edge already says or contradicts it. **Under identity a face is a tautology or an error; it cannot be informative.** The difference between an edge of a triangulation and a lone segment is, under identity, only the route by which an identification was reached. The built triads make even that vanish: the tetrahedron measured 0 of 4000 non-flat (the triadic-act instrument, its T2 seal firing as the finding), which means the loop returns exactly what the edge holds, every time. A midpoint under identity is never new: it is a selection of what the parents already held. This is the void Arman named ("either there is a difference between an edge of a triangulation and one lone segment, or we are turning in the void"), and it is measured, not felt.

**Arman's decision (16:05–16:38):** change the filling of an edge from identity to modes. Everything below follows from that decision and the fourteen answers he gave.

The criterion the ruling must pass: an edge in a triangle must differ from a lone segment in content, not in bookkeeping.

## 2 · The construction

**D0 · A cast, unchanged.** Roles R_X; a signature Σ_X of words with arities; a record T_X giving, for each word and tuple of roles, holds, does-not-hold or unknown. Unknown is absence, never a value.

**D1 · The lexicon.** A mode is a word the person declares at an edge (carries, resists, detaches, …). Modes accumulate in a lexicon L shared across the mesh. IS is a word of L reserved for transport (D12). Relatings are binary and directed: "x w y" is not "y w x".

**D2 · The medium.** Every edge e = XY of the mesh is a medium. Its extent Ext(e) is the set of all possible relatings across it: every (w, x, y) with w in L or a τ-shared word, x in R_X, y in R_Y, each three-valued and initially unknown. The extent is computable from the two casts alone, before any act; it is shown as extent and counts, never as roles, and an unknown entry is never rendered as a mark.

**D3 · A relating.** The person's act on e sets an entry of Ext(e): (w, x, y, +) is an **instance**; (w, x, y, −) is a **bar**. Detection is the person's act and only that.

**D4 · The child.** Ch(e), a cast:
- roles: the positive instances of e; each role's primary type is its mode w, with the marks of x and y secondary;
- signature: Σ_X ∪ Σ_Y pulled back through the two coordinate maps (instance ↦ its X-side, instance ↦ its Y-side); a τ-paired word is one word with two witnesses;
- record: for u in Σ_X, u(i, j) holds via X iff u(x_i, x_j) holds in T_X; via Y likewise; where a τ-shared word gives the same value on both sides it is one value with two witnesses; where the two sides disagree the entry is a **discordance** and both values are kept as content (the act is not refused);
- bars are not roles; they are form.
Ch(e) is a cast in the sense of D0, so the rule applies at every generation.

**D5 · Paths.** For a face XYZ and its edge XY: a path from x to y through Z is a pair of instances (w, x, z, +) on XZ and (w′, z, y, +) on ZY, written p = (x, w, z, w′, y). The device computes every path. Z's record induces relations among the paths through their z-coordinates: the form carries Z's configuration.

**D6 · Verdicts, rules, exceptions.** A path p and a direct instance (w″, x, y): the person may give a **verdict**, "p is this instance" or "p is not", recorded per instance and per word-pair (w, w′). The person may **name** a composite, (w, w′) ↦ w‴ in L: that is a **rule**; thereafter every path with that word-pair carries w‴, and p coincides with a direct instance iff w‴ = w″. An override of a rule on one path is an **exception**, recorded, and the rule is flagged as having exceptions. Two verdicts on one word-pair that disagree across faces are shown as a disagreement. A path with neither verdict nor rule is **UNRULED**. Same mode at the same endpoints is the same relating.

**D7 · The sorting (the projection).** For an edge e and each view Z (the opposite corner of each face containing e), every instance r of Ch(e) is:
- **OWN** if no path through any view composes to r;
- the **CENTROID'S** if some path composes to r (the face's, not the edge's; the centroid is no node, and this part is present at each of the three edges as form);
and every path is composed-to-a-direct, or **LIGHT** (composes to no direct: Z's proposal), or UNRULED. A path that composes to a barred entry is a **tension**, shown, never resolved by the device. Over several views the OWN part is the intersection of the per-view own parts (the exclusion is the union); the forms are kept one per view. The OWN part with its induced record is the **structural definition** the person names; the forms are what the name is given against. **The projection of Z onto e is the sorting of Ext(e)'s detected entries by the paths through Z.** Z contributes no role and no relating; change what stands in no relating from X or Y to Z and nothing moves. From the form alone Z's negative profile is readable: the roles of Z that the paths pass through, with Z's configuration among them.

**D8 · States.** UNDETECTED: no relating on e (the child is carried; it has not been looked at). VACUOUS: a view Z with no relating from X or Y to Z. EXHAUSTED: instances exist and OWN is empty under the union with no pocket. POCKET: each view's own part is non-empty and their intersection is empty (D9). UNRULED: paths without verdict or rule remain. COHERENT: no tension, no verdict disagreement, no pocket. CLOSED: every instance is the centroid's and there is no light. **Coherence is the naming clue; closedness is a finding, never a goal.** The flatness norm reads: the relation the geometry gives, the loop through the opposite corner, must be mirrored by the inhibitors, the bars and the verdicts, and mirrored means respected, not saturated.

**D9 · Two views.** One site, one representative. Exclusion by union. On a POCKET the person either authorizes a split into view-specific representatives or leaves the pocket open as a contested, unnamed site (v4's rule: a name splits only on a formal obstruction or by the person's authorization; the pocket is the obstruction). When a second view arrives at a named child, the device re-sorts and shows the name's status; conciliatory → the person patches the definition, contradictory → the pocket.

**D10 · Descent.** The mesh subdivides; children are casts (D4); every new edge, half-edges included, is a medium (D2). Links the device can derive on a new medium, two instances sharing a coordinate (the passage through the shared parent) or linked through the opposite midpoint, are LIGHT, never relatings. The person relates instances with L, the same act as at gen 0. The device has no reader: it computes shared roles, paths, tensions and disagreements and lays them side by side; facing them is the person's.

**D11 · What crosses; revision; extent.** The child's roles cross the lift as the child. The form is re-derived from the stored relatings and verdicts wherever the child appears and is shown as record at every new medium; nothing of the form is ever a role. A name is kept with the state it was given under (relatings, verdicts, views); when that state changes the device shows "named under a coarser resolution" and lists what left or entered the own part; it never renames and never proposes a name.

**D12 · Transport.** The lift, the door and the cargo walk ride IS-instances only. Naming uses the other modes.

- **B1 · The edge record.** Relatings (w, x, y, ±) with the lexicon; IS a word of it; every existing pairing (a, b) migrates as (IS, a, b, +); τ unchanged. Seal: the migration round-trips and the existing fixtures read as before.
- **B2 · The child as instance space** (D4), with the induced record and discordance. Positive control: IS-only relatings reproduce the core K on the flow ⊔ phi fixture exactly. Fixture: the history · language · virtuality example of the construction (three casts, six relatings) reproduces the worked sorting.
- **B3 · Paths, verdicts, rules, exceptions, the sorting and the states** (D5–D9), two views included. Positive control: under IS-only with the rule IS;IS = IS, the four link kinds of the stone and the face reading's classification reproduce on the existing tetrahedron fixtures.
- **B4 · Descent** (D10): children as casts; every new edge a medium; derivables as light. Control: an inner edge with only derivable links reads UNDETECTED with its light shown.
- **B5 · Display and revision** (D11, D8): extent and counts; the states; the forms as record; "named under a coarser resolution". The words are the designer's.
- **B6 · Transport** (D12): the lift, door and cargo restricted to IS-instances; the coder measures that nothing else was riding them.

B1–B3 are one union of meaning and should be chartered together; B4–B6 follow.

## 6 · What stays

Casts (D0). τ, the word-pairs. The geometry: the mesh, its subdivision, the media as the geometry's gift, the one-site-one-representative rule. The lift, the door and the cargo, on IS. The honesty line and the naming doctrine: the device never fills the name slot; its output at a site is a structural definition. RECORD, NOT READING: the relatings and verdicts are stored, everything else is re-derived. The two routed letters' questions (Virgin Land's 1037; the mothership's 1041, the projection's mechanism) are answered by D5–D8: the projection is the sorting by paths, and the third contributes no content.

## 7 · Falsifiers of this ruling

- F1 · If in practice a midpoint is named from a role that met nothing across the edge, D4 is wrong and the pushout stands (Q1's falsifier).
- F2 · If the names given to midpoints turn out to be names for the centroid's part and never for an own part, Q8 was wrong and the flatness norm was closedness.
- F3 · If verdicts per instance prove unmanageable at the second or third generation, Q7's promotion must become rule-first for the common word-pairs; a regime change, not a contradiction.
- F4 · If, under IS-only relatings, the instance space and the sorting fail to reproduce the core K and the stone's four link kinds, the construction does not contain the built layer as a special case and must not land.
- F5 · If an edge in a triangle and a lone segment still yield the same child under modes on a real record, the criterion of §1 is not met and the ruling has failed at its root.
