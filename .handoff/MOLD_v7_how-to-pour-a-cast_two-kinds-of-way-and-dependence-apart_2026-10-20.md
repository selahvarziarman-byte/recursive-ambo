## 0 · What the device does with your cast now — so the shape makes sense

A cast is a concept written as one file. The device takes it at a **corner of the seed solid** and nowhere else; a midpoint's space is never loaded, it is derived. From then on these things happen to it, and the file's shape exists so that each reads truly:

1. **The corner holds it, and the person names the corner.** The concept's name is typed on the corner's card by the person; loading never fills it. Inside the cast, the caster's words are the moments' labels and glosses, the words of the signature, the qualities and the subject matter. The device draws the cast and reads it back as counts, never as a grade.
2. **The person relates moments across an edge, and every relating is a WAY the first is turned into the second.** Between two corners he picks a moment on each side and says a way: in `IS` (`≡`, the one sameness: the two are one and nothing more is said) or in a word of his, the name of a channel (`the price is the case as the instituted`), with a direction and `it holds · it does not hold`. Whether a word names a transformation by a channel is his judgement at the act and never the device's. The page shows the norm's line once where a new word is typed, in the designer's words of 2026-10-20 13:02 under §9.64 (1): `a word here names a way the first is turned into the second, by its channel`.
3. **The midpoint holds the child.** The child's moments are his relatings, nothing of either parent that met nothing. Its own relations are FILLED LOOPS: between two of its moments the device lays out the loop made of your cast's way between their ends on one side, the other cast's way between their ends on the other side, and the two relatings; he says what the two ways round come to, and where they come to the same the loop is filled and is a relation of the child: a commuting square of ways. The parents' relations as read at the child's ends are shown beneath, as READ, never as the child's relations. **Only your ways are loop sides. Your dependences are not.**
4. **The third corner speaks in a light.** For each face, the corner opposite an edge states its share of the two ends' moments at the ALTITUDE: present, denied or silent, cell by cell, in its own channel-word (`the advance is spent as the outlay`, never a bare `runs as`). Those sayings are ways too, from a moment of the third to a moment of an end. Through them the device lays out passages (forks at one of the third's moments; bonds across one of the third's own ways) and he decides what each comes to; his decisions sort the edge's relatings into his own, the face's, lights, tensions and refused routes. **Your cast's ways are the bonds' middle legs; your dependences never are.**
5. **Names.** He names the midpoint, its moments and its filled relations. A name designates; filling makes a relation; a name over several kinds of filled loop makes them one relation-type of the child as a parent.
6. **The walk.** Transport (the lift, the door, the cargo) rides every way, carrying its word: a walk is a tree of routes, one road per way holding the role at each step; a route's word is the composite of the words walked, by his rules, else unruled; the identification structure's readings (Fix · Mov · Und) are read on the `IS` routes alone, and routes with a named leg are listed as lights. The order in which a route's words are composed is OPEN (§9.64 (5)); the device today folds them in the walk's order.
7. **The next generation.** The child, with its moments and its filled relations, is a cast of this mold and a corner of the next generation; nothing is poured for it. Its dependences are its two coordinates, given by the dissection.

**What changed since v6, in one list.** **A way is a TRANSFORMATION, never a sameness**: a cast's word names a way one moment is turned into the next, by a channel; only `IS` says *the same*, and a cast never writes it between its own moments. **Two kinds of way, inside and between**: your words move the concept's own moments; relatings and the third's sayings run between concepts; they compose, and a composite across them is always a word between. **Dependence has its own place**: a word that says the first cannot be without the second (a condition, a bearer, a cause, a purpose, a title) is a DEPENDENCE, poured in its own list, kept apart from the ways, read by no loop and no walk. **A bar means by no route**: where you mean *only through*, write the steps and no bar. **One spelling names one way**: spell different ways differently, within a cast and across your casts. Everything else of v6 stands: a role is a moment, glossed; the form is binary; a symmetric way is listed once; a word names its channel; the child's relations are filled loops; a born cast is a cast of this mold.

## 1 · What a cast IS

> **A concept-space is a finite set of MOMENTS, the NAMED WAYS one moment is turned into another, and the DEPENDENCES among them**: a finite set of **roles**, each a moment with its determinacy; a **signature** of way-words, each the name of a channel, of two places; the **relations** known to hold or known not to hold, each a way from one moment to another in a word, listed with its two terms in order; a **dependence signature** of dependence-words, each of two places; the **dependences** known to hold or known not to hold, listed with their two terms in order; **qualities**, relations of one place on a moment, carried as marks; **axioms**, sentences carried and never evaluated; and **marks**, recorded and never read. A concept is this structure together with its **subject matter** and its moments' **determinacies**, carried and read by the person. (ADR 0031 §1.1 with its §9.31 clause; §9.64 (1)–(3).)

- **A role is a MOMENT** of the concept: a determinate way the concept shows up, with its own determinacy in the caster's words, the gloss with its examples, and its ways to the other moments. The device reads its position (id, qualities, relations, dependences); the person reads its determinacy. **Two moments are two roles, whatever their recorded relations**; a way between them turns the one into the other and keeps both; only `IS`, the one sameness, would make two moments one, and a cast never writes `IS` between its own moments. (v5 §8 A1, ratified at §9.31; §9.64 (1).)
- **A cast's way-word is the name of a way one moment is turned into the next, by its CHANNEL**: `the promise is honoured as the payment` says that the promise, by being honoured, is the payment. A word that says only that the one becomes the other (`becomes`, `passes into`, `runs as`) names a genus of ways and no way in it, and says nothing (§9.60). A way is directed: read backwards it is not a way unless the person declares a converse (§9.53 (a); §9.62). This is the concept's own movement, a way INSIDE the concept; a relating at an edge is a way BETWEEN concepts; the two kinds compose, and what they compose to is always a word between (§9.64 (2)).
- **A cast's dependence-word is the name of a way one moment cannot be without another**: `the working requires the capacity`, `the price presupposes the wanting`, `the advance rests on the hoard`, `the product is made of the material`, `the thing is for the need`, `the share belongs to the claimant`. A condition and what it conditions; a bearer and the borne; a cause and its effect; a purpose and what serves it; a title and its claimant. **A dependence is not a way and is kept apart from the ways**: no loop takes it as a side, no rule composes it, no translation makes it one with a way, no walk rides it, no saying of the third is in it. The device reads it as structure (closure, arity, consistency), counts it apart and shows it apart; its content is the caster's. (§9.64 (3), Arman's *"b"*, Δ217.)
- **Across an edge, dependence is the construction's.** The child's moments depend on the parents' by the coordinate leg (D14), given by the dissection and never typed. So no relating is written in a dependence-word; a dependence between two concepts is the geometry's to show, not the person's to type. (§9.64 (3).)
- **The form is binary.** A way has two ends, and so has a dependence. A word of three or more places is neither; it stands outside the mold, carried as the cast lists it, read by no loop and no walk, counted apart. Re-pouring it as a moment with ways or dependences to its terms is the caster's. (v5 §8.8; §9.43 (3).)
- **Relations are given; the movement is a way, never `IS`; everything else is derived.** Never hand in a ranking of moments beside the relations, since the device would read it as structure; the concept's own movement, one moment turned into the next, is written as ways, tuple by tuple, with direction and polarity. (v5 §8 A5 with §8.8.)
- **What the device reads, and what it carries.** It reads three lists, `roles`, `signature`, `relations`, and one line, the subject matter. **The two dependence lists are carried unread today**, as `axioms` are, until the coder builds the kind; nothing on the page shows them yet. It carries everything else unread: glosses, sources, reasons, meanings, properties, premises, open forks, what did not fit, any field of the caster's own. Carried is not lost: the card says `a warrant (kept, not read)` and the drawing prints each axiom as `axiom (kept, not checked): …`. The device checks what is true of a STRUCTURE, never of a PRACTICE: it never judges whether a word names a channel, or whether a dependence is one.
- **Three structural checks, and only these.** CLOSURE: every term of every relation and dependence names a role of this cast, and every relation's word is in the signature (every dependence's word in the dependence signature, once built). ARITY: a relation has as many terms as its word declares. CONSISTENCY: the record is a function: no tuple listed both as holding and as not holding, no role id declared twice with different content, no word declared with two arities, no name that is both a word and a quality, **no spelling that is both a way-word and a dependence-word**. Closure and arity breaks are taken and marked; only consistency refuses.

## 2 · The parts of the file, one by one

### 2.1 `roles` — the moments
- Each role is an object with an **`id`** (required, unique; the device's handle, never shown as a name), a **`label`** (the caster's word for the moment; shown wherever the moment appears; a role with no label shows its id, marked as an address), a **`gloss`** (required: one line, the moment's determinacy with its examples, in the caster's words; carried, never read for a check; shown beside the label wherever the moment is picked; a role without a gloss loads and is marked on the page as an address), and **`types`**, the moment's qualities.
- **Qualities are relations of one place** and are neither ways nor dependences: a quality says something of one moment. The device reads them as structure at two places: at a pairing, two moments paired by `IS` whose declared qualities do not join are refused by name; at a face, a holonomy that merges two moments of one corner whose values do not join refuses the face (ADR §3.2). `UNKNOWN` never clashes, and beyond agreement the device never reads a quality's content. Declare a quality on every moment, or write `UNKNOWN`; an omission and a written `UNKNOWN` both mean unknown, never a value.
- **`member_status`** stays the one quality the mold itself defines (`has` · `unrecorded` · `none-by-nature`), optional, compared by the device only when both casts declare it.
- **The bearer, if you want it, is a moment.** A caster who wants the concept's whole, value in a cast of finance or the labour process in a cast of production, lists it as a moment with its gloss and writes dependences from the other moments to it (`the advance is a phase of value`: a dependence) and ways among the phases. This is the caster's option, never a rule (§9.64 (3)).
- Anything else on a role (a source, a note, an amount, a window) is a mark, carried, never read. **Amounts are marks.**

### 2.2 `signature` — the cast's way-words, each the name of a channel
- Each entry has a **`type`** (the word) and an **`arity`**, which is **2**. A `meaning` is strongly asked for, in this form: one sentence of what the word says, and then **the way the first is turned into the second** (`the promise, honoured, is the payment. The way: honouring, the first becomes the second by being paid as promised`). It is carried, never read; it is what a translator and the person use. **Every way-word names its channel**: *is honoured as*, *is spent as*, *is kept as*, never a genus-word that only says the one becomes the other.
- **One spelling names one way.** Within a cast and across the casts of one hand, two ways get two spellings, and one spelling is one way: a translation will make like spellings one and fill loops by law (§9.46 (4); §9.64 (8)). Where the same channel truly recurs across your casts (*is kept as*: keeping, the one becomes the other by being held), the same spelling; where the channel differs (owing a loan's return and owing a gift's return), two spellings, and say in the `meaning` what the channel is.
- **The words are the cast's own, and foreign across casts until translated by hand.** The device never matches two casts' words by spelling: `no words translated yet (words spelled alike are still two words)`. Nothing changes on the page under one-spelling-one-way; it is a rule of your hand, so that his translation, when he makes it, is true.
- **A converse and the opaque bit are the lexicon's, not the file's.** A way is read back without a converse word; a converse (`w the other way round: [a word]`) is an equation of the lexicon the person may give, and the opaque bit is declared there too, for intensional words only. For a cast's word they are declared where the word first meets an edge. `properties` on an entry (`symmetric`) is for the reader; the device reads none of it. (§9.53; §9.58 (2).)
- A word with no listed tuple is padding; declare a word only when it has an instance.
- A word of **three or more places** may still be written (the loader takes any arity); it is outside the mold, drawn as a tuple-node, read by no loop and no walk, and should be rare and said so in `open_forks`.

### 2.3 `relations` — the ways that hold and the ways refused
- Each has a **`type`** (a word of the signature), **`terms`** (two role ids, **in order**: the way runs from the first to the second, as the word reads), and a **`polarity`**: `holds` or `does-not-hold`. A `reason`, sourced, is asked for and carried.
- **Unlisted is unrecorded**, unknown, never false. A source's denial is written `does-not-hold`: a refused way, drawn as a bar; silence is nothing. A refused way is content: it individuates, and it is shown. A missing negative is exposure.
- **A bar means BY NO ROUTE.** `does-not-hold` on `(x, y)` in `w` says `x` is not turned into `y` by `w`'s channel by any route; a route that comes to `w` at that pair is a tension in the record, a contradiction, never a mediate holding. **Where you mean *only through z*, write the two steps, `x w₁ z` and `z w₂ y`, and no bar.** "The hoard does not come back as the return, only money parted with does" is written as `the hoard is committed as the advance` and `the advance comes back as the return`, with no bar on `(hoard, return)`; a bar there would say the hoard never comes back as the return by any route, which is not what the source says. (§9.64 (4), Arman's *"a"*, Δ217.)
- **A symmetric way is listed ONCE**, in the order the caster chooses; its symmetry is the converse equation of the lexicon, not a second tuple. Listing both orders records one way twice, and the loops count it twice. (§9.58 (1).)
- **A way with the same moment at both ends** is lawful and drawn as a loop at the moment.
- `r(x, y)` is not `r(y, x)`: term order is content. A tuple listed twice with identical content is read once.
- **A term that is not a moment** (a parameter, a condition, a thing outside the concept) is one of three things: an unlisted moment (admit it, with its ways and dependences), a quality (fold it in), or a mark (annotate). Never leave it dangling: the device takes the tuple and marks it, `relation 3: "F9" isn't a role in this cast`.
- **Dynamics are not structure**: path-dependence, a sign that flips, a relation that modifies its own terms are marks on the relation.

### 2.4 `dependence_signature` and `dependences` — the dependences, apart
- **New in v7; carried unread by the device today**, as `axioms` are, until the coder builds the kind on the mothership's order; then read as structure, counted apart and shown apart. Pour them now so that the redo is whole and nothing waits.
- `dependence_signature`: each entry has a **`type`** (the dependence-word), an **`arity`** of **2**, and a `meaning`: one sentence of what the word says and in what sense the first cannot be without the second (`requires: the first cannot take place unless the second is there. A condition and what it conditions`).
- `dependences`: each has a **`type`** (a word of the dependence signature), **`terms`** (two role ids, in order: the first depends on the second, as the word reads), a **`polarity`** (`holds` or `does-not-hold`, a denial of dependence being content as a refused way is) and a `reason`, sourced.
- **A spelling is a way-word or a dependence-word, never both.** `requires` and `is required as` on one pair is the same relation poured twice under two kinds, and it is refused by the consistency check once built; today it is a break of the mold (§5).
- **No loop, no rule, no translation, no walk, no saying reads a dependence.** It is the concept's articulation, not its movement. If a dependence seems to need a way beside it (the capacity is exercised as the working, and the working requires the capacity), pour both: they are two facts of two kinds about one pair of moments.

### 2.5 `axioms` — sentences, kept
- Strings, or objects with a `sentence`. Carried whole, counted on the card, printed on the drawing, never checked. An axiom is a sentence that is not a single instance. **A dependence is no longer written here**: it has its own list.

### 2.6 `subject_matter` — what the concept is OF
- One line, in the caster's words, read on the card beside the person's name for the corner. Two casts with one structure and two subject matters are two concepts with one shape.

### 2.7 Everything else — the caster's honesty, carried
- `premises`, `open_forks`, `did_not_fit`, `derived_check`, `cast_by`, a `source`: carried, read by nobody but the people who open the file. **`did_not_fit` no longer holds dependence-words**, which are poured in §2.4; it holds what is neither a way nor a dependence (a word of three places you could not re-pour; a relation whose terms are not moments; a reading you set aside), with the reason.

### 2.8 What the device answers when you load it
- **Taken:** the line reads `loaded: 8 roles · 11 relations in 6 words · 3 qualities, 11 values given · read as directed · …`: the tuples and the signature's words counted together, the moments' qualities apart with the values given, a zero part not printed; then the axioms and the warrant clauses as before, when there is something to carry; the dependence lists are among the carried fields until built. The rest is as v6 §2.7: refused two ways only (not a cast; the record contradicts itself, every address named); taken with marks for a dangling term, a word not in the signature, a wrong number of terms, an item it could not take; `this cast has no roles`. No tick, no `valid`, no green, ever.
- **The card's `read as directed · read as symmetric` rows** report how the tuples were listed; a symmetric way listed once reads `directed` there; that row is a reading of the file, not of the way. (§9.58 (3).)

### 2.9 The drawing
A column of points in the caster's own order, each printing its name alone; resting on a point gives the moment's meaning first, then its qualities; an arc from the first term to the second with its word on it; a loop for a way with one moment at both ends; a tuple-node for a word of three or more places; a bar for a refused way; the unrecorded as nothing at all. Dependences are not drawn until built; when built, they are drawn apart from the ways, in the designer's form.

## 3 · How to pour — the practice, in order

1. **Write the subject matter line first.** If you cannot write it, you have a word, not a concept.
2. **List the MOMENTS honestly**: the ways the concept shows up in the sources, each with its determinacy in one line with examples. Keep every moment the concept has; keep two moments two even where their ways are alike; never a count chosen to look right. Four to seven is a working size; the logical square's four corners are one honest shape of moments, its oppositions bars. If the concept has a whole its moments are phases of, list the whole as a moment too (§2.1).
3. **Give each moment a short id, a label and a gloss.** The id is a handle; the label is the word that will stand on the page and in every relating; the gloss is its determinacy.
4. **Sort every relation you find between two moments into one of two kinds, and pour each in its own list.** Ask: *does the first turn into the second by a channel I can name?* Then it is a WAY: pour it in the signature, one spelling per channel, never a genus-word (*becomes*, *passes into*, *runs as*), and write its `meaning` ending with the way the first is turned into the second. *Does the first fail to be without the second?* Then it is a DEPENDENCE: pour it in the dependence signature (*requires*, *presupposes*, *rests on*, *is made of*, *is for*, *belongs to*, *is borne by*), and write its `meaning` ending with what the first cannot be without. A relation can be both on one pair, under two spellings of two kinds, and then you pour both. A relation that is neither (a measure, a ranking, a dynamic) is a mark or an axiom. **Never re-voice a dependence into a way to make it fit**: *the spending requires a share* is a dependence; *the share is laid out as the spending* is a way, and it is a different fact; pour the one the source states, or both if it states both. **The count of a cast's way-words is the count of its channels, no fewer and no more**, for the reason the device gives: his rules bind a shape of words, a translation makes two casts' words one and fills loops by law, and a route's word is the composite of the words walked. **One spelling names one way** across your casts: spell different channels differently and the same channel the same.
5. **List the tuples of both kinds.** Two terms in order; a symmetric way once; a refused way or a denied dependence wherever a source denies it; nothing where a source is silent; a one-line reason with its page. **A bar means by no route: where the source says *only through*, write the steps and no bar.** Never pad; a refusal from a source is as good as a holding.
6. **Declare the qualities** on every moment or write `UNKNOWN`; `member_status` only if it is a fact of the concept; amounts and windows in marks.
7. **Write the axioms**, the premises, the open forks, and what did not fit.
8. **Check before loading**: every term a role id; every way-word in the signature with two terms and every dependence-word in the dependence signature with two terms; no tuple with both polarities; ids unique; no name both a word and a quality; no spelling both a way-word and a dependence-word; every moment glossed; no symmetric way listed twice; no bar standing beside the two steps it would deny.
9. **Save as `<name>.cast.json` and load it at the seed corner it belongs to**, read the `loaded:` line, open the drawing, type the person's name for the corner on the card, export the workspace afterwards.
10. **Cast each concept on its own.** The only across-concept thought allowed while casting is that your spellings should be translatable and one spelling should be one way. Shaping a concept to make its relatings with a neighbour come out well is the one thing the mold most forbids.

## 4 · What happens to your cast after it is taken — so you pour for it

- **At the edge, every relating is a way between concepts.** The person picks a moment on each side and says a way the first is turned into the second: `≡` (`IS`, the one sameness), or a word of his with a direction. A relating written in `IS` by word is refused: `in IS this is a pair; pair it instead`. A way reads back without a converse; a converse and the opaque bit are declared in the lexicon, once per word; a relating in a word he declared opaque is refused by name. **No relating is written in a dependence-word**; across an edge dependence is the geometry's (D14), and once the kind is built the word field refuses it by name. (§9.50–§9.53; §9.64 (3).)
- **The lexicon is one kind of word everywhere, in two kinds of way.** Your cast's way-words, his modes and a light's sayings are all ways; yours run inside the concept, his between; a composite across them is a word between, never one of yours. Your signature's words do not appear on the modes line by themselves, since words are foreign across casts until he translates them; a translation (τ) is his act and makes two words one, and it is why one spelling must be one way.
- **At the midpoint, the child.** Its moments are his relatings, each labelled by its sentence or by his name for it; nothing of a parent that met nothing. Its relations are the **filled loops**: for two of its moments the loop is your cast's way between their ends on one side (or their sameness), the relating, the other cast's way on the other side, and the other relating. The loop is closed when both casts say a way there, open when either is silent; a refused way on a side is form; **a dependence on a side is no side: the loop is open there**. The device lays out the loop's two ways round to each cross cell; he says what each comes to; the same fills the loop, different leaves a hole. A filled loop is a relation of the child, keyed by its kind (your word with its cast, direction and sign, and the other cast's), named by him or read as its loop. **So your cast's ways are what the child's relations are made of, and your refusals are what makes a route a refused one.** (§9.40–§9.49.)
- **The third corner speaks at the altitude.** The corner opposite an edge states, cell by cell, which of its moments are present in, or denied of, each moment of the two ends, in its own channel-word. Forks and bonds are passages he decides; what a passage comes to is a way-word at the pair; a decision with no direct relating standing is the third's light. **Your cast's ways are the bonds' middle legs; a dependence is never a leg.** (§9.29–§9.30; §9.35; §9.64 (2).)
- **Names.** He names the midpoint, each of its moments, and its filled relations. Filling makes the relation; naming designates it; only joining kinds under one word, parting them, or giving a direction with a name changes what the next generation reads. (§9.38 (c); §9.48.)
- **The walk.** The lift, the door and the cargo ride every way, carrying its word. A role held by several ways on one edge is a family; a walk is a tree of routes, finite by construction; a route with a named leg that returns to another moment of a corner derives a light of that corner, never a merge; Fix · Mov · Und are read on the `IS` routes alone. A route's word is the composite of the words walked; **the order of that composition is OPEN** (§9.64 (5)): today the device folds the words in the walk's order, by his chain rules, and reads unruled at the first missing rule; do not pour for it. No reader walks inside a cast, and none rides a dependence. (§9.50 (3); §9.54; §9.57.)
- **What your choices do downstream.** A thin cast is first-class. A missing refusal leaves exposure. A padded moment, an unfounded tuple, a ranking handed in, a placeholder: none of these is caught, and each produces a false reading with nothing to flag it. **A dependence poured as a way is not caught either**: the child's loops will ask what it comes to as a transformation, and the person will find the question unanswerable at that loop. **A bar that meant *only through* is not caught**: it stands against the composite the steps would make, and his rule will print a tension there. Both fall on the caster.

## 5 · What breaks the mold

A count chosen to look right · a relation without a reason · a ranking handed in as input · a placeholder where you were unsure · a moment's label used as a word · a premise that shaped the list and went unstated · a term that is not a moment · a quality declared on some moments and silently absent on others · **a symmetric way listed twice** · two measures under one word · a dynamic encoded as static structure · a concept shaped to fit its neighbours · a reason that cites the lens instead of the subject · a word declared with no instance · a label written as a placeholder · a moment without a gloss · **a dependence poured as a way** (*the spending requires a share* written as *is required as*) · **a way poured as a dependence** · **one spelling for two ways, or two spellings for one** · **a bar that means *only through*** · **a genus-word (*becomes*, *passes into*, *runs as*) standing for its channels** · **a relating typed in a dependence-word**.

**What the device refuses by name:** a file that is not a cast; a record that contradicts itself. **What it takes and marks:** a dangling term, a word not in the signature, a wrong number of terms, an item it could not take, a moment without a gloss. **What it never does:** fill a name, grade a cast, read an absence as a fact, offer a pairing, match two casts' words by spelling, judge whether a word names a channel or a dependence, or fail a cast for what its neighbours turned out to be.

## 6 · The template, v7

**The minimal cast — everything the device reads today, and the dependences it will read:**
