Dependence, wired under the ways

One drawing, two planes. On top, as today: the moments and their ways, arcs inside a concept and relatings across between two. Underneath, the dependences as wiring: drawn behind everything, in square runs, faint and without words. A wire leaves a moment, runs in the channel beside the column, and comes up under what that moment cannot be without. Rest on a moment and its own wires come up, the ways sink back, and a card beside it reads them in the cast's words.

Production and Distribution · each concept on its own

The plane of ways is exactly today's. The wiring runs in a channel on the outer side and passes under the names to reach its moments. A wire never bows like an arc, and an arc never runs square, so a way and a dependence cannot be taken for each other. Rest on the working: it is wired down to the capacity, the material, the instrument, the product and the purpose, and the product and the capacity are wired back to it, the circle of making the makers again.

Production–Distribution · the edge's drawing

On top, the relatings cross between the two concepts as today. Underneath, each concept's wiring stays in its own channel on its outer side, and no wire crosses between them, because a dependence crosses nothing. The child's two legs are wired too: they are its only dependences of its own. Rest on a relating, which is a moment of the child: its two ends light, and so does the wiring under them, what they rest on in each parent. That is all a child reads of its parents' dependences, one level down, never a wire from one relating to another.
