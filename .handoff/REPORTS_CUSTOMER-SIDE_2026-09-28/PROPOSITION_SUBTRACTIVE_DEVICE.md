# The connection layer as a subtractive calculus run to a fixed point

A theoretical proposition for the device and for the seam between the device and the person, written against the transcript on co-intuitionistic logic and against what the device did on 2026-09-28 (branch `team-arman`, `925a177`; the records in `ta/saves/`, the run in `REPORT_GEN2_NAMES_AND_THE_LIFT.md`). Nothing below is asserted of the device that was not measured today; nothing is asserted of the literature that was not checked against a source listed at the end. Where a correspondence is a reading rather than a theorem, it is called a reading.

## 0 · What the speaker claims, and what stands behind it

The transcript makes six claims. Each has a literature the speaker is leaning on.

1. **Co-intuitionistic logic has no implication, only a co-implication (subtraction).** Its algebras are co-Heyting (Brouwer) algebras, the order-duals of Heyting algebras; the subtraction b − a is the least c with b ≤ a ∨ c, and the co-negation is ~a = ⊤ − a, taken from the top, not from the bottom. The logic goes back to Rauszer's semi-Boolean algebras and Heyting–Brouwer logic (1970s), Goodman's "logic of contradiction" (1981), Urbas (1996); Goré (2000) unifies the algebraic, relational and sequent presentations.
2. **No discursive interpretation, but computational ones.** Crolard's "Subtractive logic" (2001) identifies the calculus of bi-cartesian closed categories with co-exponents, and his formulae-as-types paper (2004) types **first-class coroutines** in a restriction of the λμ-calculus. Bellin's term assignment for dual intuitionistic logic is a calculus of continuations on a single-assumption, multiple-conclusion natural deduction, with "remote capture of variables and remote substitution", the constructs `postpone` and `mkc` (make continuation), and coroutines as the meaning of subtraction introduction; Bellin and Menti (2014) carry this to the π-calculus for the linear case. That is the "Bellin" the speaker names.
3. **The obstacle is that co-implication is dynamic, not static.** In Rauszer's relational semantics the clause is backward-looking and existential: w ⊩ A −< B iff there is v ≤ w with v ⊩ A and v ⊮ B. Implication quantifies over all later worlds; subtraction asks whether at some earlier stage A already stood without B. The co-negation ~A says "A has failed somewhere earlier".
4. **Signals, channels, machines.** The speaker's own construction (propositions as signals, co-implication as first-class channels that pass or subtract a signal, sequents compiled into machines that run to a fixed point) is, as far as I can find, unpublished; its parts are standard: the dual reading of BHK as refutation (Shramko's falsification logic, 2005, with constructive falsity and non-constructive truth, explicitly Popperian), the single-premise multiple-conclusion shape of dual proofs (Tranchini 2012, Wansing 2016), the continuation reading (Bellin, Crolard), and fixed points as the meaning of running a coalgebra (Rutten 2000, Jacobs 2016).
5. **The duality intuitionistic/co-intuitionistic is the duality algebra/coalgebra, language side/machine side.** This is a reading, and a good one: initial algebras evaluate terms, final coalgebras classify behaviours by bisimulation; induction is the least fixed point, coinduction the greatest.
6. **The same problem as dualizing toposes.** Mortensen's closed-set logic and Estrada-González's complement-topoi read a topos's subobject lattice upside down: the internal logic becomes paraconsistent instead of paracomplete, and contradiction is confined to the boundary. Lawvere's "Intrinsic co-Heyting boundaries and the Leibniz rule in certain toposes" (1991) defines the boundary ∂a = a ∧ ~a in any co-Heyting algebra, proves the Leibniz rule ∂(a ∧ b) = (∂a ∧ b) ∨ (a ∧ ∂b), and notes that every element is its regular core plus its boundary, a = ~~a ∨ ∂a. Reyes and Zolfaghari (1996) show subobject lattices in presheaf toposes such as graphs are bi-Heyting and build modalities from the two negations.

## 1 · What the device already is, in these terms

### 1.1 The geometry: lift regions form a bi-Heyting algebra and the rim is Lawvere's boundary

A lift region is a sub-complex of the mesh: a set of cells closed under taking faces, which the store enforces ("Lift region: 1 face · connected — closes to 4V · 4E · 1F"). Sub-complexes are the down-sets of the face poset, and down-sets of a poset form a complete bi-Heyting algebra (they are the subobjects of a presheaf on that poset; Reyes–Zolfaghari). The co-Heyting negation of a region R is the sub-complex generated by every cell not in R; the boundary ∂R = R ∧ ~R is the set of faces of R that are also faces of cells outside R. For a square of the cuboctahedron lifted alone, ∂R is its four edges and four vertices. The device prints exactly this object and calls it the rim: "deficit · rim turn · 90° ×4", "○ open · local-cone — a rim stays open", "explore inside — this one has a boundary (4 free edge classes)". Identify and glue are boundary-killing maps: the band still has a rim (χ 0, H₁ ℤ, "a rim stays open"), the torus has none ("Σδ = 0 ⇄ tiles · the sheet tiles flat", genus 1, closed). So the Manuscript's verdict column is a boundary computation in a co-Heyting algebra of regions, and closedness (∂ = 0) is exactly the case the doctrine calls a finding, never a goal.

This is a claim with a falsifier: Lawvere's Leibniz rule must hold for rims of regions on the same solid. For two squares sharing a vertex v, both sides equal {v}; for a square and a triangle sharing an edge, both sides equal that edge with its ends. A witness that computes ∂ on lift regions and checks the rule on every pair of faces of the cuboctahedron would pin the statement. The device does not compute ~R or ∂R as such today; it computes the rim through the deficit register. The proposition is that they are the same object and should be exposed as one.

### 1.2 The concept layer: the four readings are the four combinations of two sets, and the subtraction is in time

At a medium e with detected instances E, and for each view Z the set P_Z of instances some passage through Z composes to under the current says, with P the union over views, the sorting reads (from `sorting.ts`):

- OWN = E − P (theirs alone: no passage comes to it),
- CENTROID'S = E ∧ P (the face's: said between them and through Z too),
- LIGHT_Z = P_Z − E (a composite with no direct: the view's proposal, record never offer),
- TENSION = P ∧ Bars (a path pressing on a bar).

On the Boolean lattice of instance-sets these are set differences and there is nothing co-Heyting about the statics. The co-Heyting structure is in the dynamics. Both E and P grow as the person's record grows (relatings enlarge E and open passages; says and rules enlarge P). OWN is the difference of two monotone maps and is therefore not monotone: today, of my 20 own relatings, 13 became the face's only after the neighbouring sites received their own relatings and the passages through them opened. Read the person–device loop as a Kripke frame whose worlds are the stages of the record ordered by extension. "x is the face's" is persistent, an intuitionistic proposition. "x is own" is not persistent. But the bi-Heyting subtraction of the two upsets, [x ∈ E] −< [x ∈ P], is by Rauszer's clause "there is an earlier stage at which x stood in the extent unreached": **x has been own**. That is what the device already records when a name is saved: "named when it was: Law of reciprocity — given when 9 relatings were said; since then, 0 left what is theirs alone · 0 entered". The stage of naming is the witness v of the co-implication; the name is its realizer; "named under a coarser resolution" is the gap between the persistent memory and the present. This is a discursive interpretation of co-implication of exactly the kind the speaker says nobody has given: a co-implication is the trace of a stage at which one thing stood without another, and the device keeps it as a name with its date.

### 1.3 The inhibitors: the device is a falsification logic

The device constructs no positive relating. It has no reader: it lays out passages, lights, tensions and disagreements and leaves the facing to the person. What it takes from the person as final are refutations: the bar ("does not hold", kept as form, never a role), the verdict "that is not it", and the exception. The flatness norm says the loop the geometry gives "must be mirrored by the inhibitors — the bars and the verdicts — and mirrored means respected, not saturated." That is Shramko's logic of scientific research: constructive falsity, non-constructive truth, a record judged by what it forbids. In Bellin and Biasi's pragmatic reading the person's relatings are hypotheses (justified by a scintilla of evidence, here a passage of Kant), not assertions; the device treats them so, since a relating can be sorted away to the face's but never validated. And the layer is paraconsistent in Mortensen's precise sense: discordance is "kept as content, never a refusal, never a join"; a POCKET keeps two views' own parts without a common part; a tension is "shown, never resolved"; the flag "your says differ across the faces" marks a boundary point where two closed regions of consequence overlap. Contradiction is confined to the boundary, which is the centroid's part, "present at each of the three edges as form" and belonging to no cell.

### 1.4 Channels into channels: descent is the speaker's first-class channel

D4 and D10 make the roles of a generation-n+1 medium the instances of generation n. Today's measurement: at Alteration–Material necessity the medium's roles were "3 × 5", the parents' relatings, and its 13 derived lights were pairs of those relatings linked through the shared corner or through the opposite midpoint. The 47 shared-corner lights over the twelve sites were exactly the 47 generation-1 passages. A relating is a signal on an edge; a passage through Z is a channel; the verdict is the channel's value: composed passes the signal along (the direct becomes the face's, subtracted from the site's own), "not" interferes, a light is a channel whose source signal nobody gave, a tension is a channel meeting an inhibitor. The generation-2 relating between two instances is the postponed composite of the generation-1 path, "the path is the mode pick", which is Bellin's `postpone` read at the next resolution. In Bellin's and Crolard's terms a LIGHT is a suspended coroutine, a continuation made and not yet resumed ("through Matter it would read x rests-on y — no relating between them says so"); giving the direct resumes it (the passage turns COMPOSED); a bar at its target raises the exception (TENSION). The device's "remote" reads (a passage at e captures a role z on another edge and substitutes a composite) are the remote capture and remote substitution of that calculus.

### 1.5 Algebra and coalgebra: the two modules

The Ambo builds the record and evaluates it: casts, relatings, rules, the sorting. Its law "RECORD, NOT READING — serialize inputs, re-derive everything" is the discipline of an initial algebra: the record is the term, every reading is evaluation. The Manuscript observes behaviour: the argument reading puts the map first and the verdict last, and "explore inside" reads a closed surface by walking it, where "a deck transformation is invisible from inside, so a cone's mark is the room returning early, counted in doors". That is a final-coalgebra reading: two rooms are the same if no walk distinguishes them, and the return count is a bisimulation invariant. The speaker's fixed point is the device's quiescence: the states OPEN, EXHAUSTED, POCKET, CLOSED are values at a stage where nothing more is said, and today the names were read only after all 196 passages had been answered.

## 2 · Where the device falls short of its own logic (all measured today)

1. **The act between instances has no route.** At generation 2 the drawing offers the born room's seed roles; a mode pick on them is refused ("B:M3 is not a role of Material necessity"); the IS pair is taken by the pairing, counted by the sorting and a stray for the child. The channel-into-channel exists in the model and not on the surface.
2. **The seed corner's view is empty at generation 2.** A corner edge has no site, so every generation-2 site reads "Relation — no passage yet". The sorting subtracts two projections of three; the own parts are over-counted by whatever the corner's coordinate lights would have reached.
3. **Channels are undirected in composition.** A leg read from the passage's own corner keeps its word, so "L1 grounds R2" prints as "R2 grounds L1". The person's words are directed; co-implication is not symmetric (a −< b is not b −< a). I had to mint fourteen inverse words by hand to say the composites in the sites' directions.
4. **The channels do not cross the lift.** D12 rides IS-instances only; every lifted corner "holds a space of 9 roles · 20 words · 72 tuples", the identity-regime gluing; the 72 relatings, the says and the rules are absent; a form born after the lift "holds no concept-space, and none is minted". The meaning-trace law says the torus is the geometry in which the concepts are identified; the geometry crosses, the concepts' channels do not.
5. **No quiescence, no dependency.** A site says how many of its own passages are unanswered, not whether passages elsewhere still bear on its own part. A name given live is given blind to that; the device reports the drift only afterwards.
6. **One label per site, whatever the state.** The method gives a name to a core and withholds it from an all-boundary (EXHAUSTED) or a contested (POCKET) site; the person overruled today and named all twelve; the device recorded the state but has no act for naming a boundary as a boundary, nor for D9's authorization of a split.

## 3 · The proposition

**T1 (regions).** The lattice of lift regions is bi-Heyting; the rim is the co-Heyting boundary ∂R = R ∧ ~R; identify and glue are maps that reduce ∂; closedness is ∂R = 0. The Leibniz rule is a law of the device's region logic and a witness should pin it.

**T2 (sites).** A site's content decomposes as core plus boundary: core = OWN = E − P, boundary = E ∧ P present at each face as form, with LIGHT the boundary's excess over the site and TENSION the boundary against an inhibitor. EXHAUSTED is "all boundary"; POCKET is a boundary the views draw differently.

**T3 (stages).** The person–device loop is a Rauszer frame whose worlds are stages of the record. "The face's" is an intuitionistic (persistent) fact; "own" is not; "has been own" is a co-implication whose witness is a stage; a name is the realizer of that co-implication and must carry its stage. The state words OPEN, EXHAUSTED, POCKET, CLOSED are readings at a stage and are final only at quiescence.

**T4 (channels).** Passages are channels between signals; verdicts are the channel's value; lights are suspended continuations; tensions are exceptions; descent turns channels into signals; a relating at generation n+1 is the postponed composite of a path at generation n. Direction is part of a channel.

**T5 (forces).** The person supplies implication (rules), hypotheses (relatings) and refutations (bars, "not"); the device supplies subtraction only. The layer is a falsification logic with contradiction confined to boundaries.

**T6 (modules).** The Ambo is the algebra of the record; the Manuscript is its coalgebra; the argument reading's order (map, incidence, stance, verdict) is the order algebra then observation; the form that crosses the lift should be the record's form, not roles.

## 5 · What is not claimed

The instance-level sorting is Boolean in its statics; the co-Heyting content is in the regions and in the stages. The algebra/coalgebra reading of the two modules is a reading. Bellin's calculus is for the disjunctive–subtractive and linear fragments and the "suspended coroutine" gloss of a LIGHT is an analogy of behaviour, not a translation of proofs. The speaker's phi machines are not in print that I could find; the parts cited are. Whether Kant's table, run through this device, has the mathematical corners exhausted and the dynamical corners keeping their own parts is one run's pattern, not a result.
