## 5 · The squares as arguments

Each square is the vertex figure of a generation-1 concept: its four corners are that concept's four generation-2 sites, one with each of its neighbours. Limit's square: Cessation (with Alteration), Thoroughgoing determination (with Matter), Moment (with Validity), State (with Quantum of substance). The four edges are the pairs of these sites that share a second generation-1 parent through a face; the two diagonals join the sites whose other parents are opposite corners of the tetrahedron.

Read as an argument in the device's own grammar: the lifted square's map says only "lifted whole" — four concepts, four derived relations, every corner a 90° rim turn, the rim open. Its content is the four own parts: the degree ends in negation (Cessation); the degree's determination is of the individual (Thoroughgoing determination); the degree is at an instant and contingent (Moment); the degree is a state of the persisting quantum (State). The band the identify made says: `Thoroughgoing determination·Cessation ← {Thoroughgoing determination, Cessation}` and `Moment·State ← {Moment, State}` — "4 concepts become 2": the complete determination of a degree *is* its cessation (the limit reached), and the degree's instant *is* its state. The twist would have paired Thoroughgoing determination with State and Moment with Cessation, reversed: the device's word for that is "non-orientable", and the reading warns "a rim stays open". The torus, made by the committed word, says the rest in the device's own line: `Thoroughgoing determination·Moment·Cessation·State ← {…}` — "4 concepts become 1", the stance `90 ⊕ 90 ⊕ 90 ⊕ 90 = 360`, the verdict "the sheet tiles flat". Read as an argument: the degree (Limit) is one concept in which its four determinations meet without a seam, and the two generators the device names are the two ways round it — the longitude `State→Cessation · Thoroughgoing determination→Moment` (from the state of the persisting quantum to its cessation, and from the individual's determination to its instant) and the meridian `Cessation→Thoroughgoing determination · Moment→State`. The four corners of the square were the site's four own parts; the torus is the geometry in which they are identified — the operation is the meaning, the name (Torus) its index.

The same reading holds for the other five squares with their corner quadruples: Quantum of substance (Community of substances, Permanence, State, Duration); Validity (Actuality, Hypothetical necessity, Duration, Moment); Alteration (All of reality, Cessation, Permanence, Law of reciprocity); Matter (Actuality, Thoroughgoing determination, All of reality, Transcendental substratum); Material necessity (Community of substances, Hypothetical necessity, Transcendental substratum, Law of reciprocity).
