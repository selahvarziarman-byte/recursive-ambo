## The division of labour

| | Arman | you |
|---|---|---|
| **the work** | the **thought**: what each concept holds, what each thing is called, which things are the same | the **device**: you make the engine take his thought, as casts, clicks, pairs and names typed where they go |
| **the reports** | **functional and conceptual** bugs and needs | the **technical** part |

**The one rule this implies: never supply the thought part.** Where the device needs a name, a relation or a decision that only Arman can give, ask him. A thing he has not named stays unnamed.
