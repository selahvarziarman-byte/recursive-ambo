## 1 · THE RANGE IS THE RULED KIND — and the type carries it whole
**Arman ruled the KIND of a concept-space once for the run: a FINITE RELATIONAL STRUCTURE, relations given, lattice derived** (P0 §8–9, 09-05: *"the kind of structure is chosen ONCE for a run"* — Form 2). ADR 0031 §1.1 is that ruling as a definition: roles; relation-types with any finite arity (unary types included); a three-valued instance function; axioms as sentences over the signature; marks unread. **`ConceptSpace` at `8bda983` carries exactly this and nothing less** — `roles` (id; caster's `label`; `types` declared-or-`UNKNOWN`; `marks` carried), `signature` (type, any arity), `relations` (type, terms, polarity — unrecorded = absent), `axioms` (carried strings), `warrant` (carried).
⇒ **Answer 1: the range of a cast IS the type — because the type is the ruled kind, not because the type is all we could think of.** *"Many shapes"* are SHAPES of relational structure: a diagram, a table, a taxonomy, a story's cast with its relations, **a triangulation with its face cast as an arity-3 relation** — anything with distinguishable positions and named relations among them. What is NOT in the range is excluded by the sovereign ruling, not by the type's poverty: an infinite or continuous instance-space (finiteness), amounts as structure (they are MARKS), and relations *between relations* except by reification (a role standing for a relation-instance). Those are the three places the range would widen if Arman ever ruled it wider; none is asked for by his examples.
**Two clarifications, neither a range gap:** (a) axioms are carried as strings now and READ at amalgams later (ADR 0031 §2.5 — dissolved bounds, marked); *the form of "a sentence over the signature" is owed by me when that check charters.* (b) **the type carries no field for the caster's SUBJECT MATTER** — and by the mold's stipulation a concept is a structure *together with what it is of* (two isomorphic casts with different subject matters are two concepts). Today it can ride only in `warrant` or nowhere. That is a gap in CARRIED (unread) content — the coder's to price, the mothership's to sequence — **and it must never be the corner's `label`** (the person's name-slot; the type's own comment already forbids the loader that would fill it).

## 2 · A THIN CAST IS FIRST-CLASS — the device may never grade thinness, and must always READ it
**Well-formedness of a STRUCTURE is three checks and no more:** closure (every term of every relation is a role — no dangling reference) · arity (every tuple has its type's length) · types declared or `UNKNOWN` on every role. **Axioms, warrant, reasons, sources, premises, the derived check, open forks, did-not-fit are MOLD v4's honesty apparatus for the PERSON — a PROFILE over the type, exactly as the designer measured. The device reads none of them** (the mold says so of each: marks unread, warrant never read, reasons are what the structure is *of*). ⇒ **Answer 2: Arman's triangulation is a cast fully and without deficiency. The device may never mark a cast as thin.** Completeness is not computable, ever (mold §2.7); its honesty lives in warrant, which the person reads.
**What thinness DOES is not a grade but a READING the structure yields, and the surface must show it — computed on Arman's own example:**
```
T-cyc   three roles, one relation-type, r(x,y) r(y,z) r(z,x)   |Aut| = 3   candidates against a copy: 3 tied (weight 3)   meet = ∅
T-sym   the same, both directions listed                        |Aut| = 6   6 tied (weight 6)                              meet = ∅
T-3typ  three DIFFERENT relation-types, one instance each        |Aut| = 1   ONE candidate (weight 3)                        meet = everything
bare    three roles, no relations                                |Aut| = 6   no candidate of weight > 0 — the EMPTY CORE
```
**Gauge (`|Aut|`), the tied candidates and their meet, the exposure — these are what a thin structure says about itself; a thick one says the opposite. They are readings (ADR 0031 §3.6), not marks of deficiency.** Against a thin neighbour an isomorphy is noise (mold §3.4) — reported as a reading with its weight, never suppressed. **The empty core** — roles and no relations — is usable only by fiat and is offered as a STATE (*no relation to agree on*), never a blank. **And the empty cast (no roles) is lawful and vacuous, and must remain distinguishable from the TRUE ABSENCE of a cast** (`cast` undefined): *a cast of nothing* ≠ *no cast* — positive presence at the corner.

## 2b · CORRECTION (appended 2026-09-19, Arman's catch): §2's "three checks" mis-filed one item and omitted one
Arman: *"the designer's third check was a relation type absent from the signature; yours is about role types — an undeclared type has no arity, so the arity check can't apply to it. Inside closure, or a fourth?"* **Inside closure — and my list was wrong twice.**
- **CLOSURE = every reference the record makes RESOLVES**: each term of each relation to a role; **each relation's `type` to the signature**; each unary key on a role is an arity-1 relation-type, declared by its use (a signature entry of the same name must have arity 1). An unresolved type is a dangling reference of the same kind as an unlisted role, and **arity presupposes closure** — an unresolved type has no arity to check.
- **ARITY** = every tuple whose type resolved has that type's length.
- **CONSISTENCY = the record is a FUNCTION** — the check I omitted: no `(type, terms)` listed with both polarities; no duplicate role id; no signature name declared twice with different arities. A cast asserting `holds` and `does-not-hold` of one tuple is not three-valued; it is ill-formed, and the device refuses it BY NAME — never picks a polarity, never averages. The type as cut (`ConceptRelation[]` with per-entry `polarity`) can represent the contradiction, so the check is real.
- ⛔ **"Types declared or `UNKNOWN` on every role" is NOT a structural check — it is MOLD v4 §2.6's clause on CASTERS (the profile).** At the device's grain an omitted unary type reads as UNRECORDED, exactly like an unlisted tuple; the surface may show *omitted* against *marked unknown* as a fact of the record, and never fail a cast on it. I substituted this profile clause for the designer's structural check and did not say so; withdrawn from §2 and §4.
⇒ **The structural checks are three: CLOSURE · ARITY · CONSISTENCY.** §4's list for the surface reads accordingly: *dangling reference (role OR relation-type) · malformed tuple · contradictory record* — and never grades.

## 2c · THE GRAIN OF THE CONSISTENCY REFUSAL (appended `Sat Sep 19 13:46:40 +03:30 2026`, the mothership's one line, the designer's reading)
