## 0 · What the device does with your cast now — so the shape makes sense

A cast is a concept written as one file. The device takes it at a **corner of the seed solid** and nowhere else; a midpoint's space is never loaded, it is derived. From then on these things happen to it, and the file's shape exists so that each reads truly:

1. **The corner holds it, and the person names the corner.** The concept's name is typed on the corner's card by the person; loading never fills it. Inside the cast, the caster's words are the moments' labels and glosses, the words of the signature, the qualities and the subject matter. The device draws the cast and reads it back as counts, never as a grade.
2. **The person relates moments across an edge, and every relating is a WAY.** Between two corners he picks a moment on each side and says a way the two are one: in `IS` (`≡`, the way with no name: the two are one and nothing more is said) or in a word of his (`the price is the case as the instituted`), with a direction and `it holds · it does not hold`. Whether a word keeps the one-ness is his judgement at the act and never the device's; the page shows the norm's line once where a new word is typed: `a word here names a way the two are one, never one that keeps them apart`.
3. **The midpoint holds the child.** The child's moments are his relatings, nothing of either parent that met nothing. Its own relations are FILLED LOOPS: between two of its moments the device lays out the loop made of your cast's way between their ends on one side, the other cast's way between their ends on the other side, and the two relatings; he says what the two ways round come to, and where they come to the same the loop is filled and is a relation of the child. The parents' relations as read at the child's ends are shown beneath, as READ, never as the child's relations.
4. **The third corner speaks in a light.** For each face, the corner opposite an edge states its share of the two ends' moments at the ALTITUDE: present, denied or silent, cell by cell, in its own word (`the deed runs as the good`). Those sayings are named ways too, from a moment of the third to a moment of an end. Through them the device lays out passages (forks at one of the third's moments; bonds across one of the third's own relations) and he decides what each comes to; his decisions sort the edge's relatings into his own, the face's, lights, tensions and refused routes.
5. **Names.** He names the midpoint, its moments and its filled relations. A name designates; filling makes a relation; a name over several kinds of filled loop makes them one relation-type of the child as a parent.
6. **The walk.** Transport (the lift, the door, the cargo) rides every way, carrying its word: a walk is a tree of routes, one road per way holding the role at each step; a route's word is the composite of the words walked, by his rules, else unruled; the identification structure's readings (Fix · Mov · Und) are read on the `IS` routes alone, and routes with a named leg are listed as lights.
7. **The next generation.** The child, with its moments and its filled relations, is a cast of this mold and a corner of the next generation; nothing is poured for it.

**What changed since v5, in one list.** Every relation at every level is a **named way two things are one**: a cast's own words, the edge's modes and a light's sayings are one kind, and `IS` is the way with no name. **The casts are under the norm**: a cast's word names a way one moment is the next, and a word that keeps its two moments two is not poured; the device checks none of it. **The form is binary**: a word of three or more places stands outside the norm. **A symmetric way is listed once.** **A role is a moment**, with a required gloss; two moments stay two whatever their relations. **The child's relations are filled loops**, named by the person; **the born cast is a cast of this mold**. The lexicon's facts (a converse, the opaque bit) hold for a cast's words as for a mode's.

## 1 · What a cast IS

> **A concept-space is a finite set of MOMENTS, and the NAMED WAYS its moments are one**: a finite set of **roles**, each a moment with its determinacy; a **signature** of words, each the name of a way, of two places; the **relations** known to hold or known not to hold, each a way from one moment to another in a word, listed with its two terms in order; **qualities**, relations of one place on a moment, carried as marks; **axioms**, sentences carried and never evaluated; and **marks**, recorded and never read. A concept is this structure together with its **subject matter** and its moments' **determinacies**, carried and read by the person. (ADR 0031 §1.1 with its §9.31 clause; §9.56.)

- **A role is a MOMENT** of the concept: a determinate way the concept shows up, with its own determinacy in the caster's words, the gloss with its examples, and its ways to the other moments. The device reads its position (id, qualities, relations); the person reads its determinacy. **Two moments are two roles, whatever their recorded relations**; a way between them keeps them two and one, as a path between two distinct points keeps both. Only `IS`, the way with no name, would make two moments one, and a cast never writes `IS` between its own moments. (§8 A1 of v5, ratified at §9.31; §9.56.)
- **A cast's word is the name of a way one moment is the next**: `the possible passes into the obtaining` says that one and the same way things could stand shows up as the next moment. That is the concept's own movement, and it is of the same kind as a relating at an edge. The caster is the namer here, as the person is at an edge: whether a word keeps the one-ness is the caster's judgement, and the device cannot read it. (§9.50 (1); §9.52; §9.56.)
- **The form is binary.** A way has two ends. A word of three or more places is not a way; it stands outside the norm, carried as the cast lists it, read by no loop and no walk, counted apart. Re-pouring it as a moment with ways to its terms is the caster's. (§8.8 of v5; §9.43 (3); §9.56.)
- **Relations are given; the movement is a way, never `IS`; everything else is derived.** Never hand in a ranking of moments beside the relations, since the device would read it as structure; the concept's own movement, one moment passing into the next, is written as ways, tuple by tuple, with direction and polarity. (§8 A5 of v5 with §8.8.)
- **What the device reads, and what it carries.** It reads three lists, `roles`, `signature`, `relations`, and one line, the subject matter. It carries everything else unread: glosses, sources, reasons, meanings, properties, premises, open forks, what did not fit, any field of the caster's own. Carried is not lost: the card says `a warrant (kept, not read)` and the drawing prints each axiom as `axiom (kept, not checked): …`. The device checks what is true of a STRUCTURE, never of a PRACTICE, and it checks nothing of the norm.
- **Three structural checks, and only these.** CLOSURE: every term of every relation names a role of this cast, and every relation's word is in the signature. ARITY: a relation has as many terms as its word declares. CONSISTENCY: the record is a function: no tuple listed both as holding and as not holding, no role id declared twice with different content, no word declared with two arities, no name that is both a word and a quality. Closure and arity breaks are taken and marked; only consistency refuses.

## 2 · The parts of the file, one by one

### 2.1 `roles` — the moments
- Each role is an object with an **`id`** (required, unique; the device's handle, never shown as a name), a **`label`** (the caster's word for the moment; shown wherever the moment appears; a role with no label shows its id, marked as an address), a **`gloss`** (required: one line, the moment's determinacy with its examples, in the caster's words; carried, never read for a check; shown beside the label wherever the moment is picked; a role without a gloss loads and is marked on the page as an address, as a role without a label is), and **`types`**, the moment's qualities.
- **Qualities are relations of one place** and are not under the norm: a quality says something of one moment, not a way between two. Declare a quality on every moment, or write `UNKNOWN`; an omission and a written `UNKNOWN` both mean unknown, never a value. The device never reads an absence as a negative. On the page a quality left open reads `left open`; `UNKNOWN` prints nowhere, and a value left open is never counted among the values given. At a midpoint a quality comes through as a mark of the child's moment.
- **`member_status`** stays the one quality the mold itself defines (`has` · `unrecorded` · `none-by-nature`), optional, compared by the device only when both casts declare it, as v5 §2.1 says.
- Anything else on a role (a source, a note, an amount, a window) is a mark, carried, never read. **Amounts are marks.**

### 2.2 `signature` — the cast's words, each the name of a way
- Each entry has a **`type`** (the word) and an **`arity`**, which under the norm is **2**. A `meaning` is strongly asked for, in this form: one sentence of what the word says, and then **the way the two are one** (`the first is the second by being counted so … The way the two are one: counting`). It is carried, never read; it is what a translator and the person use.
- **The words are the cast's own, and foreign across casts until translated by hand.** The device never matches two casts' words by spelling: `no words translated yet (words spelled alike are still two words)`. A cast's word and a mode of his are one kind of word, so a cast's word may come to be spelled like a mode at an edge; they stay two words until he translates them (τ), which is his act, never the device's.
- **A converse and the opaque bit are the lexicon's, not the file's.** A way is read back without a converse word; a converse (`w the other way round: [a word]`) is an equation of the lexicon the person may give, and the opaque bit (`in w, paired roles stand in for each other · they don't`) is declared there too, for intensional words only. For a cast's word they are declared where the word first meets an edge: a route, a translation, a rule. `properties` on an entry (`symmetric`, `a partial order`) is for the reader; the device reads none of it. (§9.53; §9.58 (2).)
- A word with no listed tuple is padding; declare a word only when it has an instance.
- A word of **three or more places** may still be written (the loader takes any arity); it is outside the norm, drawn as a tuple-node, read by no loop and no walk, and should be rare and said so in `open_forks`.

### 2.3 `relations` — the ways that hold and the ways refused
- Each has a **`type`** (a word of the signature), **`terms`** (two role ids, **in order**: the way runs from the first to the second, as the word reads), and a **`polarity`**: `holds` or `does-not-hold`. A `reason`, sourced, is asked for and carried.
- **Unlisted is unrecorded**, unknown, never false. A source's denial is written `does-not-hold`: a refused way, drawn as a bar; silence is nothing. A refused way is content: it individuates, and it is shown. A missing negative is exposure.
- **A symmetric way is listed ONCE**, in the order the caster chooses; its symmetry is the converse equation of the lexicon (`conv(w) = w`), not a second tuple. Listing both orders records one way twice, and the loops count it twice. (§9.58 (1).)
- **A way with the same moment at both ends** (`sustains(r7, r7)`) is lawful and drawn as a loop at the moment.
- `r(x, y)` is not `r(y, x)`: term order is content. A tuple listed twice with identical content is read once.
- **A term that is not a moment** (a parameter, a condition, a thing outside the concept) is one of three things: an unlisted moment (admit it, with its ways), a quality (fold it in), or a mark (annotate). Never leave it dangling: the device takes the tuple and marks it, `relation 3: "F9" isn't a role in this cast`.
- **Dynamics are not structure**: path-dependence, a sign that flips, a relation that modifies its own terms are marks on the relation.

### 2.4 `axioms` — sentences, kept
- Strings, or objects with a `sentence`. Carried whole, counted on the card, printed on the drawing, never checked. An axiom is a sentence that is not a single instance; a relation left out because its word keeps two moments two may live on here as a sentence (`the right of knowledge`, `justifications end in what we do`), which is where Virgin Land put two of its left-out words on 2026-10-17.

### 2.5 `subject_matter` — what the concept is OF
- One line, in the caster's words, read on the card beside the person's name for the corner. Two casts with one structure and two subject matters are two concepts with one shape.

### 2.6 Everything else — the caster's honesty, carried
- `premises`, `open_forks`, `did_not_fit`, `derived_check`, `cast_by`, a `source`: carried, read by nobody but the people who open the file. Under the norm `did_not_fit` has one more use, and Virgin Land made it first: **a word left out because it keeps two moments two is written there with its reason** (`'presupposes': a condition and what it conditions stay two`). That is the caster's judgement on record, and it is what a second caster, or the person, reads to disagree with.

### 2.7 What the device answers when you load it
- **Taken:** since slice 3's F the line reads `loaded: 8 roles · 11 relations in 6 words · 3 qualities, 11 values given · read as directed · …`: the tuples and the signature's words counted together, the moments' qualities apart with the values given (a value left open is not among them), a zero part not printed; then the axioms and the warrant clauses as before, when there is something to carry. The rest is as v5 §2.7: refused two ways only (not a cast; the record contradicts itself, every address named); taken with marks for a dangling term, a word not in the signature, a wrong number of terms, an item it could not take; `this cast has no roles`. No tick, no `valid`, no green, ever. The device says nothing about the norm.
- **The card's `read as directed · read as symmetric` rows** report how the tuples were listed. Under §2.3 a symmetric way listed once reads `directed` there; that row is a reading of the file, not of the way. (§9.58 (3).)

### 2.8 The drawing
A column of points in the caster's own order, each printing its name alone; resting on a point gives the moment's meaning (its gloss) first, then its qualities, each named with its value, a quality left open reading `left open`; an arc from the first term to the second with its word on it; a loop for a way with one moment at both ends; a tuple-node for a word of three or more places; a bar for a refused way; the unrecorded as nothing at all.

## 3 · How to pour — the practice, in order

1. **Write the subject matter line first.** If you cannot write it, you have a word, not a concept.
2. **List the MOMENTS honestly**: the ways the concept shows up in the sources, each with its determinacy in one line with examples. Keep every moment the concept has; keep two moments two even where their ways are alike; never a count chosen to look right. Four to seven and the logical square are a working size and one honest shape, examples both.
3. **Give each moment a short id, a label and a gloss.** The id is a handle; the label is the word that will stand on the page and in every relating; the gloss is its determinacy.
4. **Choose the cast's words, each the name of a way one moment is the next.** Two places each. Few, and shared where they honestly can be, for a reason the device gives, not a taste: his rules bind a SHAPE of words, and a translation (τ) fills a loop by law where the two casts' words are one word, so every extra word multiplies the shapes the person must rule one by one and the loops he must fill by hand; four words on each of two casts give sixteen word-pairs for a rule to cover, two give four. The old motive, a general signature the device could compose on by itself, is retired (Δ80): nothing composes but his rules and his translations. And the norm comes first: a word that keeps two moments two is not made a way by being general, and a way that is specific to this concept's movement (*counts as*) is a better word than a general one that keeps two (*presupposes*). For each word, write what it says and then *the way the two are one*. **Ask of every word: does it say the two moments are one in this way, or does it keep them two?** A word that keeps them two (a condition and what it conditions; a bearer and the borne; a terminus and what rests in it; a cause and its effect, read as two) is not poured; write it into `did_not_fit` with the reason, or keep its content as an axiom. This is your judgement and the device will not make it. The page says the same to the person at every new word: `a word here names a way the two are one, never one that keeps them apart`.
5. **List the tuples.** Two terms in order; a symmetric way once; a refused way wherever a source denies it; nothing where a source is silent; a one-line reason with its page. Never pad; a refusal from a source is as good as a holding.
6. **Declare the qualities** on every moment or write `UNKNOWN`; `member_status` only if it is a fact of the concept; amounts and windows in marks.
7. **Write the axioms**, the premises, the open forks, and what did not fit, the left-out words among them.
8. **Check before loading**: every term a role id; every word in the signature with two terms; no tuple with both polarities; ids unique; no name both a word and a quality; every moment glossed; no symmetric way listed twice.
9. **Save as `<name>.cast.json` and load it at the seed corner it belongs to**, read the `loaded:` line, open the drawing, type the person's name for the corner on the card, export the workspace afterwards.
10. **Cast each concept on its own.** The only across-concept thought allowed while casting is that your words should be translatable. Shaping a concept to make its relatings with a neighbour come out well is the one thing the mold most forbids.

## 4 · What happens to your cast after it is taken — so you pour for it

- **At the edge, every relating is a way.** The person picks a moment on each side and says a way the two are one: `≡` (`IS`, the blank way), or a word of his with a direction. A relating written in `IS` by word is refused: `in IS this is a pair; pair it instead`. Whether a word of his keeps the one-ness is his, and the page shows the norm's line once per new word. A way reads back without a converse; a converse and the opaque bit are declared in the lexicon, once per word; a relating in a word he declared opaque is refused by name, and so is declaring opaque a word already in use. (§9.50–§9.53.)
- **The lexicon is one kind of word everywhere.** His modes, your cast's words and a light's sayings are all named ways. Your signature's words do not appear on the modes line by themselves, since words are foreign across casts until he translates them; a translation (τ) is his act and makes two words one.
- **At the midpoint, the child.** Its moments are his relatings, each labelled by its sentence or by his name for it; nothing of a parent that met nothing. Its relations are the **filled loops**: for two of its moments `i = (x_i, y_i)` and `j = (x_j, y_j)`, the loop is your cast's way between `x_i` and `x_j` (or their sameness), the relating `j`, the other cast's way between `y_j` and `y_i` (or their sameness), and the relating `i`. The loop is closed when both casts say something there, open when either is silent (nothing, never shown). The device lays out the loop's two ways round to each of its two cross cells; he says what each comes to; the same fills the loop, different leaves a hole, a refused way on a side is form. A filled loop is a relation of the child, keyed by its kind (your word with its cast, direction and sign, and the other cast's), named by him or read as its loop. **So your cast's ways are what the child's relations are made of, and your refusals are what makes a route a refused one.** (§9.40–§9.49.)
- **The third corner speaks at the altitude.** The corner opposite an edge states, cell by cell, which of its moments are present in, or denied of, each moment of the two ends, in its own word: named ways from its moments to the ends'. Forks (two of its sayings at one of its moments) and bonds (two sayings across one of its own ways) are passages he decides; what a passage comes to is a way-word at the pair; a decision with no direct relating standing is the third's light, never a relating. **Your cast's own ways are the bonds' middle legs**, which is why they must be ways: a composite is decided to a way-word only if every leg is a way. (§9.29–§9.30; §9.35; §9.56 addendum.)
- **Names.** He names the midpoint, each of its moments (a given name beside the sentence; unique in the child), and its filled relations (a word for one or more kinds). Filling makes the relation; naming designates it; only joining kinds under one word, parting them, or giving a direction with a name changes what the next generation reads; nothing he said at a later generation is ever dropped silently. (§9.38 (c); §9.48.)
- **The walk.** The lift, the door and the cargo ride every way, carrying its word. A role held by several ways on one edge is a family; a walk is a tree of routes, finite by construction; a route with a named leg that returns to another moment of a corner derives a light of that corner (`F5 carries F7`), never a merge; Fix · Mov · Und are read on the `IS` routes alone. Your cast's ways are of the same kind, and no reader walks inside a cast today. (§9.50 (3); §9.54; §9.57.)
- **What your choices do downstream.** A thin cast is first-class. A missing refusal leaves exposure. A padded moment, an unfounded tuple, a ranking handed in, a placeholder: none of these is caught, and each produces a false reading with nothing to flag it. **A word that keeps two moments two, poured as a way, is not caught either**: the child's loops will ask what it comes to as a way, and the person will find the question unanswerable at that loop. That is the norm's own kill test, and it falls on the caster.

## 5 · What breaks the mold

A count chosen to look right · a relation without a reason · a ranking handed in as input · a placeholder where you were unsure · a concept-specific term used as a word · a premise that shaped the list and went unstated · a term that is not a moment · a quality declared on some moments and silently absent on others · **a symmetric way listed twice** · two measures under one word · a dynamic encoded as static structure · a concept shaped to fit its neighbours · a reason that cites the lens instead of the subject · a word declared with no instance · a label written as a placeholder · a moment without a gloss · **a word poured as a way that keeps its two moments two**.

**What the device refuses by name:** a file that is not a cast; a record that contradicts itself. **What it takes and marks:** a dangling term, a word not in the signature, a wrong number of terms, an item it could not take, a moment without a gloss. **What it never does:** fill a name, grade a cast, read an absence as a fact, offer a pairing, match two casts' words by spelling, judge whether a word keeps the one-ness, or fail a cast for what its neighbours turned out to be.

## 8 · A born cast is a cast of this mold

A midpoint's child, once the person has related, is a cast in the sense of §1, poured by the device from his acts and never by hand:
- its **moments** are his relatings, each glossed by its own sentence (`the price is the case as the instituted`) and labelled by his given name where he gave one;
- its **words** are the kinds of its filled loops, each named by him or read as its loop until named; one word over several kinds is one relation-type;
- its **relations** are the filled loops, each a way from one of its moments to another, with the direction his name gives (or none, where the parents' words ran opposite ways), every one holding; a hole (the two ways round coming to different words) is information, shown and never resolved by the device; a loop across a refusal is form; an open loop is nothing;
- its **qualities** are the parents' marks pulled back; its **READ record** is the parents' relations at its ends, shown beneath and never read as its relations;
- with nothing filled it is a **thin cast**, moments and no relations, and it is a legitimate corner of the next generation; nothing waits on filling or naming.
The three checks hold of it by construction; the norm holds of it because every filled loop is two ways agreeing; the device never pours a word into it. At the next generation its moments are points and its relations arcs, and the loops, the altitude and the walk read it as they read a seed. (D4; §9.40; §9.41; §9.46; §9.48; §9.56.)
