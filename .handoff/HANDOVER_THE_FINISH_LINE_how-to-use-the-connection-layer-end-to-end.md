# THE FINISH LINE — how to use the connection layer, end to end

*For Arman and the agent beside him (Δ93). The engine is whole at `6cea4a1`. Every step below was walked by the mothership in the in-app browser (§127 · §129 · §142). What you find broken or unreadable is the first set of findings after the finish — write them to `.handoff/inbox/mothership/`.*

**Run it:** `npm run dev` in the repo, then open `http://localhost:5173`. The Ambo Universe opens on a tetrahedron.

## 1 · Give concepts to the corners (Ambo)
1. Click the solid. **Selection** tab → click corner **A**. **Packets** tab → **load cast… (.cast.json)** → pick a file from `scripts/fixtures/casts/` (`flow` · `phi` · `t-cell`). The card says *taken — N roles · …*.
2. Do the same for **B**, **C**, **D**. *(Casts go on the seed's corners only — Δ86. A midpoint's concept is never loaded; it is made.)*

## 2 · Map concepts at the midpoints
3. **Apply Ambo Dissection.** **Workspace** tab → select the **octahedron** (the core). **Selection** tab → click **AB**.
4. The panel unfolds the two corners' concepts. **A role:** click a role in A's column, then one in B's. **A word:** click a word in A's row, then one in B's. Each pair is yours and has a **withdraw**. A pair that contradicts a cast is **not taken**, with the reason and the hand to undo.
5. The midpoint draws its own concept (its own column below). Do the same at **AC** and **BC**.

## 3 · Read a face
6. Back at **AB**: the face **A·B·C** reads once A–B, B–C, C–A all hold pairs — per corner, *returned to itself · returned elsewhere · did not return*, the direction stated.

## 4 · The born room (every generation)
7. Workspace → select the octahedron → **Apply Ambo Dissection** again. Select the **cuboctahedron** → Selection → **ABAC**. Corner A's roles stand on both sides as one (*composed, not yours* — hollow rings); the rest is yours to pair (*yours, born here*). A pair elsewhere that would break it is refused with two hands.
8. **To read an interior face:** the left panel's **Explode View** slider pulls the cells apart; select the face.

## 5 · Lift into the Manuscript
9. ⚠ **Lift BEFORE a second dissection** (the app has no way back to an earlier shape yet). Workspace → select a corner cell (the **tetrahedron** whose corners are `A · AB · AC · AD`) → **Lift selection → Manuscript**.
10. **Manuscript** (top). Drag the shelf entry onto the sheet. Its card reads *read from the record the lift carried — N of N corners hold a space*; click a corner to see its concept.

## 6 · Build a room and give its door a concept transport
11. **aperture — build a 3-manifold**. In the row pick face A (e.g. `A·AC·AB`), face B (`A·AB·AD`), and a map (e.g. `A→A · AC→AD · AB→AB`).
12. The door says *carries no concept yet*. Click a role on one side's corner, then one on the other's: **taken** takes its whole line (one withdraw); **not taken** says where the lines part, or what the record forbids.
13. **glue — the S² gate judges.**

## 7 · Walk carrying a concept
14. Double-click the built room on the sheet to select it → **explore inside**.
15. Pick a role at the entry corner (*carry from the corner A: F1 F2 …*). Move it corner to corner with the rod buttons (*carry it along: A–AC …*). Cross a door by its key (the door's letter, e.g. `a`).
16. The line reads what happened, with the route: *returned to itself by A–AC · a · AD–A* · *returned as …* · *not here by …: lost at the door a, which does not carry it* · a spur told from a residue by the reduced route.
