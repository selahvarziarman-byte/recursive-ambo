# THE PROJECTION · a construction

**from:** the researcher · **for:** Arman's grill on the projection problem · **status:** a construction for grilling, not a ruling · **ground:** Arman's early picture (04-24 15:53 / 16:16: the midpoint is where the opposite edge "contains the third vertex in an orthogonal, excluded form", "an inverted reflection of the third vertex on the opposite edge") and the engine's own objects (ADR 0031: casts, the person's pairings, the pushout, the feet). The later record (readers R1–R4) enters only as tests at the end.

---

## 0 · The question, and what a construction must deliver

The engine has a triangulated complex. Each vertex carries a concept. Each edge AB carries the person's pairing of roles, and its child M_AB is the pushout: the two casts side by side with the paired roles made one. Each face ABC has, for its edge AB, an opposite vertex C.

The question: **what is the relation between C and M_AB?** A construction must give the relation's *type* (what kind of thing it is), its *direction* (from what into what), its *sign* (positive or negative), its *material* (what it is made of), its *composition* (two opposite corners on one edge), its *recursion* (the next generation), and its *tests* (what would show it false).

The early picture says, in geometry: drop the perpendicular from C to the line AB; its foot q is the point of the edge at which C's direction has no component along the edge. Reflect C across AB to C′; q is the midpoint of CC′. At q, C is "present in an excluded form": nothing of C lies along the edge there, and that very absence is what marks the point.

The construction below translates that picture into the engine's objects and nothing else. One translation carries the whole thing: **the part of C that lies along the edge is what C shares with A and with B, and the person has said exactly what that is: the pairings on the face's other two edges.** Everything C has beyond that is its perpendicular part and never reaches the edge.

---

## 1 · The objects

- A concept X has a finite set of roles R_X (each with its relation-type; types ride along and are never changed here).
- For an edge XY the person's pairing is J_XY, a partial one-to-one correspondence between R_X and R_Y. J_YX is its inverse. "J_XY(x) = y" reads: the person said the role x of X is the role y of Y. Where J_XY(x) is undefined the person has said nothing.
- The child of the edge AB is M_AB = (R_A ⊔ R_B)/~, the roles of A and of B laid side by side, with a ~ b whenever J_AB(a) = b. Its classes are of three kinds: {a} (A's alone), {b} (B's alone), {a, b} (a coupling).
- A face ABC carries three pairings J_AB, J_BC, J_CA. The **passage of a through C** is f_C(a) = J_CB(J_AC(a)), the role of B that a reaches by going through C; it is defined only where both steps are.

Nothing else is assumed. In particular no metric: the engine's edge is the equilateral probe, so the foot and the midpoint coincide and the construction has nothing to say about position along an edge (§7).

---

## 2 · The construction

**Definition 1 · the shadow of C on the edge.**
Sh_C(A) = { a ∈ R_A : J_AC(a) is defined }, the roles of A the person paired with a role of C.
Sh_C(B) = { b ∈ R_B : J_BC(b) is defined }, likewise.
Sh_C(AB) = Sh_C(A) ⊔ Sh_C(B).
This is the part of C that lies along the edge: what C shares with the edge's two ends, as the person stated it. It is a subset of the child's material, never a set of C's own roles.

**Definition 2 · the projection of C onto AB is its excluded form.**
Ex_C(AB) = ( Sh_C(AB) ; K_C ; ρ_C ), three things:
1. the shadow itself, marked INACTIVE in the child;
2. K_C = { (a, b) ∈ J_AB : a ∈ Sh_C(A) or b ∈ Sh_C(B) }, the child's own couplings that touch the shadow, marked REFUSED-AS-FORM (the child keeps them as the shape of what it does not take up);
3. the reading ρ_C(a) for each a ∈ Sh_C(A), comparing C's passage with the child's own coupling:
   - UND if f_C(a) is undefined (C reaches a but not B);
   - FIX if J_AB(a) = f_C(a) (the child's coupling and the passage through C agree);
   - PROPOSAL if J_AB(a) is undefined but f_C(a) is (C would couple a to a role of B that the person did not couple);
   - DISAGREEMENT if both are defined and differ (the passage through C and the child's coupling tear).

**Definition 3 · the child read from C.**
M_{AB|C} = ( M_AB , Ex_C(AB) ). Its **active part** is
Act_C(AB) = ( R_A ∖ Sh_C(A) ) ⊔ ( R_B ∖ Sh_C(B) ), with the identification generated by J_AB ∖ K_C.
The active part is what the person names. The excluded form is what the name is given under.

**Definition 4 · several opposite corners on one edge (the tetrahedron).**
For every face F that contains AB, with opposite vertex Z_F: Sh(AB) = ⋃_F Sh_{Z_F}(AB); Act(AB) is the complement, with J_AB restricted to it; the forms Ex_{Z_F}(AB) are kept one per view. A **pocket** is opened at a role a where two views' passages are both defined and differ: f_Z(a) ≠ f_{Z′}(a). A bar the person declares (Arman's added pressures: "also feel another exclusionary pressure from Copy/quotation") enters as one more view, by the same rule.

**Definition 5 · refusal and trace.**
If Act(AB) = ∅ the site is **EXHAUSTED**: every role of A and of B is shared with an opposite corner; there is nothing to name; the device refuses. If Sh_Z(AB) = ∅ for a view Z, that view is **VACUOUS**: the person has not related Z to A or to B; the device says so and applies nothing.

**Definition 6 · the next generation.**
A child's roles are its parents' roles (its members) together with its **own** roles: what the person adds at the child (its name and gloss). A child's identifications (its couplings from J) are its constitution and are never a shadow. In the subdivided face, the inner edge M_PQ–M_PR lies in two triangles: the corner triangle at P, whose opposite vertex is P, and the central triangle, whose opposite vertex is M_QR. P's shadow on M_PQ is the P-members (the pairing between P and its child is the identity on P's roles). M_QR's shadow on M_PQ is the shared inheritance (Q's roles, present in both) together with any pairing the person gives on that inner edge. Definitions 1–5 then apply unchanged.

That is the whole construction. In one sentence: **the projection of C onto AB sends the person's pairings from A and from B to C into a partition of the edge's child, active against excluded, together with the reading of the excluded part against the child's own coupling.**

## 3 · A worked example, by hand

A has roles a1, a2, a3. B has b1, b2, b3. C has c1, c2, c3 (c3 is C's own: paired to nothing).
J_AB: a1~b1, a2~b2. J_AC: a2~c1, a3~c2. J_BC: b2~c1.

- The pushout M_AB has classes {a1,b1}, {a2,b2}, {a3}, {b3}.
- Sh_C(A) = {a2, a3}; Sh_C(B) = {b2}. Inactive: a2, a3, b2.
- K_C = {(a2,b2)}: the coupling a2~b2 touches the shadow.
- Readings: f_C(a2) = J_CB(c1) = b2 = J_AB(a2): FIX. f_C(a3) = J_CB(c2): undefined: UND.
- Active part: a1, b1, b3 with a1~b1. Classes {a1,b1}, {b3}. That is what the person names.
- Excluded form: "the common ground a2~b2 is C's, coherently (FIX); a3 is C's one-sidedly (UND)". Present, inactive.

Checks on the example:
- Change c3, or add roles to C that are paired to nothing: nothing changes. C's own content never enters.
- Remove C (no face): everything active, no form: a different thing to name.
- From the form alone, read C back: "a concept sharing a2 and b2 in one role and a3 in another", C's negative profile, exactly what a backward run recovers and no more.
- The coupling a1~b1 is the child's own and has nothing of C in it: it is what survives.

A gen-1 example. Fb is the child of H and S with h1~s1, h2~s2, plus its own role "feedback" (its name). Under S as opposite (the corner triangle at S): S's shadow on Fb is {s1, s2}; inactive. h1, h2 and "feedback" stay active; the couplings h1~s1, h2~s2 are refused-as-form. What remains is the structural form of the recursion without the stream's own roles: Arman's example of 05-10 09:21, reproduced by the rule, not by the words.

## 4 · What the relation IS, answered

- **Type:** a partition of the child with a reading on the excluded part. Not a record beside the child; a structure on it.
- **Direction:** from C's pairings with the two ends into the child's material. C acts through exactly what the person said of C at A and at B, because nothing else about C's bearing on the edge exists in the record; to let C's own roles act would be to invent a bearing.
- **Sign:** negative. C inactivates; C refuses couplings; C never adds a role, never adds an identification. The passage through C (the foot) is present, but as what the child does not take up.
- **Material:** the child's own, never C's. The shadow is made of roles of A and of B.
- **Composition:** union of shadows over the faces containing the edge; the active part must survive every view; a pocket where views disagree; never a merge of views into one.
- **Recursion:** the same rule at every generation, with the parent as the corner triangle's opposite and the shared inheritance as the central triangle's shadow; a child's own content (its name) is the only thing the next generation can mix.
- **The inverted reflection, translated:** C′, the reflection of C across the edge, is C seen only through its pairings with A and B: C stripped of its own part. What C and C′ share is the shadow; where they differ is C's perpendicular part, which never reaches the edge. The midpoint "of CC′" carries the shadow, and carries it with the opposite sign to C's participation: where C takes part in A (a paired to c), the child switches a off. That is the inversion.

## 5 · The construction against the record's constraints and tests

| constraint / test (from the readers' letters; SHAPE v2) | how the construction meets it |
|---|---|
| C1 no content from C enters the child | by construction: Ex_C ⊆ R_A ⊔ R_B; C's own roles do nothing |
| C2 C recoverable from the child (the backward test) | Sh_C(A), Sh_C(B) and f_C are read off Ex_C; that is C's negative profile, and no more |
| C3a a residue per parent before they meet | R_A ∖ Sh_C(A) and R_B ∖ Sh_C(B) are the residues; they meet along J_AB ∖ K_C |
| C3b a subtraction on the named child | a declared bar is one more view: Sh grows, Act shrinks, the name is given again |
| C4 one child per edge, each corner a view, survive both | Definition 4: one M_AB, one Act, forms per view, a pocket on clash |
| C5 the shared parent becomes the exclusion at the inner edge | Definition 6: the parent is the corner triangle's opposite; its shadow is its own members |
| C6 the filler test | remove C: Act = everything, no form. With C: a smaller Act and a form. The nameable content changes |
| C7 pressure runs down the generation order only | a shadow falls only on the edges of faces that contain the vertex; faces live inside one generation's mesh; a gen-1 vertex has no face with a gen-0 edge |
| C8 a bar is declared, never inferred; a prohibition, not a filter | the opposite corner is given by the triangulation; a person's bar is declared; the shadow inactivates, it does not rank |
| the blend test ("if it only names a blend of A and B, it fails") | the active part is not A plus B: it is A and B with C's share cut out and its couplings refused; a different edge under a different C gives a different active part |

## 6 · What this changes in the engine, as I read ADR 0031

1. **The feet flip sign.** ≡_X is in M⁺'s signature as a holds-only relation: "a and b are joined through X". Under the construction the same pairs are the excluded form: "a and b are X's; their joining through X is not taken up by the child; it is present as form." The material is the same; the sign and the place are not. The stone's four link kinds (FIX / DISAGREEMENT / PROPOSAL / UND) are exactly the reading ρ; nothing measured is lost.
2. **The partition is new.** Nothing in the engine today switches a role off inside M under C. This is the one structure the construction adds, and it is the one the record demanded ("the excluded vertex must generate the name's morphology … added the exclusion as a guardrail. That is not enough").
3. **Two new honest outputs.** EXHAUSTED (a refusal: nothing to name) and VACUOUS (a trace: this corner casts no shadow). Both are states of the person's record, shown rather than filled. And the POCKET where two views disagree, which is where "gluing" legitimately lives: between views of one site, never between contents.
4. **A consequence to check against the build.** At the first descent, an inner site's members are all inherited, and its two views (the shared parent, the opposite midpoint) shadow all of them. So the inner site is exhausted unless the children carry own content: the person's name and gloss at the child. In the engine, a born concept the person has not named has nothing the next generation can mix. This is the naming doctrine seen from the other side, and it is checkable: does the gen-1 born concept carry any role that is not inherited?
5. **The respects** (a ↦ b ⟨c⟩) are the person's own statements that a coupling runs through c. They belong to the form of c's view, with the person's authority, and are read by the same ρ.

The device's line (Δ80) is unchanged: it says FORM (the partition and its reading), RECORD (the pairings as given), REFUSAL (exhausted), TRACE (vacuous). It never names.

## 7 · What the construction does not do, said plainly

- **It does not produce the name.** The vault never had a constructor for the child's name either; every privation there was a person's act. Here the device produces the form; the person names the active part under it. This is by design, not by omission.
- **It says nothing about position along an edge.** The engine's edge is metric-free; the foot and the midpoint coincide, as Arman noted on 04-24 18:01. Position (the eight-step ladder) belongs to a metric edge the engine does not have. If one is wanted later, the construction gives the balance condition in these terms: a point of the edge is the foot when C's shadow on what it keeps of A and what it keeps of B is unbiased. That is a further construction, not this one.
- **C's own roles do nothing.** If that is felt as a loss, the loss is the record's: the person has said nothing of how C's own part bears on AB, and the device must not invent it.

## 8 · Falsifiers, and the instrument

The instrument `.handoff/instruments/connection_layer_reference/the_excluded_form.py` runs Definitions 1–6 on the worked example, on random casts over a tetrahedron edge with two views, and on a gen-1 inner edge with and without own content. Seals, fixed before the run:

- S1 · adding roles to C that are paired to nothing never changes Ex_C: 100 %.
- S2 · a non-empty shadow always shrinks the active part; an empty one is always reported vacuous: 100 %.
- S3 · Sh_C(A), Sh_C(B) and f_C are recovered exactly from Ex_C: 100 %.
- S4 · with two views the shadow is exactly the union; pockets are counted.
- S5 · a gen-1 inner edge whose children carry no own content is exhausted under its two views: 100 %.
- S6 · with one own role per child, the active part is exactly the own roles: 100 %.
- S7 · the worked example yields exactly the sets written in §3.
- Regime, unsealed and reported: at pairing densities 0.2, 0.5, 0.8, the rates of vacuous views, exhausted sites and pockets. If exhaustion dominates at moderate density, the device refuses more than it lets the person name, and the construction is too strong; that is what the numbers are for.

The RESULTS file beside the instrument carries the run and its clock.

## 10 · Two turns of the frame, appended Sat Sep 26 15:27:05 IST 2026 (Arman, 14:39 and 15:24)

**The medium (14:39).** The edge is a medium the geometry gives; what fills it is the interaction of the two concept-spaces in it; the person's pairing "a IS b" is one kind of filling. The child's roles are the interactions that passed, (a, b), not the roles of A beside the roles of B. The pushout's private classes were the concatenation; they go. The medium of AB carries three things: the direct interaction I_AB; the passage through C, P_C = I_AC ; I_CB; the passage through D when a second face shares the edge. **The projection of C onto AB is P_C laid against I_AB.** The child's own part is I_AB minus every passage; the form is the rest. The role-based Definitions 1–3 are this rule one generation down, at the half-edge from A to its child, whose canonical filling is participation, a ↦ (a, b): the passage through the corner triangle's opposite picks out the participations of roles of A that C touches. One rule, two generations. Under a general interaction (talk, affect, illuminate) there are no tears, only overlap; DISAGREEMENT is a property of the copula IS, which is one-to-one.

**Detection (15:24).** The medium never lacks its child. The child is carried; being born is being detected and named. So the interaction is not what makes the child but the instrument that resolves it; the passages through C and D are oblique detections of the same medium; the name fixes a representative of what was resolved. The seed itself is a fixing: the engine looked and fixed three points a priori, and every node after is a detection relative to that look. Casts are detection records, revisable handles, never essences.

What this changes:
1. **The medium's detected content decomposes into four parts.** The child's own: direct and through no opposite corner. The centroid's: direct and also through C (and D): shared by the whole face, and the centroid is not a lattice point, so it lives at no node and is excluded from every midpoint; "three aspects of one thing (the absent centroid)". C's light alone: through C and not direct; C's proposals. The tears: the passage lands elsewhere than the direct coupling; only under IS. The name at the midpoint represents the first under the pressure of the other three.
2. **A new honest state.** UNDETECTED (I_AB empty: the person has not looked into this medium; the child is there) is distinct from EXHAUSTED (I_AB non-empty and every direct interaction mediated: looked, and nothing of the pair's own resolved) and from VACUOUS (a view used no instrument: no pairing with that corner). Definition 5 splits accordingly. An undetected midpoint must never read as "nothing there".
3. **Every output is "resolved so far".** More direct detection enlarges own-or-centroid; more oblique detection moves own into centroid or tear. The own part is a current resolution, not a lower bound. A name given under a coarser resolution is to be flagged when the resolution changes: record the inputs, re-derive, which the engine already does.
4. **The recursion is epistemic.** An inner medium's child is carried like any other; resolving it needs the person's fresh interaction between the siblings, and the person acts on names. So the interior of generation n+1 cannot be resolved before generation n is detected and named: "no descent while the generation has unworked regions", now as a consequence.
5. **A reading to check against the build.** A triad a ↦ b ⟨c⟩ is an oblique detection through c. If every pairing on a face is given as a triad, then I_AB ⊆ P_C for each edge and each midpoint's own part is empty: the faces are flat and the midpoints are exhausted. Flatness as coherence (no tears) is the naming clue; flatness as saturation (everything mediated) empties the edges. Whether C-14's triads produce the second is a question for the substrate.
